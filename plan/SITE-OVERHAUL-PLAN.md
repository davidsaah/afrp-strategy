**30 September 2026 · approved by David the same day; the ten decisions in §7 are recorded as D43–D52.** Build progress is recorded in `design/AFRP-Delivery-Status.md`.

---

## 1. What the site is for

One public place where the Federation's strategy and its implementation in
AFRP-Hub are discussed, decided and shown side by side. It serves two
audiences who today use different documents:

- **The strategy team** (Strategic Planning Committee, Board, programme
  chairs): what the Federation is trying to do, how that has changed since
  2018, and what still has to be decided.
- **The build** (David, Claude sessions, advisers): what each programme and
  each person needs from the platform, how the workflows fit together, and
  how far the build has got.

The repository becomes the **staging area**: a question is raised, discussed,
decided, written into the Decisions Register, and only then built. The site
shows every item's position in that sequence.

```
Proposal ──► Discussion ──► Decision (D-number) ──► Design note ──► Build slice ──► Verified on staging
 strategy/     Q-n page       design/Decisions-      design/          plan/MASTER-      status/
                               Register.md                            PLAN.md
```

**The organising frame is the four branches** (D38, D42): **Education ·
Leadership · Heritage · Care**, with the **family tree as the roots** beneath
all four and the **Convention as the junction** where they meet. Every section
of the site uses it.

### 1 · AFRP Strategy — `strategy/`

**The question this section answers:** what is the Federation's strategy
today, and how can each part of it be carried out in the Hub, building on the
member database (the CRM) at its centre?

**Sources.** The Strategic Planning Committee's Drive folder and the
documents added to this project on 30 Sep 2026: the 2018–2023 Strategic Plan
(May 2019) and its SWOT; the 2020–21 Recommendations ("Federation 2.0", 3 May
2021) with its working papers (app requirements, PR business plan,
press-release proposal, social media audit, communications deck,
communications intern description, Mid-Year guidelines, report templates,
welcome and renewal letters, programme flyer); the tasks list for the
subcommittees; the Convention MOU (Oct 2022); the committee's minutes
2020–2023; the committee SWOT responses (Mar 2025); the committee's minutes of
10 Sep 2026; and the committee's working draft, *AFRP Strategic Plan 2026
Refresh* (Drive). All are **cited, not reproduced**: several name living
people, and FED2 is a governing document the by-laws incorporate (17.1.4).

**What "the current strategy" is.** No successor to the 2018–2023 plan has
been adopted. The Federation's strategy today is four layers, and the page
says so plainly:

1. **The mission** (programme flyer, 2019 plan): strengthen the ties between
   Ramallah people in Palestine and the United States; give young people born
   here their culture and heritage; educate the American public.
2. **The adopted commitments still in force**: the 2018–2023 plan's goals and
   the 2021 Recommendations, which the 2024 by-laws partly incorporate
   (Selection Committee, Art. XVII).
3. **The direction set on 10 Sep 2026**: an outcome-based plan, an endowment
   campaign, an Executive Director, a central archive, and refreshed priorities
   (Camp Ramallah, Government Affairs, Arabic classes, Ramallah Works, the
   Family Tree).
4. **The refresh in draft**: the four branches (D42) and the 2026–27
   priorities, awaiting the committee and the Board.

**Building on the CRM** (`strategy/on-the-crm.html`) shows the Hub as four

## 4. How the site is built

**Recommended: Markdown sources plus a small generator.**

- Narrative pages are written as Markdown in `site/` (one file per page) so
  the strategy team can propose edits as ordinary text changes.
- `site/build.py` renders them into `docs/` with one template and one
  stylesheet, and builds the data pages (crosswalk, questions, status,
  programme grid) from structured files:
  - `site/data/programmes.yaml` — the 19 programmes (branch, entity, shape,
    status, needs, questions).
  - `site/data/questions.yaml` — open questions with IDs and owners.
  - The Decisions Register and the Crosswalk, parsed from `design/`.
  - The build board's embedded JSON, read from `AFRP-Analyst-Dashboard.html`.
- A GitHub Action runs `build.py` on every push to `main`, then a link check
  and a privacy check, and fails the build if either finds a problem.
- No external scripts, fonts or CDNs; every page works offline and on a phone.

**Checks every build runs:**

1. Links: no broken internal links; every old URL resolves.
2. Privacy: no personal names outside an allow-list, no email addresses or
   phone numbers, no FED2 text.
3. Counts: decisions, journeys and programme totals come from their sources,
   never typed by hand.
4. Framing: no "rails" or "pathways" in the progression sense on new pages.

## 5. Content rules

- **Precedence (D41):** by-law texts, then the Decisions Register and design
  documents, then the prototype, then the Hub's code. The site is narrative:
  it states no rule the record does not hold, and cites the record for every
  rule it mentions.
- **Where the record is silent, the page says so** and links the open
  question. It never fills the gap with a default.
- **Public repository:** nothing about a living person beyond what is already
  public; no member data; FED2 and the committee's Drive documents are cited,
  not reproduced; camp facts from internal folders are not published; demo
  people and money are labelled fictional.
- **The branches everywhere.** Leadership Ramallah sits on Education, as D38
  placed it (§7, decision 3).
