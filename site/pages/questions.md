## How to use this page

Every open question is in the tables below. Each row gives the question, what
the record already knows about it, who owns the answer, the goal and branch it
affects, and where it came from. Use the two menus above the tables to show only
the questions for one goal or one branch, and the owner box to show only the
questions whose owner contains a word (for example "David" or "ARFECF Board").
The two lists further down this section group the owners most often asked for.

Since 4 October 2026 (D99) every question carries a **status**: *open*;
*answered for now*, where a decision holds for now and the lasting answer is
still open; *answered in part*; *pending an act*, where the platform's answer is
set and a body's act is awaited; *parked*, where no slice of the master plan and
no gate in a design note cites it, so it holds no build; or *answered*. The open
ones are grouped into **twelve clusters (A to L)**, each put to its owning body
as one ask; the rest follow, then the parked list, then the answered list. What
the decisions already settle, topic by topic, is on
[Rules in force](rules-in-force.html).

The platform never fills a gap itself. Where the by-laws and the decisions are
silent, the part of the platform that depends on the answer waits for it, and
the question here names who must give it.

## How to take part

Every question has a **Q-number**, and the same number is used everywhere it is
discussed (D43).

- **The build team** discusses on GitHub: each row's *Discuss* link searches the
  repository's Discussions for that number. If nothing is there yet, start a
  discussion with the Q-number in its title.
- **The Strategic Planning Committee** comments in its own working draft, citing
  the Q-number. That draft is behind this page in places; how the two are
  brought level is [Q-235](#Q-235).
- **To raise a new question**, start a GitHub discussion, or add a comment in the
  committee's draft, saying what is undecided and who you think should decide
  it. It is given the next free Q-number when it is added to the record. A
  question that only asks an existing one again is added to that row instead.

## How the questions are numbered

A Q-number is given once and never reused. Numbers are given in batches, as
each part of the record was written:

- **Q-1 to Q-49** come from the design record, the club plan and the directory
  note.
- **Q-50 to Q-157** were raised by the sixteen design notes of 1 October 2026.
  Where several notes asked the same thing, the question was merged under one
  number (the non-member contact record, the retention periods, the Mid-Year's
  agreement and standing terms).
- **Q-158 to Q-237** were raised by the research round of 2 October 2026, which
  read the Federation's own records and public pages against this record. Where
  that round only asked an existing question again, the finding was added to
  the existing row, marked "research, 2 Oct 2026". Where it found practice
  contradicting a decision or a by-law, the conflict is listed in the
  Decisions Register under that date.
- **Q-238 to Q-261** were raised by the seven design notes written from that
  round on 2 October 2026 (P21 to P27). One question that asked Q-204 again was
  added to that row instead.
- **Q-262 to Q-270** were raised by the family tree module (P28), which follows
  David's decisions D68 to D74 of the same day.
- **Q-271 and Q-272** were raised the same evening, when the design documents
  were brought into step with that day's record: recruitment of scholarship
  alumni through the tree (D36) and the two readings of D71's radius.
- **Q-273** was raised by the review of that work: the scholarship
  conflict-of-interest rule of 8 September 2026 rests on David's correction
  (P26 §1.4) and has no D-number. *Answered by D84 (4 October 2026),
  conditional on the Scholarship Fund Committee's adoption.*
- **Q-274 to Q-278** were numbered later the same day, from questions the
  record already held without a number: extracting the 2012 by-law text into
  rules, which club moves from Breeze first, the email and payment
  credentials, which entity holds the Preservation Project (D55 does not name
  it), and the order of the next build slices. A sixth, the suspension
  question held by journey JM-042, was already [Q-10](#Q-10) and was added to
  that row.

When a question is answered, the answer is written into the
[Decisions Register](https://github.com/davidsaah/afrp-strategy/blob/main/design/AFRP-Decisions-Register.md)
as a D-number, and the question moves to the **Answered** list at the end of
this page, keeping its number. On 2 October 2026, for example, D69 answered
[Q-258](#Q-258) and D70 answered [Q-259](#Q-259). On 3 October 2026 D76, D77
and D79 answered [Q-272](#Q-272), [Q-270](#Q-270) and [Q-262](#Q-262); D75 and
D78 answered [Q-278](#Q-278) and [Q-265](#Q-265) in part, and both stay open
for what remains. Later that day D80, D81 and D82 answered [Q-279](#Q-279),
[Q-264](#Q-264) and [Q-163](#Q-163), and with D82 the successor question
[Q-20](#Q-20) fell away; D83 answered [Q-109](#Q-109) in part (AFRP holds the
Relief Fund), and it stays open on who approves a transfer between the
Board's votes and whether D63's closing records apply. On 4 October 2026, under
D99, [Q-136](#Q-136) was closed by D65 and [Q-278](#Q-278) by D75 with D99;
seven questions were relabelled as pending an act and eight as answered in part,
and those no slice or gate cites were parked. Questions are kept in
[`site/data/questions.yaml`](https://github.com/davidsaah/afrp-strategy/blob/main/site/data/questions.yaml).
