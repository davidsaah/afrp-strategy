## How to read it

The prototype is **strong evidence of a story and weak evidence of a rule**
(D41). It shows most of the journeys a member, an officer or a staff member
walks, end to end. It is thin on the logic behind the screens. For a rule, the
by-laws and the Decisions Register come first, and the prototype comes after
them.

People, households and money in it are fictional.

## Where it disagrees with the record

The prototype was built before some decisions were made, and it has been left
as it was rather than quietly edited to agree (D48). It will be re-framed later.

- **It still shows the four "rails"** (youth pipeline, advocacy, heritage,
  Palestine-direct) on the member's *My pathway* screen. The record now uses the
  four branches: Education, Leadership, Heritage and Care (D38).
- **Its Programme lens groups programmes the old way** (Medical, Cultural,
  Leadership, Educational, Camp, Convention), not by branch. The tours below
  regroup them by branch.
- **The family tree appears as a programme in the Cultural group.** It now sits
  on the Heritage branch, with a node for every member (D30, D53).
- **It draws the family tree in the platform's own style.** Since 2 October
  2026 the tree is drawn in the printed book's plate style on every surface
  (D68, replacing D27; P28 §3).
- **It shows an Emerging Leaders Summit for ages 16 to 24.** No programme by
  that name is found in the Federation's records, and the entry is retired
  (D90, answering [Q-178](../history/questions.html#Q-178)).
- **It shows Senior Living as a Federation campaign with a pledge ledger.**
  The sources show the senior home is the Ramallah Foundation's project, and
  no pledge was ever held in a Federation system; the capital-campaign design
  is marked as never operated (P23 §9.7;
  [Q-203](../history/questions.html#Q-203)).
