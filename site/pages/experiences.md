lede: The people who use the platform, what each of them needs, and the journeys that test whether they get it.
---

The platform has five lenses, one for each kind of work. The **front door** is
for people who are not signed in. The **member** lens shows what is mine and
what I can do next. The **club** lens is for a club's officers. The
**programme** lens works across all clubs within one programme. The
**federation** lens is for staff and officers running the whole.

Each person below is tested by journeys: short stories the build walks against
itself, rated *meets*, *guarded*, *open*, *not built* or *fails*. The sixteen
canonical journeys, J01 to J16, are the stories every version of the design has
had to pass.

The design notes add journeys of their own, numbered by note (P26-J01, for
example). Where a note's journey is not yet built, the person's page lists it
under *Not built yet*. A note's journey tests the design; until it is built and
walked it has no verdict.

**The scholar**, added on 2 October 2026, is a scholarship recipient whose
award is paid in instalments. That page follows the award year designed in P26:
the semester filing, the release of each instalment, the hold and the letters.
Where the 2026 award letters say something different from the record, for
example that a missing filing "may result in suspension", the page marks it
"in practice" and names the question
([Q-159](../history/questions.html#Q-159)).

Demo people in the prototype are fictional, and none is named here.
