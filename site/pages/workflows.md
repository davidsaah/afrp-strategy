## How the pieces fit together

The workflows are not separate systems. They pass work to each other through
the same records:

- **Joining creates a node on the family tree**, and the member appears in the
  directory under the choices they made (D30). At join and renewal the member
  is invited to find themselves on the tree. Skipping that step is a complete
  answer, and no membership decision reads the tree (D73).
- **Standing gates everything with a franchise or a prize**: ballots, awards and
  committee seats read the same standing, computed from payments.
- **A life event flows outward.** A birth, a marriage or a death is declared, the
  Family Tree Committee reviews it, and it reaches the household, the magazine
  and any change in dues. One committee member's approval is enough for a
  household's own life event; a change to the tree's structure needs two
  different committee members (D72). The platform offers those changes and never makes them on its
  own.
- **A programme's top rung invites the next programme**, but only on the two
  ladder branches, Education and Leadership (D39).
- **The remittance ledger gates the Convention.** A delegate's credentials are
  checked against what their club has remitted, not against what anyone says.
- **Every payment goes through one door**, whichever workflow takes it.

## Seven workflows added from practice, 2 October 2026

The research round of 2 October 2026 found seven processes the Federation
already runs that the record had not written down as workflows. Each is now on
this page as the sources show it running, set against the design that would
carry it:

- **Convention close and host payment** — designed as dated acts in P22 §9.
- **Relief Fund: money in and three paths out** — designed in P23 Part A.
- **Scholarship award letters, renewal and instalment release** — designed in
  P26.
- **Magazine announcements, subscriptions and the issue as run** — designed in
  P24.
- **Family-tree submission and correction, as run** — designed in P27, as
  amended for D68 to D74, and P28.
- **Women to Women: proposal to disbursement** — designed as a committee
  sub-fund on the Care grants design (P11 §2, §6, with P26 §6.1).
- **Government Affairs: meetings, town halls and local action** — designed in
  the advocacy note (P14) and P25 §5.4, which say what the platform does and
  does not do.

A step marked "in practice" describes what the sources show today. It is
evidence of how the work is done, never a rule (D41). Where practice departs
from a decision or a by-law, the step names the question that will settle it.
