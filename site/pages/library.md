These documents hold detail the section pages summarise. They are kept at their
original addresses so that links shared earlier still work. Where one uses older
framing (the "rails" or "pathways" in place of the four branches), the table
says so. Where a later design note or decision has replaced part of one, the
last column names it: the design notes of 2 October 2026 (P21 to P28) and the
family-tree decisions D68 to D74 replace parts of several.

The Federation's own strategy documents, and the public pages and tax filings
the research of 2 October 2026 cited, are listed on
[the strategy documents page](../strategy/documents.html).
