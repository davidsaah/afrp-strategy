## Where the strategy stands

The Federation has not adopted a plan to follow the Strategic Plan 2018–2023.
Its strategy today has four layers, and it is worth being exact about which is
which.

1. **The mission.** Strengthen the ties between Ramallah people in Palestine and
   in the United States; give the young people born here their culture and
   heritage; and educate the American public about Palestinian aspirations.
2. **The commitments still in force.** The goals of the 2018–2023 plan, and the
   2020–21 Recommendations ("Federation 2.0"). The 2024 by-laws adopted part of
   the Recommendations outright: the Selection Committee in Article XVII points
   to them for its details.
3. **The direction set on 10 September 2026.** When the Strategic Planning
   Committee restarted, it asked for a plan built on outcomes and data, an
   endowment campaign, an Executive Director and a central archive. It named
   Camp Ramallah, Government Affairs, Arabic classes, Ramallah Works and the
   Family Tree as current priorities.
4. **The refresh, in draft.** The committee's working draft organises the
   Federation's work on four branches (Education, Leadership, Heritage and Care)
   and proposes priorities for 2026–27. It is not yet adopted. Until the
   committee adopts it, the committee's own draft is the text of record (D44);
   this site shows it and links to it.

## Building on the CRM

Almost every goal depends on one thing being true first: every member is in
one database, with their household, their clubs, their standing and their
payments. That database is the CRM, and it is the first layer of the Hub.
[How the Hub builds on the CRM](on-the-crm.html) explains the layers and the
order they are built in.
