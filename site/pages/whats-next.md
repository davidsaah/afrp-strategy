## Design notes the strategy needs

Eight elements of the Federation's plans have no design yet. Each needs a
design note before the Hub can build it. The first, the committee workspace, is
the one the Hub's refused board and committee screens are waiting for.

## Documents nobody has on file

The by-laws cite five documents the platform needs and nobody has produced:

1. The Endowment Fund Policy Statement. The endowment campaign cannot start
   without it.
2. The 2015 Scholarship Fund Policy Statement, which settles the 4% floor against
   the 5% cap for both funds.
3. ARFECF's exempt-status determination letter, needed for donor receipts.
4. The Board Rules & Regulations, the authority for the dues tiers.
5. A clean copy of the ARFECF 2013 by-laws, and the ARFHSN trustee roster.

## Votes the Convention floor must take

Four amendments are drafted as a package, each a two-thirds delegate vote:

1. By-Law 9.2, to conform the text to digital voting (D4).
2. By-Law 9.1.3, so the vote weights are fixed when voting opens.
3. By-Law 9.1.3, an at-large roll for members with no chapter club.
4. The Scholarship Committee: constitute it and write down its cross-entity
   election.
