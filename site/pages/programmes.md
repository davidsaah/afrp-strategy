## Two ways of moving

People move in two directions, and they are kept apart (D39).

- **Along a branch, between programmes.** How that works depends on the
  branch's shape. Education is a ladder by age, Leadership a ladder by capacity,
  Heritage a cluster with no "next", and Care a set of destinations.
- **Up the rungs inside one programme.** Every programme uses the same five:
  **hear, show up, take part, give or serve, lead** (D25). Members never see a
  score, and moving down a rung is never a flag.

Pages marked **Plan only** have a register row and a written plan and little
more. They say so, and where the record is silent they say that too.
