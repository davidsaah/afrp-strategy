## Two ways of moving

People move in two directions, and they are kept apart (D39).

- **Along a branch, between programmes.** How that works depends on the
  branch's shape. Education is a ladder by age, Leadership a ladder by capacity,
  Heritage a cluster with no "next", and Care a set of destinations.
- **Up the rungs inside one programme.** Every programme uses the same five:
  **hear, show up, take part, give or serve, lead** (D25). Members never see a
  score, and moving down a rung is never a flag.

Pages marked **Plan only** have a register row and a written plan and little
more. They say so, and where the record is silent they say that too.

## What the research of 2 October 2026 changed

The second research round read each programme's own files and public pages
against this register. Four entries read differently as a result. In each case
the record keeps what was decided, shows what the sources found beside it, and
leaves the choice to the people named.

- **The Leadership branch's entry rung.** The record names *Emerging Leaders*
  as the way into the Leadership branch. No programme by that name appears in
  any of the Federation's documents, mailings, budgets or web pages. The one
  mention most likely refers to the Youth Summit, reported since 2023 as the
  Day of Action. Whether the entry rung is retired or pointed at another
  programme is for David and the Strategic Planning Committee
  ([Q-178](../history/questions.html#Q-178)). The path from a high-school
  senior to a Board seat, through what the record already holds, is design
  note P25 (`AFRP-Leadership-Pipeline.md` §3).
- **Senior Living.** The sources show the senior home is the Ramallah
  Foundation's own project: the Foundation owns it, built it and will run it.
  The Federation's part has been communications, gifts passed through and a
  budget grant voted at the Convention. That part is designed in P23 Part B;
  the capital-campaign design is marked as never operated by the Federation
  (P23 §9.7). Whether Senior Living is a Federation programme at all is for
  David and the Boards ([Q-203](../history/questions.html#Q-203)).
- **The Relief Fund.** It has run since 2020 from a separate bank account,
  with the Board voting emergency support and the Federation's representative
  in Ramallah distributing it. AFRP holds the fund (D83, 3 October 2026,
  David's decision for the design record); the books carry it inside ARFECF,
  which is a reconciliation item, and who approves a transfer between the
  Board's votes is still open ([Q-109](../history/questions.html#Q-109)). P23 Part A designs the fund as run, with three paths for money out and
  who authorises each.
- **The bookstore.** Two online stores already sell the Federation's books,
  one on afrp.org and one on the member portal, with no member price. The cook
  book's sales are the educational fund's income; for the other titles no
  entity is stated. Who owns the bookstore is for the Boards and David
  ([Q-197](../history/questions.html#Q-197); P24 §8).

The Outstanding High School Senior Award was not retired and continues
(D82, 3 October 2026, answering [Q-163](../history/questions.html#Q-163)); the
record's earlier "retired 31 August 2026" is corrected. Which committee runs
it is still open ([Q-164](../history/questions.html#Q-164)).
