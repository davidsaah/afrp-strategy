## At a glance

<!--FACTS-->

AFRP-Hub is **not yet in service**. It has no public address, it takes no live
payments and it holds no real member data.

**Reading the figures.** The decisions and open questions are counted from the
record each time the site is built. The slice count includes every row the
design notes have added to the master plan: eighty-seven from P1 to P20
(seventy-seven from P1 to P17, ten from P18 to P20),
forty-seven from P21 to P27, and the family tree module's T2 rows from P28.
On 4 October 2026 (D99) the queue was reshaped: finished rows moved to an
appendix, rows that build one thing were merged, and six rows were added for
the shared pieces of design note P29, so the count changed again without any
change in the work done.
Most of those rows have not started, so the share done fell when they were
added, without any loss of work. The journey figures are the build board's, as
of the date it was last generated. They do not yet include the journeys the
design notes propose, which are listed as *not built* on each person's page
until they are added to the board's catalogue.

<div class="note">
<p><b>Which constitution the record is built on.</b> The design and the Hub follow the Federation's <b>2024</b> Constitution and By-Laws. A restated constitution prepared in 2026 (a smaller Board, advisory Convention votes, standing on Federation dues alone) is deferred and under Board review as of October 2026. If it is adopted it outranks every decision built on the 2024 text, so work that depends on the governance articles waits on the Board's outcome (<a href="../history/questions.html#Q-33">Q-33</a>).</p>
</div>

## Decisions

Every decision in the Register, with its answer in one line. The Register holds
the full wording, the date, and what each decision settles. D68 to D74
(2 October 2026) settle the family tree; D75 to D83 (3 October
2026) set the build order, settle six of the tree's open points, record that
the Senior Award continues and name AFRP as the Relief Fund's holding entity;
D84 to D98 (4 October 2026) answer further points on scholarships, the tree,
the Magazine, Senior Living, oral-history releases and documents at the door,
and clear the way for the build. The latest, D99 (4 October 2026), adopts the
review of everything designed: the pieces every design note shares are built
once, first (design note P29), the build queue is reshaped and the open
questions are sorted by status. Where one changes an earlier
decision, the earlier text is kept in the Register with a pointer to the later
one: D68 supersedes D27, D69 supersedes D11's staging, D71 amends D17, D72
amends D14's mitigations, D76 reads D71's radius and D77 limits D68 as applied
to D19.

The Register also lists, under 2 October 2026, the places where the research
round found practice departing from a decision or a by-law. No decision's text
was changed for them; each has a question number and an owner.
