## 6. Rules

1. The path is read from records the person already has; it creates no record of its own and infers nothing from attendance, gifts or seats (D39; P7 §2; *design choice*).
2. No rung is a prerequisite for another; nothing auto-enrols; a milestone invitation fires only on an age ladder (D39). The Leadership branch has no milestone invitations.
3. No person under eighteen has an interest row, a seat, a directory row or their own channel (4.1.1; R6; R7; P7 §2). A minor applies to anything through the household adult who holds the consent power (R6).
4. A seat exists only with the authority its text names and that authority's dated act; where the text is silent the seat is a register row naming who seated it, labelled "no appointing text" (P1 §2.2; DIR2).
5. The Young Leader Committee has the five seats of the 8.10 paragraph; a sixth is refused naming the paragraph and Q-179; the alumni check is shown and never refuses (8.10 paragraph; P12 §2.9).
6. Every Board seat shows the 6.2.3 standing check; every committee seat the 8.1.2 check; the platform tells and ends nothing (P1 rule 3; P3 rule 6).
7. Seat history is kept when a seat ends, never deleted; a historical seat entered by the office is an attested row naming its drive source, shown apart from platform rows (P1; D66; *design choice*).
8. The Deputy President's service test reads seat history and shows attested and platform rows separately; "proven record of service" without a club is a human finding (9.3.4(4); P5 §3.4).
9. Everything advocacy costs is AFRP's General Fund; an affiliate entity on any advocacy row is refused with no override (D65).
10. *Candidate* is refused as a position's subject until the Board decides with the Legal Advisor's and the CPA's advice recorded (P14 §2.1; Q-132).
11. A cycle runs in the mode its deciding body set for that cycle; a cycle with no mode does not open (*design choice*, §5.3).
12. No money line raises for a programme whose holding entity the approved budget does not name (D55; P12-J01): the Senior Award, the internships, the Young Leader Committee's drive.
13. A giving appeal publishes only under a catalogue purpose naming its receiving entity (D8), with the surplus and shortfall disclosure (D7).
14. Branch outcomes are counts under the floor; no list of who did or did not progress exists (S7; D67).
15. Every refusal names its rule and, where one holds it open, its question.
