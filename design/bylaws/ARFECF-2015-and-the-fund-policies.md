## 1. The by-laws as revised in 2015

What changed against the 2013 text the record analysed (`AFRP-ARFECF-ARFHSN-Rulesets.md` Part 1):

- **Board of fifteen voting members** composed by role as in 2013, with **five** at-large members chosen by the President (the 2013 draft read "three five (5)").
- **Three regions of five seats each** (Art. V §4.1): West, Central and East, each defined by a list of clubs plus the individual members in that region's time zone. The club lists are those of 2015 and are out of date.
- **No more than four ARFECF directors may also sit on the AFRP Board.**
- Everything else the ruleset records from 2013 stands: the Scholarship Fund Committee Secretary as Board Secretary; the President's unbudgeted limit and the Board and Annual General Meeting thresholds; spending from each fund limited to 5%; each fund's committee the only body that authorises its spending; two-thirds of the Board for rules and policy amendments; two-thirds membership vote at the Annual General Meeting with 60 days' notice for by-law amendments; the Executive Director hired by the Board after a five-member selection committee; AFRP staff administering ARFECF for an annual fee.

Consequence for the record: EC-OV-1 is superseded. The ARFECF Board-composition check uses the regional seats and the four-member cap. The club lists per region are a parameter for the ARFECF Board to maintain.

## 2. The two policy statements

Both statements set the same spending rule, in the same words:

- Each fiscal year (1 June to 31 May) the fund makes a **mandatory** distribution of **4% of its assets** for its purposes (programmes for the Endowment; scholarships and educational causes for the Scholarship Fund) **plus 1% of its assets to AFRP** as compensation for administering the fund day to day.
- "Assets" are the value of the property and funds allocated to the fund less its liabilities. In practice every budget and audit since 2012 applies the percentages to the valuation at 31 May.
- The fund's committee approves and distributes the annual expenditure.
- An annual report of donations and distributions by 31 March; thank-you letters within 48 hours, with the tax-deductibility statement.

So: the by-laws **limit** spending to 5%; the policies **require** 4% + 1% = 5%. There is no floor-versus-cap conflict. The distribution is fixed at the cap, split four to programmes and one to AFRP. What the texts do not say is who may waive or carry over an unspent balance; that stays open.

Only the Endowment statement adds:

- in an emergency confirmed by the Board, a transfer to AFRP of up to a fixed sum every three years;
- a share of third-party donations in a year may fund the committee's donor-visit travel, on a budget the ARFECF Board approves;
- a committee of at least seven: a chair, the executive director, past AFRP presidents and other donors, regionally spread; paid employees do not vote.

Only the Scholarship statement adds:

- a committee of at least nine: a chairman chosen by the ARFECF President, the executive director, at-large members chosen by the ARFECF Board, and **since 2015 three members elected at the Annual Convention**; two three-year terms then a year off; employees do not vote;
- published application guidelines (not found anywhere), and follow-up to confirm students keep the required academic standing.

The Scholarship statement names **no seat for the Ramallah Foundation**. The committee's current overview lists three Foundation representatives (Q-29); that is a departure from this policy and from D3.
