Every design note the crosswalk proposed is written as of 1 October 2026: P1 to P9 and P11 to P17, and P18 to P20, all on that day, with P10 (the club share and settlement) inside P18. Documents nobody has on file

Two of the documents the by-laws cite were found in October 2026: the Endowment Fund Policy Statement (2012) and the Scholarship Fund Policy Statement (revised 2015), together with the ARFECF by-laws as revised in 2015 (what they say). Three are still missing, and two more are now needed:

  1. ARFECF's exempt-status determination letter, needed for donor receipts. Its audits state the status; the letter is not on file.
  2. The Board Rules & Regulations, the authority for the dues tiers.
  3. The ARFHSN trustee roster, with seats and term dates.
  4. The minutes of the Houston General Assembly (July 2026), which would show what the General Assembly did with the restated constitution (Q-33). Every club-governance feature waits on this answer. In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): a Board thread of August 2026 records that the restatement was taken off the Convention's agenda and not voted. The minutes themselves are still not on file.
  5. The revised host agreement under which the 2026 Convention was settled. The Board's minutes of September 2026 mention it, and no folder holds it (Q-93).

Votes the Convention floor must take

  1. By-Law 9.2, to conform the text to digital voting (D4).
  2. By-Law 9.1.3, so the vote weights are fixed when voting opens.
