Design notes the strategy needs

Eight elements of the Federation's plans have no design yet (P1–P8). Each needs a design note before the Hub can build it. The first, the committee workspace, is the one the Hub's refused board and committee screens are waiting for.

Nine more notes (P9–P17) follow from the working files read in September and October 2026 and the decisions taken on them (D55–D67). They are queued in the order the Federation is deciding things now: the fund rules first, then the club share and settlement, Care grants and partner agreements, the selection programmes and the Arabic term, host agreements, advocacy, incident response and data retention, the Executive Director as the first staff role, and AFRPWorks.

Three notes are written. P18, the club experience plan, carries the club share and settlement (P10) inside it and gives the Hub six club slices (C1–C6) with their gates; a deep read of Breeze on 1 October 2026 then adjusted the migration path and the gates on club giving and texting. P19, the directory in three formats, gives the Hub three more (DIR1–DIR3). P20, the club journeys, is the corpus of fifty-two tests the clubs component is built from, each starting from what a club officer does in Breeze today.

Documents nobody has on file

Two of the documents the by-laws cite were found in October 2026: the Endowment Fund Policy Statement (2012) and the Scholarship Fund Policy Statement (revised 2015), together with the ARFECF by-laws as revised in 2015 (what they say). Three are still missing, and one more is now needed:

  1. ARFECF's exempt-status determination letter, needed for donor receipts. Its audits state the status; the letter is not on file.
  2. The Board Rules & Regulations, the authority for the dues tiers.
  3. The ARFHSN trustee roster, with seats and term dates.
  4. The minutes of the Houston General Assembly (July 2026), which would show what the General Assembly did with the restated constitution (Q-33). Every club-governance feature waits on this answer.

Votes the Convention floor must take

Four amendments are drafted as a package, each a two-thirds delegate vote:

  1. By-Law 9.2, to conform the text to digital voting (D4).
  2. By-Law 9.1.3, so the vote weights are fixed when voting opens.
  3. By-Law 9.1.3, an at-large roll for members with no chapter club.
  4. The Scholarship Committee: constitute it and write down its cross-entity election.
