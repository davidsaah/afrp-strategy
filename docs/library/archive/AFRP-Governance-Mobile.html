My AFRP — Governance & Membership

Fourteen annotated phone screens covering the two modules that carry the most rules: dual membership — national and club as separate memberships with separate dues and independent lapse — and governance: eligibility frozen at a record date, secret and recorded ballots, the one-time receipt, and results that show their arithmetic. Every colour, size and component comes from AFRP Design System v2, inlined here verbatim; this document adds only device frames and annotations on top of it. The screens are live 375 px renderings, not drawings.

How to read these screens

Each frame is a live 375 px-wide rendering built only from the .c-* classes and --* tokens of Design System v2. Touch targets are raised from the 44 px WCAG 2.2 AA floor to the 48 px --touch token throughout and every input sits at the 16 px floor, because AFRP's membership skews older. Frames extend past 812 px so a whole scrolling screen is visible at once rather than cropped at the fold.

The five rules these screens build to

Rule 1National and club are separate membershipsSeparate dues, separate cycles, independent lapse. There is no isInGoodStanding(person); only standingAt(scope, date). Frames 01–04, 12, 14.
Rule 2Eligibility is frozen at a record date and never recomputedTallies read the snapshot, not live membership — so a result stays reproducible after the fact. Frames 05, 06, 11, 12, 13.
Rule 3An ineligible member is told why, precisely, with a fixNever a hidden ballot. The reason names the payment date, the record date and the gap between them. Frames 05, 11.
Rule 4A lost receipt cannot be recovered or reissuedReissuing would require storing the voter→token link, which is the thing that makes the ballot secret. Said before the member dismisses the screen. Frames 06, 08, 09.
Rule 5Abstentions are counted and excluded from the denominator; ties are never broken silentlyBoth documented on the result rather than buried in the tally. Frames 06, 07, 10.

What these screens commit AFRP to

Four things on this page are promises, not decoration. Each has a cost, and the cost is the reason it is worth writing down before build.

CommitmentEvery eligibility answer names a scope and a dateWhich means no screen, report or email may ever say “member in good standing” unqualified — including the ones officers write themselves.
CommitmentAn ineligible member sees the ballot and the reasonWhich means the eligibility engine must return a reason string good enough to show a member, for every rejection path, not just the common one.
CommitmentSupport can never recover a receiptWhich means staff need a scripted answer, and the screen has to carry the warning hard enough that the call never happens. Frame 08 is that screen.
CommitmentEvery result publishes its arithmetic and its certifierWhich means quorum, abstentions, the denominator and the threshold are member-facing output, not an admin export. Frame 10 is that screen.

Still open

The record date offset, the quorum basis, the threshold per motion type, whether proxy voting is permitted and who breaks a tie are all bylaw configuration, not code. The values shown here — 30 days before the meeting, quorum at 25% of the frozen electorate, simple majority for officers, two-thirds for bylaws — are illustrative and tuned to the seed data. Send the bylaws and they become data.
