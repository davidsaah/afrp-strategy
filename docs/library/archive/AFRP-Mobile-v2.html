My AFRP — Mobile Screens v2

Eighteen annotated phone screens for the next phase of the My AFRP member portal: life events, the 500-year family tree, the two member directories, the local club portal that replaces Breeze ChMS at the 18 clubs, and the social and program integrations around them. Every colour, size, component and interaction on this page comes from AFRP Design System v2, inlined here verbatim — this document adds only device frames and annotations on top of it. The screens are real 375 px renderings, not drawings: resize this page and they reflow exactly as the portal will.

How to read these screens

Each frame is a live 375 px-wide rendering using only the .c-* classes and --* tokens of Design System v2. The markup inside every phone is the markup a developer should ship. Nothing on these screens uses a colour, radius, or type size that is not in the token set.

The four decisions these screens build to

Decision 1A wedding upgrades the couple to Family tier, free for year oneThen it renews at the standard Family rate, on the anniversary of the wedding date. Frames 01, 02.
Decision 2Life events write to the tree as provisional recordsVisible at once, marked unverified, out of the canonical tree until a genealogy moderator confirms. Frames 02, 05, 06, 08, 09.
Decision 3The club portal replaces Breeze ChMSSo club tooling is first-class and works on an officer's phone, and migration state stays visible. Frames 13, 14, 15.
Decision 4Directories are members-only, per-field opt-inNothing is published unless the member switches it on, and hidden fields render as an explicit state. Frames 10, 11, 12.

What these eighteen screens commit to

AccessibilityNothing below the floorEvery input is 16 px so iOS never zooms on focus. Every tappable element is at least 44 px, most are 48 px. Every input has a real <label for>. Colour is never the only carrier of a state — provisional records are amber and dashed and labelled.
LayoutNo horizontal overflow at any widthEvery field is width:100% with min-width:0; every grid is auto-fill/auto-fit, never a fixed column count. Long emails and citations wrap inside their cell. Verified at 1600, 390 and 320 px.
PrivacyRestriction is a visible stateMinors, living people in the tree, and unpublished directory fields all render as an explicit, labelled state — never as a blank line, a broken link, or a silent omission.
GenealogyEvidence, not assertionSources are first-class records with real citations. Corrections require one. Contested facts show the pending edit beside the current value rather than quietly overwriting it.
