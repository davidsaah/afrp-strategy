What this note rests on. The research round of 2 October 2026 (the Leadership report in full, with the Education report's section on the Senior Award), read against the 2024 by-laws, the Decisions Register (D25, D38, D39, D41, D45, D53, D55, D57, D61, D64, D65, D67) and its new section "Conflicts between practice and the record found in the research round of 2 October 2026", and the notes that already design every object the path runs through: P1 (AFRP-Committee-Workspace.md, bodies and seats), P5 (AFRP-Selection-Committee-Workflow.md, the Selection Committee's year), P7 (AFRP-Volunteer-Interests.md), P12 (AFRP-Selection-Programmes.md, the SEL engine), P14 (AFRP-Advocacy.md), P17 (AFRP-AFRPWorks.md) and P19 (AFRP-Directory-Formats.md). Precedence (D41). The by-law texts first (the 8.10 paragraph, 6.2.2, 6.2.3, 6.5, 6.6.1, 7.3.2, 8.1, 8.13, 8.14, 8.15.1, the Governmental Affairs paragraph, 9.3), then the Decisions Register and the named design documents, then the prototype (a story, never a rule), then the Hub's code (what is, never what should be). Public-repo rule. Roles only; no living person named; no figures; no club named beyond what the record already names.

1. What the record already holds

1.1 In the by-laws

  • The Young Leader Committee is a standing committee (Const. Art. VIII §1; 8.2.1). The unnumbered paragraph printed under 8.10.1 (whose heading reads "Constitutional Committee", P1's Q-50) says: "The President shall appoint, with approval of the Board of Directors, the Young Leader Chairman and four (4) additional members to the Young Leader Committee. The Young Leader Chairman and committee members shall be alumnus of the A.F.R.P. Project Hope or Leadership Ramallah Programs. The Young Leader Committee shall help develop and implement Service and Leadership Programs for members and Local Chapters of the A.F.R.P."
  • The Board includes "One (1) member of the Young Leader Committee" and "One (1) member of the Ramallah Business and Professional Network" as voting members (6.2.2). The RBPN seat has an appointing text (6.6.1: the RBPN Committee appoints one of its board members for one year, at the General Assembly, never a past President); the Young Leader seat has none (P19 DIR2; P1 §2.2). Every Board member must be in good standing of the Federation and of their chapter club (6.2.3).
  • Two further standing committees sit in the same list and are never mentioned by practice: the Young Adults Program Committee (8.13.1) and the Youth Program Committee (8.14.1), each with "rules, regulations and policies" the Board establishes. Neither appears in the 2026–27 committee directory as read by the research round (structure only).
  • Members at Large: not a Past President or a chapter president; file at the Mid-Year; elected for two years by secret ballot at the Convention (6.5.1, 6.5.2).
  • Deputy President: three prior years as an active member and four years' service in an elected or appointed position in the club or the Federation, or a proven record of service where no club exists (7.3.2, 9.3.4). This is the one place the by-laws ask for a service record, and it is the reason the path below keeps a dated history of seats.
  • Committees in general: a member in good standing the year before appointment and throughout, with their club (8.1.2); one-year terms; the President appoints standing-committee members and chairs except where a text says otherwise (8.1.3); the Board, the President or the Executive Committee may create any other committee (8.15.1).
  • Government Affairs: "The Board of Directors shall establish a Governmental Affairs Committee to represent this organization In Washington, D.C. with the U.S. State Department, Congress of the United States, and the White House. The chairperson and members of this committee shall be appointed by the Board" (the paragraph under 8.15; 8.2.1 lists it).
  • Regular membership begins at eighteen (4.1.1). Below eighteen a person is a household member, never a member in their own right.
  • ARFECF By-Law 6.3.1 names "Project Hope, Leadership Program, and Internships in Washington" among the Endowment Fund's example purposes.

2.1 The Young Leader Committee of the 8.10 paragraph, as it runs

Practice. A Young Leaders Committee, called the Youth Outreach Committee in the President's letters of 2024–25 and in the October 2025 entities summary: two co-chairs and members listed one or two per club city — several times the five seats the text sets — with a group address and a filled Board seat titled "Young Leaders Representative". No appointment record and no alumni check were found. It runs a winter donation drive among the clubs as a competition with a per-club tally, virtual gatherings for young adults, and Mid-Year events; the Leadership Ramallah minutes call it a "parallel youth pipeline". 2.5 Government Affairs in operation

Practice (Leadership report §6.1; workflow government-affairs).

  1. Local action committees inside chapter clubs (2025–26) for year-round meetings with federal, state and local officials — the 2020 "representative per club" proposal adopted in practice.
  2. A meeting tracker of the committee's own on Microsoft Forms, SharePoint and Power Automate, replacing the spreadsheet the record says was last edited in 2023.
  3. Town halls co-hosted with other village associations: the coordinators draft the agenda, the chair and co-chair review it, the President opens, and an action segment asks members on the call to contact their representatives on a named bill.

2.6 AFRPWorks, against P17

Practice. An initiative of AFRP run from the Executive Committee: the President announced it, the Executive Committee carries it on its agenda, the working group is the President, the Deputy President, the Legal Advisor, the programme director and two member-business owners, and the working group's prototype runs on Airtable. Record. Questions: Q-147 (the owner, now with "an Executive Committee project?" in its detail), Q-155 (browse or brokered), Q-151 (the prototype already holds candidates' profiles outside any rule), Q-153 (the Ramallah partner; a municipal youth innovation hub is a second candidate).

Where it sits on the path. 6. Rules

  1. The path is read from records the person already has; it creates no record of its own and infers nothing from attendance, gifts or seats (D39; P7 §2; design choice).
  2. No rung is a prerequisite for another; nothing auto-enrols; a milestone invitation fires only on an age ladder (D39). The Leadership branch has no milestone invitations.
  3. No person under eighteen has an interest row, a seat, a directory row or their own channel (4.1.1; R6; R7; P7 §2). A minor applies to anything through the household adult who holds the consent power (R6).
  4. A seat exists only with the authority its text names and that authority's dated act; where the text is silent the seat is a register row naming who seated it, labelled "no appointing text" (P1 §2.2; DIR2).
  5. The Young Leader Committee has the five seats of the 8.10 paragraph; a sixth is refused naming the paragraph and Q-179; the alumni check is shown and never refuses (8.10 paragraph; P12 §2.9).
  6. Every Board seat shows the 6.2.3 standing check; every committee seat the 8.1.2 check; the platform tells and ends nothing (P1 rule 3; P3 rule 6).
  7. Seat history is kept when a seat ends, never deleted; a historical seat entered by the office is an attested row naming its drive source, shown apart from platform rows (P1; D66; design choice).
  8. The Deputy President's service test reads seat history and shows attested and platform rows separately; "proven record of service" without a club is a human finding (9.3.4(4); P5 §3.4).
  9. Everything advocacy costs is AFRP's General Fund; an affiliate entity on any advocacy row is refused with no override (D65).
  10. Candidate is refused as a position's subject until the Board decides with the Legal Advisor's and the CPA's advice recorded (P14 §2.1; Q-132).
  11. A cycle runs in the mode its deciding body set for that cycle; a cycle with no mode does not open (design choice, §5.3).
  12. No money line raises for a programme whose holding entity the approved budget does not name (D55; P12-J01): the Senior Award, the internships, the Young Leader Committee's drive.
  13. A giving appeal publishes only under a catalogue purpose naming its receiving entity (D8), with the surplus and shortfall disclosure (D7).
  14. Branch outcomes are counts under the floor; no list of who did or did not progress exists (S7; D67).
  15. Every refusal names its rule and, where one holds it open, its question.
