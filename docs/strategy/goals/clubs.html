Strategic goal

2. Connect the clubs and the Federation

Goal 2 of eight, distilled from the Federation's documents: what was committed, what is open, and how the Hub carries it out.

Distilled from the Federation's documents, for the Strategic Planning Committee to confirm (D52).

All branches

What the Federation has committed to

Services for local clubs and connections between them (2019); club presidents as the welcome contact, a buddy system, and care for dormant and new clubs (2021); clubs driving national sign-ups and families referring families (2023).

What is still open

Club giving, texting and forms wait on David's answers (Breeze Parity §5); the deep read of Breeze on 1 October 2026 showed club giving is two questions, not one (each club's own processor under its own tax number, or the Federation's one door holding and remitting), and that texting in Breeze is a shared short code, one way. Family referral is designed (P4; slices RF1–RF3) and not built. Who approves a club's affiliation, whether a member may hold two clubs, who appoints a club's programme seats and the statement's unwritten terms are Q-40 to Q-47; how local dues are passed on has never been written down (Q-36). The research of 2 October 2026 found the clubs to be separate exempt organisations, three different counts of clubs in use (Q-229), Breeze onboarding presented as a Federation initiative (Q-230), clubs giving to Ramallah projects directly (Q-228) and one club's membership rule wider than By-Law 4.1.1 (Q-226). How a club's Convention delegation is chosen is recorded as the club's own dated act, with its method stated (P22 §6; Q-37).

How the Hub implements it, on the CRM

Clubs work on the same records as the Federation: the club lens, rosters, club groups and follow-ups, club events with check-in, and the remittance ledger that moves dues between them. The club experience plan (design note P18) designs the rest: the officer year from the 1 August form to the hand-over, one statement per club per holding entity (D57), the club's seat in each programme, the move from Breeze club by club, and the Federation's clubs page as counts under the floor. Breeze itself was read module by module on 1 October 2026: it has no membership object, its child check-in code is printed and never checked, its export omits attendance, and only each club's account owner holds the key the import needs (Breeze Parity §1a). The crosswalk's One Federation rows say where money and work cross today.

What no software can do

Each club's decision to move onto the platform, and the monthly club-president calls.
