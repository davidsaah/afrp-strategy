Strategic goal

2. Connect the clubs and the Federation

Goal 2 of eight, distilled from the Federation's documents: what was committed, what is open, and how the Hub carries it out.

Distilled from the Federation's documents, for the Strategic Planning Committee to confirm (D52).

All branches

What the Federation has committed to

Services for local clubs and connections between them (2019); club presidents as the welcome contact, a buddy system, and care for dormant and new clubs (2021); clubs driving national sign-ups and families referring families (2023).

What is still open

Club giving, the import from each club's Breeze account, texting and forms are still to come. Family referral has no design yet.

How the Hub implements it, on the CRM

Clubs work on the same records as the Federation: the club lens, rosters, club groups and follow-ups, club events with check-in, and the remittance ledger that moves dues between them.

What no software can do

Each club's decision to move onto the platform, and the monthly club-president calls.
