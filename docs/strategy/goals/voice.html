Strategic goal

5. Speak with one voice

Goal 5 of eight, distilled from the Federation's documents: what was committed, what is open, and how the Hub carries it out.

Distilled from the Federation's documents, for the Strategic Planning Committee to confirm (D52).

Heritage Leadership

What the Federation has committed to

A communications strategy, a fixed newsletter date, a press-release process, a consistent brand and paid communications staff (2018-2022), and advocacy with elected officials.

What is still open

The email provider is not yet connected. The press workflow (P6) and the volunteer roster (P7) are designed and not built. A Communications Coordinator has not been hired. The research of 2 October 2026 found Government Affairs running local action committees in the clubs and its own tracker (Q-131, Q-183), congressional internships voted without a funding source (Q-182), and the Magazine without the board By-Law 6.7.1 names (Q-192). The Magazine as it is actually run (six issues a year, announcements through correspondents, a subscriber list kept outside the CRM) is designed in P24 (slices MG1–MG7); who chooses whether a Day of Action takes applications or open registration is Q-254.

How the Hub implements it, on the CRM

Messages sent from the CRM to the people who consented to receive them, the comms and website desks, the magazine's operations, and the public site.

What no software can do

The communications strategy itself, the staff role, and relationships with the media and elected officials.
