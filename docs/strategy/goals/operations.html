Strategic goal

8. Run the Federation professionally

Goal 8 of eight, distilled from the Federation's documents: what was committed, what is open, and how the Hub carries it out.

Distilled from the Federation's documents, for the Strategic Planning Committee to confirm (D52).

The Convention

What the Federation has committed to

An Executive Director (planned since 2019), staff, standards for the Mid-Year and the Convention (2021), and the Convention agreement (2022).

What is still open

The Executive Director has not been hired. The Convention element is designed and not built; Convention operations as run, from the award to the close, are designed in P22 (slices CV1–CV8). Mid-Year host bids are designed (P8); the research of 2 October 2026 found the 2020–21 Mid-Year guideline on file, with the Executive Committee choosing the host where 2026–27 practice had the Board choose (Q-92), the 2026 Convention settled under a revised agreement no folder holds (Q-93), and a path set towards a Convention in Ramallah in 2029 (Q-223).

How the Hub implements it, on the CRM

Roles and access for staff, the Convention element (registration, credentials checked against the remittance ledger, the store, settlement), and events.

What no software can do

Hiring the Executive Director, which the 2024 by-laws require to live in the headquarters area.
