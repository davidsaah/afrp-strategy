Strategic goal

3. Bring in and keep the next generation

Goal 3 of eight, distilled from the Federation's documents: what was committed, what is open, and how the Hub carries it out.

Distilled from the Federation's documents, for the Strategic Planning Committee to confirm (D52).

Education Leadership

What the Federation has committed to

"Attract and retain young adults, the next generation of Ramallah leadership" (2021). A youth pipeline from childhood to professional life (2019); young people on every committee (2020); a successor in every programme (2025).

What is still open

Several programmes on the Education and Leadership branches exist only as a plan. The invitation from one programme's top rung to the next programme is not yet built.

How the Hub implements it, on the CRM

Each person's place on the branches and on each programme's five rungs sits on their record. The platform can therefore count how many campers became members, instead of guessing.

What no software can do

Recruiting young leaders into committee seats, and changing the by-laws to require them.
