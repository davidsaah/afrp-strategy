Strategic goal

7. Keep the heritage and the memory

Goal 7 of eight, distilled from the Federation's documents: what was committed, what is open, and how the Hub carries it out.

Distilled from the Federation's documents, for the Strategic Planning Committee to confirm (D52).

Heritage

What the Federation has committed to

The Cultural Preservation Project and the family tree (2019); archives, after "No archives" in the 2018 SWOT; a central repository (2026).

What is still open

The shared drive is the archive of record and the platform links to it (D66). Adoption, and three spouse questions under By-Law 4.1.1, are open for the tree. The research of 2 October 2026 found the tree kept in one person's desktop genealogy file with one approver (Q-200, Q-201), the archive spread across four places including a second archive-and-tree site (Q-190), and oral histories recorded on a spoken permission (Q-189). On 2 October 2026 David decided how the tree is run on the platform (D68 to D74); the move from today's file is designed in P27 and the module in P28. The parallel run that makes the Hub the tree's record waits on a production database with backups and on the terms for holding the tree data (Q-201).

How the Hub implements it, on the CRM

The family tree sits on the Heritage branch with a node for every member, and a node is never a lineage claim (D30, D32, D53). It is drawn in the printed book's plate style (D68); members in standing propose changes and the committee approves them in tiers (D70, D72); a signed-in member sees living relatives in full only inside their own branch (D71). The magazine archive and the document registries sit alongside it.

What no software can do

The Family Tree Committee's judgements and its clan stewards (Q-263), collecting oral histories, and keeping the shared drive in order as the archive of record (D66).
