Strategic goal

7. Keep the heritage and the memory

Goal 7 of eight, distilled from the Federation's documents: what was committed, what is open, and how the Hub carries it out.

Distilled from the Federation's documents, for the Strategic Planning Committee to confirm (D52).

Heritage

What the Federation has committed to

The Cultural Preservation Project and the family tree (2019); archives, after "No archives" in the 2018 SWOT; a central repository (2026).

What is still open

The shared drive is the archive of record and the platform links to it (D66). Adoption, and three spouse questions under By-Law 4.1.1, are open for the tree.

How the Hub implements it, on the CRM

The family tree is the CRM's spine: every member has a node, and a node is never a lineage claim. The magazine archive and the document registries sit alongside it.

What no software can do

The Family Tree Committee's judgements, collecting oral histories, and keeping the shared drive in order as the archive of record (D66).
