Strategic goal

6. Fund the mission, and show the return

Goal 6 of eight, distilled from the Federation's documents: what was committed, what is open, and how the Hub carries it out.

Distilled from the Federation's documents, for the Strategic Planning Committee to confirm (D52).

Care

What the Federation has committed to

A budget for each programme and giving that is acknowledged (2019); an endowment campaign and outcome metrics that show donors a return (2026).

What is still open

The endowment waits on its Policy Statement. The contributor moments are designed but not built. How outcomes are measured for each branch is not yet defined.

How the Hub implements it, on the CRM

Funds and restricted purposes on one ledger across AFRP, ARFECF and ARFHSN, one door for every payment, and outcome reporting for each branch drawn from the same records.

What no software can do

Fundraising, grant writing, the endowment campaign itself, and the Policy Statement.
