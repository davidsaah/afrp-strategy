Strategic goal

6. Fund the mission, and show the return

Goal 6 of eight, distilled from the Federation's documents: what was committed, what is open, and how the Hub carries it out.

Distilled from the Federation's documents, for the Strategic Planning Committee to confirm (D52).

Care

What the Federation has committed to

A budget for each programme and giving that is acknowledged (2019); an endowment campaign and outcome metrics that show donors a return (2026).

What is still open

The endowment waits on its Policy Statement. The contributor moments are designed but not built. How outcomes are measured for each branch is not yet defined. The research of 2 October 2026 found the books already moving towards the design (a card processor per entity, QuickBooks fed from the CRM, an audit for ARFECF), four roles answering to "the CPA" (Q-231), a single fundraising strategy promised before any new endowed fund (Q-210), and Senior Living to be the Ramallah Foundation's home (Q-203). The Relief Fund as it runs, and the Federation's part in the Foundation's home, are designed in P23 (slices RL1–RL6); the Relief Fund's holding entity is AFRP (D83, 3 October 2026), and Q-109 stays open on transfers between votes.

How the Hub implements it, on the CRM

Funds and restricted purposes on one ledger across AFRP, ARFECF and ARFHSN, one door for every payment, and outcome reporting for each branch drawn from the same records.

What no software can do

Fundraising, grant writing, the endowment campaign itself, and the Policy Statement.
