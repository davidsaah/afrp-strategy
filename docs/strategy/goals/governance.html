Strategic goal

4. Govern well, and be seen to

Goal 4 of eight, distilled from the Federation's documents: what was committed, what is open, and how the Hub carries it out.

Distilled from the Federation's documents, for the Strategic Planning Committee to confirm (D52).

All branches

What the Federation has committed to

A constitutional review (2019); job descriptions, the Selection Committee, committee reporting and enforcement of good standing (2020-2021); the 2024 by-laws adopted part of this.

What is still open

Committees have no workspace yet; it is designed in P1 (slices CW1–CW5) and not built. The Selection Committee's workflow waits on the 2021 proposal being filed. The four-amendment package is not yet calendared. The research of 2 October 2026 found no written path for approving the budget (Q-234) and a Young Leader Committee that does not match By-Law 8.10 (Q-179).

How the Hub implements it, on the CRM

The by-laws held as rules, standing computed from the CRM, and elections and digital voting on a frozen electorate. Committee records and reports are designed (P1) and not yet built.

What no software can do

The Board's and the General Assembly's votes, and the amendments themselves.
