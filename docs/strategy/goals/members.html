Strategic goal

1. Know and serve every member

Goal 1 of eight, distilled from the Federation's documents: what was committed, what is open, and how the Hub carries it out.

Distilled from the Federation's documents, for the Strategic Planning Committee to confirm (D52).

All branches

What the Federation has committed to

One member database, online dues including the Manara tier, member demographics and retention. The 2018 SWOT named the gap plainly: "No database/CRM". The 2019 plan made a CRM the first IT goal; the committee returned to it in 2020, 2021 and 2023.

What is still open

The platform exists but is not yet in service. Nobody has yet been named to maintain it after launch, and no budget line pays for it. The research of 2 October 2026 found today's CRM already holding non-members as contacts, with no consent or retention rule, and its occupation field used for outreach (Q-70, Q-236); the Board recorded in May 2026 that nothing tracks membership standing centrally. One record for every non-member the platform must hold, with dated roles and a consent and retention period for each, is designed in P21 (slices CR1–CR7); whether it is adopted is Q-70.

How the Hub implements it, on the CRM

This goal is the CRM itself: one record for each person, their household, their clubs, their standing and every payment, in one ledger. Every other goal builds on it. The directory the Federation once printed comes back in three formats, behind sign-in only: the members' directory with membership and club shown, at Federation and club scale; the professional services directory; and the leadership directory of seats and whether each seat votes (design note P19).

What no software can do

Deciding who maintains the system, funding it every year, and moving members onto it. Whether members may be found "near me" by distance, which needs a consent nobody has given (Q-48), and who the community contacts are for places without a club (Q-49).
