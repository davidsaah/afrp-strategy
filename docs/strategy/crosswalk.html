The crosswalk

Every element of the Federation's strategic plans, 2018 to 2026, with what the platform does about each.

Each row is one commitment or request from the Federation's documents. The source column uses short names: SP19 the Strategic Plan 2018–2023, FED2 the 2020–21 Recommendations, TASKS the subcommittee task list, MOU the Convention agreement, MIN the committee's minutes 2020–2023, APP the app requirements, PR the communications papers, SWOT25 the 2025 committee responses, and SP26 the minutes of 10 September 2026.

The platform column says how far the Hub has got. Built means built and tested, though nothing is in service yet. Plan only means a register row and a written plan with no working screens. Proposed means it needs a design note first. Outside means it is a hire, a policy or a vote, not software. Gap means nothing in the record accounts for it.
