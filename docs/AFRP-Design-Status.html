Decision 5 — the two By-Law 9.1.3 amendment drafts

Reopened 21 August after being recorded in error. Whether either goes to the floor is undecided. Both are 2/3 delegate-vote matters, which means 9.1.3's weighted fractional voting applies to the amendments that fix 9.1.3.

Board · Constitution Committee
The directory grace parameter

One number. After dues lapse, how long does a member stay visible in the directory? It defaults to 0 — an instant disappearance, almost certainly not the intent. 90 days proposed. The platform will not invent it, and labels its own proposal a proposal.

The text admits a person “married to one having such origin”. It is gender-neutral and names no restriction.

Membership Committee → Board
Does a membership survive the divorce it was admitted through?

A member admitted under “married to one having such origin” divorces that spouse. The by-laws are silent.

Membership Committee
Which club does a minor in two households belong to?

Answered in part: pickup runs through a release list, and directory choices take the more protective reading. Club attachment is ratified as staying open — the platform holds both and keeps asking, because it carries dues and franchise consequences and By-Law 4.3.1 is silent.

Membership Committee
Adoption

An adopted child may be wholly a family member socially and not a descendant genealogically, and the tree's premise is that descent is evidence. Until this is answered the platform records an adoptive link as non-lineage, says so plainly, and infers nothing.

Membership Committee · Family Tree Committee
Does a suspension recorded after certification withhold the ballot?

The roll is frozen so nobody can be disenfranchised after it. But a member suspended on the morning of the poll still holds a roll entry. Both readings are defensible and the wrong pick touches the integrity floor.

Board · Elections Committee
A Scholarship Committee no by-law constitutes

AFRP members are certified to vote for members of a Scholarship Committee that no AFRP by-law creates. With ARFECF confirmed as the scholarship's home this is a cross-entity election, which works but must be written down.

Constitution Committee
How the design changed · and what it supersedes
Rails became branches, and each branch got a shape

The four programme pathways were renamed to hold the family-tree motif, and re-cut in the same move rather than relabelled: RBPN left the youth pipeline for Leadership, the Arabic Program moved to Education, Emerging Leaders to Leadership, the bookstore joined Heritage, Senior Living joined Care. More importantly, only one of the four is genuinely a sequence. Education ladders by age; Leadership by capacity; Heritage is a cluster you use for life; Care is a set of destinations. A model that assumed otherwise would have invited people to advance from the Preservation Project to the magazine.

D38 · D39 — supersedes the four rails in the prototype
The family tree stopped being a programme

It was one of nineteen. It is now the roots: every member resolves through a node on it, and Education, Leadership and Care grow from it. The Convention came off the branches too and became the junction where all four meet — reachable from each, a rung on none.

D30 · D38
R6 went from inferred to ratified in a week

“Who may act for a minor” was inferred in the rules register — thirteen citations and no document stating it. Three build slices refused work rather than guess a rule about children. A document was written, routed to the Membership Committee, Camp Ramallah and the Legal Advisor, ratified as drafted, and built against. The refusals it caused were not waste; they are why nothing was invented.

R6 · D34 · D35
The tree stopped being able to decide anything

D16 always said the tree is evidence and never a verdict. Two later decisions made that structural rather than aspirational: a node is not a lineage claim (D30), and the new edition of the book carries no eligibility force (D32) — so platform data cannot quietly become the test that By-Law 4.1.1 points at.

D16 · D30 · D32
How to read the prototype. It is a first pass, and a substantial one: most of the major user stories are in it, walked end to end, and it is where this platform's design system comes from. What it is thin on is logic — the rules behind the screens, the edge cases, the by-law mechanics — and in places it is inconsistent with itself. So it is strong evidence of what someone is trying to do and weak evidence of what the rule is. Where it and the by-laws or the decisions register disagree on a rule, they win and the divergence is recorded rather than settled by whoever noticed it. It also predates the changes above: it still shows the four rails (youth pipeline · advocacy · heritage · Palestine-direct) and the family tree as one programme among nineteen. The screens are kept as they were rather than quietly edited into agreement — corrections appear at the same weight as the claim they replace — and resolving those gaps is part of the build, not a prerequisite to it.
