AFRP design delivery · the map

The Platform Map — one system, three institutions

v1.1 · 19 Aug 2026 · every formerly-missing element now designed — see The Complete Platform

The build badges below are dated. They were audited on 19 August against AFRP-Portal, which is now the design record rather than the application. The application is AFRP-Hub, and its current state is on Design Status — 1,106 tests, 374 journeys catalogued, 271 walked. Several rows badged “designed, not built” here have since been built.

This is the index of the design, not another mockup: every module of the platform, organized by the institution that owns it — AFRP the federation, ARFECF the Education & Charitable Fund, ARFHSN the Human Services Network — each badged for consistency with the backend prototype and linked to its canonical frames in the narrative documents, the focused v1.0 documents, and the interactive prototype.

Below the map sits the element matrix: every element raised across the project, where it lives, and — in deliberate red — what has no screen anywhere yet. The matrix is the working definition of done and not-done. Governing texts are versioned rulesets: bylaws-2012.2 in force, bylaws-2024.1 parallel with 48 provisional overrides across three entity-lineages (18 AFRP · 22 ARFECF · 8 ARFHSN), arfecf-2013, arfhsn-2017.
