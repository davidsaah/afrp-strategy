Club lens

Club treasurer

The officer who collects dues and club money and remits AFRP's share.

Lens
Club
What they need
  • Collect, close, remit and reconcile each month
  • Keep AFRP money and club money apart
  • See on one statement what the club is owed and what it owes across every programme
  • Take the club's own income without a second system
  • Hand the books to a successor at the end of the officer year
  • When the club hosts, reconcile the Convention with the Federation on one sheet and be paid by a channel the bank allows (Q-221, Q-222)
  • Know whose money a closed branch balance held by an affiliate is (Q-216)
  • Record the club's co-funding of a Care grant once its trustee confirms it in the affiliate's meeting
  • When the club hosts, see the per-registration Relief Fund amount post as one dated line to the liability 'the entity to be named' at the close (P22 §5.3; P23 §3; Q-243)
  • Record a club gift to the Ramallah Foundation's home made through the Federation as a line on the club's statement, if Q-205 allows it (P23 §9.4)
What the platform does for them
  • Split at capture of national and club dues
  • A remittance statement with carry-forward
  • Chargebacks after remittance handled as a receivable
  • The club-share statement (D57) with a line per programme term and a dated close
  • The club's ledger profile, agency against revenue
  • Club giving through the one payment door onto the club's books: proposed (B3), waiting on David's answer
  • The year-end hand-over as a dated change of seat with the balance carried, never a copy of the data
  • The host's balance shown as due to the host until paid, with netted items as their own lines (Q-221)
  • A relief drive's per-club tally, with its receiving entity named (Q-180)

Rules that protect them

  • Collected AFRP dues are custodial on the club's books, never revenue (D1)
  • The treasurer reads only their own club's dues
  • A gift to a club lands on the club's books and the member's statement for that club; a purpose outside the club's entity refuses by name
  • The Executive Director's access to Chapter Club records (By-Law 7.9.3) needs a named purpose and an audit line; the Federation otherwise reads counts
  • Today's per-payment 'due to local club' figure is gross after the discount and the card cost; it is not the settlement (Q-127)
