Club treasurer

The officer who collects dues and club money and remits AFRP's share.

Lens
Club
What they need
  • Collect, close, remit and reconcile each month
  • Keep AFRP money and club money apart
What the platform does for them
  • Split at capture of national and club dues
  • A remittance statement with carry-forward
  • Chargebacks after remittance handled as a receivable

Rules that protect them

  • Collected AFRP dues are custodial on the club's books, never revenue (D1)
  • The treasurer reads only their own club's dues
