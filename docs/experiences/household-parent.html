Household parent or guardian

An adult acting for minors in a household: registering, applying to camp, declaring births and naming who may collect a child.

Member
What they need
  • Act for a child without guesswork
  • Apply to camp and register the family
  • Know what a partner or step-parent may do
What the platform does for them
  • R6's four authority paths and enumerated powers
  • A release list writable only by a standing holder
  • Life-event declarations queued to the committee

Rules that protect them

  • The more protective answer wins when holders disagree (D35)
  • A step-parent holds nothing until named (D34)
  • A child's announcement is withdrawable and re-asked at eighteen (D33)
