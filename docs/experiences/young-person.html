Young person

A minor in a member household, and the same person at eighteen when their consents become their own.

Lens
Member
What they need
  • Take part in camp and programmes safely
  • At eighteen, own their channel and consents
What the platform does for them
  • A refresh window before eighteen that asks them on their own channel
  • Education-branch invitations at milestones, never enrolment

Rules that protect them

  • Minors never appear in any directory payload; no birth date means possible minor (R7)
  • Camp cabins ship as counts and bands, never a minor's id
  • Campers have no account; a CIT gets a limited one
