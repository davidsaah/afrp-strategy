Young person

A minor in a member household, and the same person at eighteen when their consents become their own.

Lens
Member
What they need
  • Take part in camp and programmes safely
  • At eighteen, own their channel and consents
  • Apply for the Outstanding High School Senior Award, which continues (D82), through the household adult (R6) ; the Scholarship Fund Committee runs it (D85)
  • Volunteer on a programme's team (a website rebuild asked for 16-to-22-year-olds in 2026) under a rule (Q-191)
  • At eighteen, see my own path page: what I have taken part in and the Leadership and Education programmes open to me, as invitations to look (P25 §3; D39)
What the platform does for them
  • A refresh window before eighteen that asks them on their own channel
  • Education-branch invitations at milestones, never enrolment
  • Camp ages 13 to 17 on the record and the public page; the 2025 annual report says 13 to 18 (Q-167)
  • The path view (slice YL1) from eighteen; the Senior Award shown as continuing (D82), with no form, cycle or letter until a budget line exists (D55); the Scholarship Fund Committee runs it (D85) (P25 §5.1; YL6)

Rules that protect them

  • Minors never appear in any directory payload; no birth date means possible minor (R7)
  • Camp cabins ship as counts and bands, never a minor's id
  • Campers have no account; a CIT gets a limited one
  • Past Senior Award applications from minors are kept or deleted on a rule not yet written (Q-76)
  • A minor volunteer needs supervision and consent rules before being taken on (Q-191)
  • No interest row, seat or own channel for a seventeen-year-old; at eighteen the person is asked directly (P25 §6; R6)
  • A seventeen-year-old cannot be recorded as an archive volunteer or an interviewer (P27 §7.3; P7)
