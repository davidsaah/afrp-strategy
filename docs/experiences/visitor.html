Front door lens

Visitor

Someone not signed in: a prospective member, a relative or a member of the public reading about AFRP. Also a non-member the platform holds as a contact (a learner, applicant, subscriber, candidate or giver), who signs in with a verified channel to see only their own record (P21 §4).

Lens
Front door
What they need
  • Understand what AFRP and its programmes are
  • Find a way to join
  • Browse the bookstore and reach the public giving door without signing in
  • Register for an Arabic class at the public rate without joining (D60)
  • Know before checkout that the stores ship only to US addresses
  • As a non-member contact, see my own record: each role, its scope, source and date, its consent rows, and a withdrawal for each (P21 §4)
  • Apply to a second programme on the same channel and be asked to sign in, so the new role joins my one record (P21 §7.1)
  • Offer my time to the Medical Mission and be answered by the office (in practice, research 2 Oct 2026; Q-87)
  • Read a back issue of the magazine signed out, with no page naming a living person (P24 §7)
What the platform does for them
  • Public site and programme pages from the programme register
  • One front door to join or sign in
  • Public job postings with complete markup
  • The Arabic class door at the public rate (D60); the public page still says members only (Q-171)
  • Two online stores today, on the website and the member portal, with the same titles and no member price (in practice, research 2 Oct 2026) (Q-197)
  • One person record with dated roles for every non-member the platform holds; no role is a membership, a directory row, a tree node or a count as a member (P21 §3; D60)
  • Operational messages about my own transaction only; a next-term or next-cycle invitation waits on Q-70 and is not asked for at the door (P21 §8)
  • The shipping rule is a catalogue parameter shown on the catalogue page before the cart; while it is unset, checkout refuses and names Q-197 (P24 §8.2)
  • A purchase is never a gift: no product, order or receipt carries a deductibility statement (P24 §8.2; UC-11)

Rules that protect them

  • Nothing about living people shows without sign-in (D28)
  • Sign-in and recovery are existence-blind
  • Living people in the public build are synthetic (D15)
  • A role is read only in its owning scope; one programme's consent row is never offered to another programme's audience (P21 §5, §8)
  • No consent row is created by a migration; an imported occupation or date of birth is not loaded onto the person (P21 §7.5)
  • Withdrawing a role closes it at once; its fields wait for a retention clock that has no value yet, so nothing is deleted (P21 §9)
  • A door whose person does not attest to being an adult creates no contact (R7; P21 §3)
  • Signed out, a tree plate shows the deceased in full and nothing about the living (D28; P28 §5)
  • An oral-history narrator who withdraws is removed from every platform surface the same day; a printed page cannot be recalled and the consent screen says so (P27 §6)
