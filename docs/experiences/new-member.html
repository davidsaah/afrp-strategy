Front door lens

New member

A person joining for the first time, alone, as a family, as a student or as a newlywed.

Lens
Front door
What they need
  • A join that cannot be captured by a mistyped email
  • Clear dues and what they buy
  • A first login that says what to set up next
  • Find myself on the family tree at join or renewal, or skip the step, and have either be a complete answer (D73)
  • Join after an Arabic class or an application on the same channel and keep that history (P21 §7.2)
What the platform does for them
  • Channel verification before any payment or PII (S1)
  • A tree node on join, never a lineage claim (D30)
  • The Associate route offered where the genealogy cannot reach
  • A first contact through an Arabic class counted when the learner later joins; some learners joined this way (in practice, research 2 Oct 2026)
  • A 'Find yourself on the family tree' step at join and renewal: search by own name or the oldest ancestor I can name; candidates shown, the platform never picks; a link proposal queues and the node stays unattached meanwhile (D73; P28 §4.4)
  • A contact who joins confirms the record is theirs; the membership is added, each role and its consents carry over unchanged, and no role consent becomes a member consent (P21 §7.2)
  • A walk-in at the Convention desk who is not in standing is offered dues and registration in one payment as separate lines (P22 §5.4)

Rules that protect them

  • Consent at join is per field with its source recorded
  • A minor's consent is held by the household and re-asked at eighteen
  • No password can exist
  • Today's registration system makes a vetted account with a profile before any membership or consent; the platform's contact record is narrower (D60; Q-70)
  • No membership decision reads the tree node (D30, D73)
  • A staff seat cannot link a contact to a member on a name match without the person's confirmation (P21 §7.2)
