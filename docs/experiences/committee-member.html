Committee member

A member serving on a committee such as the Family Tree, Scholarship or Membership Committee.

Lens
Federation
What they need
  • A queue of decisions with evidence
  • Conflicts disclosed on the record
  • Authority that is theirs alone where the by-laws say so
What the platform does for them
  • The tree committee queue, triaged
  • Scholarship review with median ranking
  • Open questions routed by name (R40)
  • Their seat on the leadership directory, with its dates, whether it votes, and a seat channel instead of personal contact details (P19)

Rules that protect them

  • Refusals name the rule, not the relatives (S6)
  • A declined invitation is never visible to a committee
  • Fund committees hold exclusive expenditure authority
