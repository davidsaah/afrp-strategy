Committee member

A member serving on a committee such as the Family Tree, Scholarship or Membership Committee. Also a clan steward the Family Tree Committee appoints, who pre-reviews and recommends for one clan and never approves (D72; Q-263).

Lens
Federation
What they need
  • A queue of decisions with evidence
  • Conflicts disclosed on the record
  • Authority that is theirs alone where the by-laws say so
  • Women to Women: approve a project and see its receipts against it (Q-114)
  • ARFHSN board: dispose of each funding request one of four ways with a date (Q-217)
  • Scholarship Committee: assign a named scholarship under its donor's criteria (Q-102)
  • Family Tree Committee: see the change-request form's rows in the queue rather than in one person's inbox (D14). *Since 2 October 2026:* the form's rows are not imported; members propose in the platform and earlier requests arrive through the record keeper's export, labelled as made outside the queue (P27 §4.3; D70)
  • Family Tree Committee: run the parallel run by dated acts, decide each conflict, and see the nightly backup row (D69; P28 §4.3)
  • Clan steward: annotate and recommend accept, decline or needs information for my clan, and apply nothing (D72)
  • Convention Innovation Committee: keep a sponsor-prospect list by region with who is working each prospect, and enter past Conventions as attested history rows (P22 §7.2, §11; Q-245)
  • Scholarship Committee: suspend or cancel an award only by a decision item with the seats present, and reconcile the named-scholarship list to instruments (P26 §2.4, §5)
What the platform does for them
  • The tree committee queue, triaged; one approval applies an own-household life event or a non-structural correction, and a structural change needs a second key from a different committee member (D72; P28 §4.2)
  • Scholarship review with median ranking
  • Open questions routed by name (R40)
  • Their seat on the leadership directory, with its dates, whether it votes, and a seat channel instead of personal contact details (P19)
  • The Care grant record with its ceiling, two signatories and three closing records (D63)
  • The steward's pre-review on the clan's queue; the structural kinds read from the rules-register row D81 set, which the committee may amend by a dated act (P28 §4.2, §6)
  • The conflict list during D69's parallel run: a change made on both sides since the last export is held with both readings; after the run an external import is refused naming D69 (P28 §4.3)
  • A nightly GEDCOM backup under tree:moderate, each night a dated row and a failed night shown on the console (D69)
  • The custody page: licence holder, account and cadence shown as "not stated (Q-201)", the last export imported against the cadence, and the queue's counts (P27 §3, §4.4)
  • The Young Leader Committee as the text reads: five seats, the sixth refused naming the 8.10 paragraph and Q-179 (P25 §4)
  • The Scholarship Committee's seats as the 2015 policy reads; a "Foundation representative" seat refused naming D58; an attendee without a seat in no tally (P26 §3.1)

Rules that protect them

  • Refusals name the rule, not the relatives (S6)
  • A declined invitation is never visible to a committee
  • Fund committees hold exclusive expenditure authority
  • Applicant data for Women to Women's tuition awards: its class and holder are not stated (Q-211)
  • Who approves a Women to Women project is open between the committee and ARFHSN's Board (Q-114)
  • The same committee member cannot give both keys on a structural change; the second key-holder is a committee member the committee seats (D72, D79)
  • A steward recommends and never applies (D72)
