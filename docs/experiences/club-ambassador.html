Club lens

Club ambassador in a programme

The club's seat in one programme — the camp liaison, the scholarship outreach contact, the Day of Action travel coordinator, the Arabic volunteers' lead, the dabke lead, the Mid-Year sponsor-outreach lead. In practice also the magazine's city correspondent, the club city's Young Leaders Committee member and the club's local action committee (research, 2 Oct 2026).

Lens
Club
What they need
  • See the programme from the club's side (this club's families, scholars, travellers, volunteers)
  • See what the club owes or is owed for them
  • Pass news both ways between the club and the programme
  • As the magazine's correspondent, collect announcements and remind members to renew
  • As the club city's Young Leaders member, run the club's part of a relief drive and see the tally (Q-180)
  • As Women to Women's city representative, be listed with the yearly minimum recorded (in practice; Q-213)
  • As the club city's Young Leaders member, host a virtual gathering or a Mid-Year young-adult event (in practice, research 2 Oct 2026)
What the platform does for them
  • A programme seat scoped to one club, appointed on the officers form or after it, dated, expiring with the officer year
  • The programme's roster filtered to the ambassador's own club
  • The club's lines on the club-share statement for that programme (D57)
  • As the magazine's correspondent, an assisted submission on a household's behalf, printing on my recorded attestation that the family agreed (D89; P24 §3.2)
  • The club's programme seat sees accepted participants from the club and counts of the rest; a learner, subscriber, applicant or candidate is never named to it (P21 §10)

Rules that protect them

  • A programme seat does not grant the club lens, and a club seat does not grant the programme lens
  • Minors in a programme are a count to the ambassador unless the R6 authority model names them
  • The ambassador sees nothing of other clubs in the same programme
  • A correspondent enters a household's life event on a recorded attestation that the family agreed (D89)
  • A drive's tally shows the club's total, never who gave
