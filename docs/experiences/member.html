Member lens

Member

A signed-in member with a national membership and usually one or more club memberships.

Lens
Member
What they need
  • See each membership's standing and date
  • Renew, give, register and vote
  • Control what others see
  • Register for the Day of Action by one published deadline, free as an adult member, with first-timer costs covered (in practice, research 2 Oct 2026)
  • Send a wedding, birth, graduation or obituary once and have it reach the tree and the magazine
  • Propose a family-tree change while in current national standing and track it (D70), and ask for clan-book access and be told the test (Q-200)
  • Take the action a town hall asks for and contact my representative (Q-133)
  • See my own plate in the book's grammar, my living relatives in full inside my own branch (D68, D71)
  • See my own path: my programme alumni records, interests and seats in date order, with programmes I have not taken part in as links, never as a next step (P25 §3; D39)
  • Apply for a sponsored Arabic-class place with a short statement and hear the outcome before registration closes (in practice, research 2 Oct 2026; Q-168)
  • Register for an RBPN speaker event and be reminded (Q-181)
  • Submit a photograph or document with its consent class and a credit, linked to a tree node, through the platform (D66; D22; P27 §6.4)
  • Read the digital issue on the platform rather than from an email link (Q-196)
  • As a family, submit an obituary with its tribute and pay the placement, approved through the family contact (R34; P24 §3.3; Q-193)
  • Register a guest for the Convention as a name and a credential class, not a contact (P21 §4; Q-238)
  • Forward a relative's late scholarship application and be told the rule (Q-117; Q-257)
What the platform does for them
  • Home with an attention list and two standing pills
  • Scoped standing: national and club lapse independently
  • Ballots with the reason when ineligible
  • My branches and the next step on each
  • {'The directory in three formats, signed in': 'members with membership and club shown, professional services, and the leadership seats with their votes (design note P19)'}
  • A registration door for programmes that take registration rather than applications (the Day of Action in 2026; Q-115)
  • The change-request form's rows land in the Family Tree Committee's queue (D14). *Since 2 October 2026:* the form's rows are not imported; a member proposes in the platform and requests already sent by form arrive through the record keeper's next export (P27 §4.3; D70)
  • The plate: one plate per tree, a trunk to the founder, each limb carrying its grade, the continuation marks opening in place at phone width (D68; P28 §3). *Since 3 October 2026:* the plate is the default view on every surface and the hourglass stays available as an alternative view (D77), drawn in the plate grammar (D80)
  • Edit mode on a plate: add a child or a spouse, correct a name or date, record a death, attach a source, flag a duplicate or propose a parent; submit the sitting as one bundle and track it as pending, needs information, accepted or declined, with the committee's reason (D70, D21; P28 §4.1)
  • Archive items linked to a deceased person's node show on that person's page to signed-in members, each under its consent row (P27 §7.2)
  • The path view of one's own record, seen by nobody else (P25 §3; slice YL1)
  • The unified Convention registration: a banquet seat from the sub-event's capacity, refunds under terms published before opening, dues paid with registration standing for the year (P22 §5)
  • A graduation is an announcement on my record, printed only with my print consent; a correspondent's entry is pending until I confirm it once (P24 §3.1, §3.2)
  • An appeal reaches me on the channel I consented to, never chosen by my age (P24 §5.5)

Rules that protect them

  • Display, contact and export are three separate consents (R27)
  • A declined invitation is recorded nowhere a committee can see
  • All members see the whole tree, a recorded choice (D17). *Since 2 October 2026:* a member sees the living in full only inside their own branch and their spouses (out to third cousins, a common great-great-grandparent; D76), and outside it a name and a position on the plate (D71; P28 §5)
  • A constituent story or a town-hall action is not a member record until consent and retention are stated (Q-184, Q-133)
  • Outreach by a profile field such as occupation waits on consent rows (Q-236)
  • Clan-book access follows D17 (now D71) and D28, not a personal approval; a book carrying living details is not offered by standing (P27 §5; Q-200, Q-202)
  • Own branch runs along blood links only; a step or adoptive link never widens it (D71; P28 §5). Whether relatives inside it see minors' dates is Q-268
  • A lapsed member cannot propose a tree change; no account exists for a non-member to contribute (D70)
  • No plate, sheet or search result says whether a person is a member (D30; P28 §3.2)
  • No member-facing export of the tree exists; the nightly backup is the committee's (D69)
  • The path view is mine alone; a committee sees only what its own scope already shows (P25 §3)
