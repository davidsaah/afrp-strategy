Club president

The president of a chapter club, who files the officers each year, sits on the Council of Chapter Club Presidents and the Federation Board, and brings the delegation to the Convention.

Lens
Club
What they need
  • Know the club is affiliated and in good standing for the year
  • File the officers once and have every Federation list read from it
  • See the club's health as the Federation will see it
  • Bring the delegation to the Convention credentialed
  • Bid to host a Mid-Year or a Convention
What the platform does for them
  • One officers form a year, started from last year's, which is the By-Law 3.2 filing and the source of the Council, Board and Membership Committee seats that derive from it
  • A standing pill for the club (affiliated for the year, filed on, approved on)
  • The club dashboard as the first screen (roster counts, dues position, open follow-ups, next events, the club-share balance)
  • The delegation selected from certified members (By-Laws 9.1.3, 9.1.4) and handed to the convention workflow
  • The host-bid form and the host agreement on the event (D59)

Rules that protect them

  • The president must be in good standing of club and Federation, because every compliant president sits on the Board (By-Laws 6.2.2, 6.2.3, 4.3.1); the president's seat is refused to a lapsed member by name; other seats follow the club's own laws (3.1) and are shown, not refused
  • The president reads the whole club and nothing of any other club
  • The officers' contact details on the form are the office's and the Council's, never the directory's (R27)
