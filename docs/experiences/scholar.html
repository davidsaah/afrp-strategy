Scholar

A Scholarship recipient, new or continuing, on the undergraduate or medical track in the United States or Ramallah, whose award is paid in instalments.

Lens
Member
What they need
  • Know what to file after each semester to have the next instalment released
  • Know what happens after a bereavement or a hardship before the payment is late
  • Receive the award letter and the first cheque without chasing
  • See on my own page each instalment's state, what is still missing for the next one and the date the office received each item (P26 §2.2)
  • Ask for hardship by category and have the account I give stay outside the platform (P26 §2.4)
What the platform does for them
  • A semester filing (transcript, full-time enrolment with the next schedule, cumulative GPA) checked by the office before release (in practice, research 2 Oct 2026) (Q-159)
  • A missing-documents letter that says what is missing and by when (in practice, research 2 Oct 2026)
  • A free student membership on award (J06 step 6)
  • Instalment states: scheduled, due, verified, released, held, suspended, cancelled; three verification rows per instalment, each a dated act with no content; release once all three are recorded (P26 §2.1–§2.3)
  • A missed filing holds the instalment and never lapses it; only the committee's act suspends or cancels, and the window that turns due into held has no value until Q-256 sets it (P26 §2.4)
  • Letters on P3's register in ARFECF's name; a letter row with no signatory is a draft the office sees and is not sent (P26 §4; Q-158)

Rules that protect them

  • Applicant material never enters the Hub; the platform records references only (scholarship spec)
  • Recipients' names are never published; counts and amounts by track are (in practice, research 2 Oct 2026)
  • A named scholarship's donor sees which award carries the name, by count, never the scholar (Q-102)
  • Relationships the tree sees are disclosed with their degree on review (D16)
  • No transcript, schedule or GPA figure enters the platform; the verification row records only that the item is on file (P26 §2.2; D61)
  • An instalment is seen only by the scholar, the office's seat, the committee's seats and the fund's treasurer (P26 §2.2; R39)
