Club lens

A member, seen from the club

An ordinary member of a chapter club — what the club lens shows them of themselves, and what it never shows of anyone else.

Lens
Club
What they need
  • See their own club standing beside their national standing
  • See their own groups, attendance and statement with the club
  • Control what the club may send them
  • Move clubs or leave one
What the platform does for them
  • Scoped standing on the member's home (built)
  • The member's own groups (B1) and own attendance (B2) on their record
  • The member's statement with the club: proposed with B3
  • A request to move clubs opens a secretary's-queue item in both clubs

Rules that protect them

  • Display, contact and export are three separate consents (R27); a club mailing honours the contact consent
  • A follow-up about a member is the club's record, not shown to the member; whether the member may know one exists is a question for the Legal Advisor
  • The member never sees another member's groups, follow-ups or attendance
  • A certified member may vote independently at the Convention and the vote is deducted from the club's delegation (By-Law 9.1.3)
