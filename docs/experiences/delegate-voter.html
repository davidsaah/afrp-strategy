Delegate and voter

A member voting in federation elections, and a delegate carrying a club's weighted vote at convention.

Lens
Member
What they need
  • Know whether they can vote and why not
  • Cast a secret ballot and verify it
  • Vote through a delegate or extract to vote directly
  • Be checked in at the desk so that my presence counts on the floor (9.1.3; P22 §6)
  • As a candidate for office, submit a platform article to the Magazine under the staff's criteria (By-Law 9.5.2)
What the platform does for them
  • A frozen electorate at the record date (R12)
  • An inclusion receipt shown once
  • Weights fixed when voting opens (S4)
  • Certification as the Legal Advisor described it in May 2026: each community certifies its paid members, who vote on delegates; a member present votes in person with the vote deducted from a delegate's total (in practice, research 2 Oct 2026) (Q-37)
  • Presence at the desk: only a present delegate carries a weight and only a present certified member may extract; the weight is certified members over present delegates, computed, never typed (P22 §6's reading of 9.1.3's 'delegates sent'; Q-37; D5)
  • The delegation as a dated act of the club with its method stated (officers' selection, members' vote, or the club's own by-laws), the weights computed the same way whatever the method (P22 §6; Q-37)
  • The Convention city: one qualified bid by voice or hands (9.1.2); two by a weighted delegate vote (9.1.1(2)) (P22 §3, §6)
  • A candidate's service check lists platform seats and attested historical rows separately (P25 §3; Q-252)

Rules that protect them

  • The voter-to-token link is never stored
  • A lost receipt cannot be reissued, and the screen says so first
  • Dues count for voting only when paid from the member's own funds
  • A Mid-Year registrant who is not a member is asked for dues; the gate did not fire for one registrant in 2026 (Q-225)
