Delegate and voter

A member voting in federation elections, and a delegate carrying a club's weighted vote at convention.

Lens
Member
What they need
  • Know whether they can vote and why not
  • Cast a secret ballot and verify it
  • Vote through a delegate or extract to vote directly
What the platform does for them
  • A frozen electorate at the record date (R12)
  • An inclusion receipt shown once
  • Weights fixed when voting opens (S4)

Rules that protect them

  • The voter-to-token link is never stored
  • A lost receipt cannot be reissued, and the screen says so first
  • Dues count for voting only when paid from the member's own funds
