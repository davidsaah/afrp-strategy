Member lens

Donor

A member or friend of AFRP giving to a cause or fund, including restricted appeals and the endowment.

Lens
Member
What they need
  • Know where the money goes before paying
  • A correct receipt from the right entity
  • Hear what the gift did
  • Make a memorial gift to the Ramallah Foundation's senior home through the Federation and know which entity receipts it (Q-204)
  • Give to the Relief Fund on the public giving page (Q-208)
  • Establish a named scholarship and see, by count, which award carries the name (Q-102)
  • Give an endowed gift for Women to Women and know how it is held and spent (Q-210)
What the platform does for them
  • Named funds by entity
  • Ticket and gift receipted separately (D9; designed, not built)
  • Four contributor moments then an annual statement (D10; designed, not built)
  • A gift for an outside body passes through and its transfer shows on the purpose's page; ARFECF receives and releases senior-home gifts today (in practice, research 2 Oct 2026) (Q-204)
  • An appeal's purpose wording kept as published, so a later use can be read against it (D8; Q-218)
  • A named scholarship is a sub-fund inside the Scholarship Fund; a name with no instrument is a candidate row that takes no gift and is offered to no scholar (P26 §5; Q-102)
  • A gift for the senior home states, before payment, the receiving entity, the pass-through and the transfer schedule, and does not publish while its receiving entity is unset (P23 §9.2; Q-204)
  • An honoree and a recognition request travel with the transfer; the page promises no recognition on the Foundation's behalf (P23 §9.3; Q-248)
  • A gift by a non-member keeps its ledger line and receipt after the giver's role closes (P21 §9)

Rules that protect them

  • Money on Member 360 shows only with giving:read at the exact scope
  • Restriction comes only from donor designation (S6)
  • A public claim that all donations go to programmes waits on the CPA and the Legal Advisor (Q-215)
  • A relief gift posts to AFRP, which holds the Relief Fund (D83); its receipt claims no deductibility until the CPA gives the text (D9; P11 §4)
  • No pledge, campaign total or naming opportunity exists for Senior Living (P23 §9.7; Q-203)
