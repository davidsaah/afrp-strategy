Deep dives · each surface treated whole, every moment paired
Six programs, rung by rung v1.1new · the engagement ladder Camp Ramallah, Leadership Ramallah, the Scholarship, the Medical Mission, RBPN and the Convention — each walked across the same five rungs of engagement, the person running it beside the person living it. Thirty paired moments, red-teamed and reconciled. The Convention, in full v1.0new · four dimensions The hardest thing the federation does, taken apart: the year-long timeline from bid to handoff, role by role (chair, treasurer, credentials, delegate, attendee, youth, vendor), the four days hour by hour including the moment the hotel wifi dies — and the money, where three different legal treatments of a dollar meet in one ballroom. The programs, one at a time v1.0new Nineteen programs, six sections, one shared shape — charter, committee, budget, calendar, enrollment, metrics. A representative program walked end to end in each section (Camp, Scholarship, Medical Mission, the magazine and archive, Leadership), with the rest tabulated against the same template and each program’s extra gate named. A club’s year v1.0new The surface members actually experience: monthly meetings, the hafli season, youth activities, holidays, fundraisers, the convention delegation — and condolences, handled with the care they require: the platform never announces a death, and a condolence gathering publishes no attendee list. Running a club v1.0new The officer year, the treasurer’s month, the membership secretary’s queue, migration onto the platform — and the states nobody designs for: a club with no officers, a club going dormant, a handover where the outgoing officer will not sign. Running the federation v1.0new Five queues with named owners and service clocks, the board year between conventions, four institutions administered without being merged, and the discipline underneath it all: federation staff configure but never edit — corrections happen at source.
