What we are trying to do

The Federation's Strategic Planning Committee has worked on the Federation's direction since 2018: a five-year plan in 2019, the "Federation 2.0" recommendations in 2021, a survey of every committee in 2025, and a fresh start in September 2026. Those plans kept asking for the same foundations: one member database, a stronger link between the clubs and the Federation, the next generation brought in and kept, and good governance that members can see.

AFRP-Hub is the member platform being built to provide those foundations. At its centre is the CRM: one record for every member, their household, their clubs, their standing and every payment. Everything else in the strategy, from the programmes to the Convention to the endowment, builds on it.

This site puts the strategy and the platform on the same pages, so that the committee, the Board and the people building the Hub are working from one picture.

Four branches, one meeting place

Everything here is organised the same way. The Federation's work grows on four branches, Education, Leadership, Heritage and Care. The family tree sits on Heritage, and every member has a place on it; it is drawn in the plate style of the printed book (D68). The Convention is where the branches meet each year.
