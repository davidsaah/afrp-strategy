Care · programme

Women to Women

Direct financial help to women in Ramallah and their families for basic needs and self-improvement. Projects are proposed in writing from Ramallah and approved by a committee, which runs its own luncheon and co-funds Care projects.

Who it's for

Members and non-members as donors and representatives; women in Ramallah as beneficiaries. Since 2026 also young women applying for tuition support, whom the committee evaluates (Q-211).

The operating year

Projects year-round; fundraising at the Mid-Year and Annual Conventions and at its own luncheon. Founded in 2020 as an ARFHSN initiative. A September meeting finalises donations, proposal reviews and student scholarship evaluations (2026). From the fall 2026 term it pays two members' Arabic-class seats each term.

The five rungs

Not stated in the record for this programme. The five rungs are hear, show up, take part, give or serve, and lead (D25).

Who runs it

The Women to Women Committee approves projects proposed in writing with a need justification and budget. It attends ARFHSN's meetings as a committee. What it escalates is not stated. A contact in Ramallah receives proposals and disburses against receipts; the committee chair reports at ARFHSN's meetings. In practice the committee approves projects, as the public page says, and the ARFHSN minutes show it reporting, not the Board voting (Q-114).

Money

A sub-fund of ARFHSN (D55), receipted by ARFHSN (D56). Gifts are restricted to its purposes; representatives give a minimum yearly gift published on afrp.org. Money leaves under D63. It has also given to the Federation's Relief Fund (ARFHSN minutes, 2020). In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): money leaves on the committee's approval: gifts enter ARFHSN's US account, are accounted for and transferred to an account in Ramallah, from which the committee's contact disburses against receipts (afrp.org); see Q-114 and Q-104. Funding comes from direct gifts, a book sale, the Convention women's luncheon and a Convention fundraiser; projects fall in a published band (Q-213). ARFECF's books carried Women to Women gifts and a project payment for one year (Q-212). It pays members' Arabic-class seats, an Education fee (Q-168). An endowed fund was raised with the Endowment Committee in September 2026; the CPA's view is that such money stays in ARFHSN's investment accounts as a separately accounted fund, and the AFRP President asked that no new endowed fund be created until a single fundraising strategy exists (Q-210).

Hand-offs

Destination on the Care branch, entered by choice. Sister programme to the Medical Mission; fundraises at the Convention. It gave to the Relief Fund (2020), co-funded an elevator with ARFHSN (2020), pays Arabic-class seats (2026) and awards tuition beside the Scholarship (Q-211).
