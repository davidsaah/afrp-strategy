Women to Women

Direct financial help to women in Ramallah and their families for basic needs and self-improvement. Projects are proposed in writing from Ramallah and approved by a committee, which runs its own luncheon and co-funds Care projects.

Who it's for

Members and non-members as donors and representatives; women in Ramallah as beneficiaries.

The operating year

Projects year-round; fundraising at the Mid-Year and Annual Conventions and at its own luncheon.

The five rungs

Not stated in the record for this programme. The five rungs are hear, show up, take part, give or serve, and lead (D25).

Who runs it

The Women to Women Committee approves projects proposed in writing with a need justification and budget. It attends ARFHSN's meetings as a committee. What it escalates is not stated.

Money

A sub-fund of ARFHSN (D55), receipted by ARFHSN (D56). Gifts are restricted to its purposes; representatives give a minimum yearly gift published on afrp.org. Money leaves under D63. It has also given to the Federation's Relief Fund (ARFHSN minutes, 2020).

Hand-offs

Destination on the Care branch, entered by choice. Sister programme to the Medical Mission; fundraises at the Convention.
