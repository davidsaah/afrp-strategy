Education · programme

Project Hope

A two-week, hands-on programme in Palestine, run since 1997, that connects young adults to their ancestral homeland through volunteer service and meetings with local organisations, officials and youth. Paused since 2023; the committee plans a revival for its 30th anniversary in 2027.

Who it's for

Young adults aged 18 to 29, Arab and non-Arab, actively engaged in their community, able to commit to the whole trip, with a passport valid six months beyond it. About 20 delegates a year when it ran.

The operating year

Paused. When it ran (2019 procedure): recruitment from October to November, deadline in mid-May, trip in July or August. Revival plan: a new concept paper in October 2026, an alumni call in December 2026, trips resuming in 2027 or 2028, with activities in Jordan as a fallback if Palestine is unsafe.

The five rungs

Not stated in the record for this programme. The five rungs are hear, show up, take part, give or serve, and lead (D25).

Who runs it

A Project Hope committee and a trip leader with a three-year term; applicants are chosen by a selection subcommittee of at least two committee members, the programme coordinator and one alumnus (2019 procedure, a draft). What it escalates is not stated.

Money

ARFECF's Educational Fund (D55): a Project Hope line and Project Hope donations in the approved budget; ARFECF By-Laws 6.3.1 name it among the Endowment's purposes. Delegates pay their own airfare, booked through Federation staff; in-country costs come from the programme budget.

Hand-offs

Fed by Camp Ramallah and scholarship recipients. Feeds Leadership Ramallah at 21 and the Day of Action. Its alumni are the revival's first recruits.
