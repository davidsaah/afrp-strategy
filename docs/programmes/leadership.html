Leadership

A ladder by capacity

Service, advocacy and professional life. People enter at whichever of the branch's programmes they first take part in (P25 §2.2, a design choice; the "Emerging Leaders" entry was retired by D90, having no source) and move by what they are ready to take on, not by age. The order is a suggestion, never a prerequisite.

Programmes on this branch

Ramallah Business & Professional Network

A network for members in business and the professions: mixers, a LinkedIn group, a speaker series, local gatherings and, in earlier years, an annual conference. It holds one voting seat on the AFRP Board.

Built Flagship other
AFRPWorks (the Ramallah Jobs Initiative)

Connects Federation member employers with skilled professionals in Ramallah for gig, part-time and full-time work, so that income reaches Ramallah households. A prototype exists; a pilot sourced through a partner in Ramallah is planned.

Plan only other
Congressional Outreach

Year-round advocacy for Palestinian rights at every level of government and across party lines: members meet their own elected officials, and the committee briefs State Department and DHS officials. An issue becomes a statement, a member alert and meetings that are logged.

Plan only Committee
Day of Action

A yearly advocacy summit on Capitol Hill: young Palestinian-American adults meet their representatives and senators after training and advocacy-tools sessions with partner organisations. Its history runs back to the Federation's Youth Summit of 2015; in 2026 it was an open registration rather than an application.

Plan only other
Educational & Cultural Exchange Mission

Missions to Ramallah since 2014 for American journalists, educators, clergy and political leaders, graduate-student treks, and since 2024 a Palestine Fellowship; in 2026 it is moving to 'reverse missions' and a symposium at the Convention.

How people move along it

A ladder by capacity. The branch is entered at whichever of its programmes a person first takes part in (P25 §2.2, a design choice); the 'Emerging Leaders' entry, which had no source, was retired by D90. People move by what they are ready to take on, and the order is a suggestion, never a prerequisite. Inside every programme, people move through the same five rungs: hear · show up · take part · give or serve · lead (D25).
