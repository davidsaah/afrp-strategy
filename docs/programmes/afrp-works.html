Leadership · programme

AFRPWorks (the Ramallah Jobs Initiative)

Connects Federation member employers with skilled professionals in Ramallah for gig, part-time and full-time work, so that income reaches Ramallah households. A prototype exists; a pilot sourced through a partner in Ramallah is planned.

Owning entity
AFRP (Decision 64). Its owning committee and fee model are the working group's to name.

In service today
A prototype on Airtable built by the working group: an employer submits a form, is approved, then signs in to browse candidate profiles; candidates create profiles with skills, experience and availability. Announced to members in the President's mailing of 21 September 2026; no afrp.org page.

Who it's for

Member employers in the United States, and professionals in Ramallah in fields such as legal assistance, accounting, information technology, design and medical records.

The operating year

A soft launch is planned for autumn 2026; the pilot runs for one to two months before widening.

Who runs it

A working group of the Federation run from the Executive Committee: the President leads it; the group is the President, the Deputy President, the Legal Advisor, the programme director and two member business owners; the Executive Committee carries its launch on its agenda (August–September 2026); the Board heard it is intended to become part of RBPN. No committee under By-Law 8.15.1 is evidenced, and the owning committee is not yet named (Decision 64; Q-147).

What it needs

From the Federation's strategy
  • Named as a priority at the Strategic Planning Committee's September 2026 meeting as "Ramallah Works" (crosswalk A8)
From the platform
  • A programme page only until the working group names an owner and a fee model; RBPN's job board (slice 10) is the nearest built surface
  • Employer profiles are private by the initiative's own rule; candidate profiles are described as verified. In practice approved employers browse and the group also matches: a gate followed by browsing, not a contradiction
  • Whether the prototype's data comes onto the platform or stays outside it, so that it is not a second system of record (D66; Q-151)

Open questions

  • Who owns it, and whether it is a portal or a managed service
  • The fee or sponsor, and whether placements are recorded on AFRP's books
  • Is it a committee created under By-Law 8.15.1, an Executive Committee project, or a programme of the RBPN Committee; who approves an employer today, and is that act recorded? (Q-147)
  • The prototype already holds candidate profiles of people in Ramallah: what consent and retention apply before the platform exists? (Q-151)
  • Is a youth innovation hub in Ramallah's municipality (March 2025) a second candidate for the local partner? (Q-153)
