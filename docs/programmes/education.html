Branch

Education

A ladder by age

From a teenager's first week at camp to a young adult's first leadership course. A person moves up by age, and a milestone opens an invitation to the next programme. The Arabic Program is the exception: it is open at any age.

Programmes on this branch

Camp Ramallah

A residential summer camp bringing young members from across the US together for Ramallah heritage, Palestinian history and culture, outdoors. For most people it is the first rung of the whole federation.

Built Flagship Cohort
Arabic Program

Weekly online Arabic classes for adults, taught by a partner teaching college: a Conversational track in the Palestinian dialect and a Traditional reading, writing and speaking track. It runs today outside the Hub, through My AFRP and the CRM.

Plan only Cohort
Outstanding High School Senior Award

An award recognising community service and leadership in graduating high-school seniors. The register retired it in August 2026 by Board resolution, though afrp.org still offered it when the Hub's plans were written. The last files are from 2024.

Retired Award
Scholarship

Financial support for college students of Ramallah descent on two tracks, in the US and in Ramallah, each with a fixed annual amount: up to four years of undergraduate support, extended through graduation for medical students, with named scholarships alongside the general fund.

Built Flagship Award
Project Hope

A two-week, hands-on programme in Palestine, run since 1997, that connects young adults to their ancestral homeland through volunteer service and meetings with local organisations, officials and youth. Paused since 2023; the committee plans a revival for its 30th anniversary in 2027.

Plan only Mission
Leadership Ramallah

A two-day summit for young Palestinian-American adults, held alongside the Mid-Year Meeting, with expert-led workshops on leadership, advocacy and cultural preservation, a case-study lab, a pitch competition and a career panel.

Plan only Flagship Cohort

How people move along it

A ladder by age. A milestone on one programme opens an invitation to the next, by age. The order follows a young person's life: camp, then Project Hope, the Scholarship and Leadership Ramallah. Inside every programme, people move through the same five rungs: hear · show up · take part · give or serve · lead (D25).
