Education · programme

Scholarship

Financial support for college students of Ramallah descent on two tracks, in the US and in Ramallah, each with a fixed annual amount: up to four years of undergraduate support, extended through graduation for medical students, with named scholarships alongside the general fund.

Who it's for

Students with direct lineage to the original Ramallah families, or sponsorship and approval; full-time at an accredited college; documented financial need; minimum 3.0 GPA. Two tracks, US and Ramallah. In practice one sibling per family is accepted, as a note in the selection workbook, not a written rule.

The operating year

1 December: applications open. 31 May: deadline. Summer: review and vote. After the Convention: decisions (letters went out in early September in 2026). Each year: continuing recipients are carried as a cohort, with renewal reports (design/AFRP-Scholarship-Program-Spec.md) and the follow-up on academic standing the 2015 policy requires.

Who runs it

The Scholarship Fund Committee under ARFECF, seated as the 2015 policy reads: a chairman chosen by the ARFECF President, the executive director, members chosen by the ARFECF Board and three members elected at the Annual Convention (D58). Ramallah Foundation seats exist only once the ARFECF Board amends the 2015 policy (D58). The committee approves and distributes the annual expenditure (2015 policy; By-Law 6.4.4); no designated officer may stand in for it.

Money

ARFECF's Scholarship Fund, on ARFECF's books with ARFECF's receipts. The by-laws limit spending to 5%; the 2015 Scholarship Fund Policy Statement makes 4% to scholarships plus 1% to AFRP mandatory, which together equal that limit. Who may waive or carry over is open (Q-35).

Hand-offs

Fed by lineage on the family tree and formerly by the HS Senior Award. Recipients get a free student membership and feed Project Hope, Leadership Ramallah, RBPN and the alumni programme.
