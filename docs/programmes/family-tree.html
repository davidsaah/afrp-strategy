Ramallah Family Tree

The genealogy of the original Ramallah clans and their descendants: about 28,000 individuals, with more to add among those born since 1990 (Family Tree committee, 2026). It sits on the Heritage branch (D53). Every member still has a node on it, and a node is never a lineage claim (D30). A print edition is planned.

In service today
The family-tree application and its GEDCOM database, with a Google Form for change requests feeding a sheet.

Who it's for

Members; the whole tree is visible to signed-in members, while the living show no dates when signed out. Anyone may propose a change; the committee decides.

The operating year

Continuous: proposals, life events and imports queue to the committee. Corrections come in by a form into a sheet; accuracy checks at events in November and March. A short first print run is planned, with a family-update deadline before print (30 September 2027) and sample chapters at the Mid-Year in January 2027.

The five rungs

Not stated in the record for this programme. The five rungs are hear, show up, take part, give or serve, and lead (D25).

Who runs it

The Family Tree Committee decides every change; imports, exports and direct edits are audited. Undecided questions go to the Membership Committee. The committee lists two members (overview, 2026).

Money

Not stated: print costs have no approved budget (Family Tree committee, September 2026).

Hand-offs

On Heritage, with the Preservation Project, the magazine and the bookstore. Joining creates a node; life events flow to the household, the magazine and the heritage layer; the Scholarship reads lineage from it; Preservation items attach to its nodes. A GEDCOM link into the CRM is planned (September 2026).
