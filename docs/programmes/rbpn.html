Ramallah Business & Professional Network

A network for members in business and the professions: mixers, a LinkedIn group, a speaker series, local gatherings and, in earlier years, an annual conference. It holds one voting seat on the AFRP Board.

In service today
A LinkedIn group, check-in at the Convention mixer through My AFRP and the CRM, email lists, and Microsoft Teams events for the speaker series (September 2026), a door outside My AFRP and the CRM (Q-181). The Hub's job board is built but not in service.

Who it's for

AFRP members at any career stage, in fields from healthcare and STEM to law and the arts. One ambassador per club; clubs that host its events are reimbursed (business plan, 2019; D57). afrp.org (re-established July 2017) says events are for members only and gives no join step; no ambassador is named in 2026.

The operating year

A mixer during Convention week, included in base registration. A speaker series from September 2026, local happy hours in several cities by year end, and slots at the Mid-Year and the next Convention (RBPN committee, August 2026). An annual conference ran from 2018 to 2022. The first speaker event was held on 23 September 2026; a joint Mid-Year session with Leadership Ramallah and the Young Leaders Committee is planned for January 2027.

Who runs it

The RBPN Committee and its own board, with the Federation's programme director; one RBPN member holds a voting seat on the AFRP Board. What it escalates is not stated. The committee directory (2026–27) lists about nine members with no board titles; the Board's RBPN seat is filled.

Money

An AFRP committee whose first budget ARFECF's Board approved (business plan, 2019). Income has been registration fees and sponsorships; clubs that host RBPN events are reimbursed (D57). Dues are undecided. Paying for a listing on the platform is not decided, so the desk records payment and does not charge. No RBPN line was found in the 2026–27 budget pages read; whether the 2019 model of ARFECF Board approval has lapsed is open (Q-55).

Hand-offs

Fed by Leadership Ramallah, scholarship alumni and the Convention mixer. Feeds mentoring, Medical Mission recruiting and Board service. Its job board sits beside AFRPWorks on the Leadership branch (D64).
