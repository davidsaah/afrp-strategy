Educational & Cultural Exchange Mission

Missions to Ramallah since 2014 for American journalists, educators, clergy and political leaders, graduate-student treks, and since 2024 a Palestine Fellowship; in 2026 it is moving to 'reverse missions' and a symposium at the Convention.

Who it's for

Journalists, educators, clergy, political leaders and graduate cohorts. October delegates are chosen by a vetting subcommittee and a full committee vote, with the coordinator and chair breaking ties (2020 operating procedure); graduate-student treks choose their own people. The Palestine Fellowship is open to members of the AFRP community aged 21 and over.

The operating year

An October delegation each year under the 2020 procedure, with the number of delegations decided at least six months ahead (none evident since 2023). Graduate-student treks in spring. The Palestine Fellowship: terms in September, applications by 1 December, quarterly and final reports. 2026: a symposium slot at the Washington DC Convention.

The five rungs

Not stated in the record for this programme. The five rungs are hear, show up, take part, give or serve, and lead (D25).

Who runs it

The Exchange Mission committee, whose chair holds a rotating three-year term ratified by the Federation President; a four-member vetting subcommittee; the programme coordinator as single point of contact. The chair reports quarterly to the Federation (2020 procedure). What it escalates is not stated.

Money

ARFECF's Educational Fund (D55): a cultural exchange line and exchange fees in the approved budget. Past partner and sponsor funding under agreements; the Fellowship's 2026 grant came from a private fund through a partner foundation. The Federation pays in-country costs; trip leaders pay their own flights. Figures are not given here.

Hand-offs

Fed by scholarship graduates and young adults. Its alumni are the natural recruits for Congressional Outreach.
