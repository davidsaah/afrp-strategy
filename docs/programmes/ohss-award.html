Outstanding High School Senior Award

An award recognising community service and leadership in graduating high-school seniors. The register retired it in August 2026 by Board resolution, though afrp.org still offered it when the Hub's plans were written. The last files are from 2024.

None: retired. The last applications (2024) were mailed or emailed to the Federation office.

Who it's for

High-school seniors with a 3.5 GPA minimum, whose applicant and family were members in good standing.

The operating year

Applications due in May each year (15 May in 2019 and 2020, 31 May in 2023); awards announced at the Convention. Retired effective 31 August 2026.

The five rungs

Not stated in the record for this programme. The five rungs are hear, show up, take part, give or serve, and lead (D25).

Who runs it

The Scholarship Committee, on an award track (prototype). What it escalates is not stated.

Money

Small fixed prizes as gift cards, as published on afrp.org; historically paid from the Education Fund (the Education Fund's report, 2017).
