Heritage · programme

Hathihe Ramallah

The Federation's magazine since 1952, published bimonthly in English and Arabic, carrying family news, recognition lists and statutory notices. On the platform its printed sections are generated from live data at a copy freeze.

In service today
Subscription records in the Federation's CRM and QuickBooks; layout by a design agency; print and mail through a printer.

Who it's for

Subscribers and members; members appear through life events they record and consent to print.

The operating year

Bimonthly issues; an appeal notice each November; a subscription price rise planned for 2027. An issue cycle: window opens, copy freeze, proof, press, mail and archive.

The five rungs

Not stated in the record for this programme. The five rungs are hear, show up, take part, give or serve, and lead (D25).

Who runs it

The magazine's own board, with an executive editor, a subscription manager, a general manager, an Arabic editor and a design agency (magazine overview, 2026). The magazine's authority is its own and no federation grant confers it.

Money

Its assets are held by ARFECF (D55); magazine sales appear on the Educational Fund page of the approved budget (2026–27). Subscriptions, appeals and advertising. Magazine money is kept apart from Federation money on the ledger.

Hand-offs

Fed by life events, the family tree's accepted lines, recognition tiers and RBPN listings. Feeds the Preservation archive and is the statutory notice channel for the Convention.
