Heritage · programme

Hathihe Ramallah

The Federation's magazine since 1952, published bimonthly in English and Arabic, carrying family news, recognition lists and statutory notices. On the platform its printed sections are generated from live data at a copy freeze.

In service today
Subscription records in the Federation's CRM and in the subscription manager's own ledger, reconciled in 2026; QuickBooks; layout by a design agency; print and mail through a printer and a mailing house; the digital issue by email campaign; the magazine's own mailboxes; a web agency for the afrp.org page.

Who it's for

Subscribers and members; members appear through life events they record and consent to print. Subscribers include non-members and households; since the May/June 2025 issue print goes only to paid print subscribers, and a digital copy comes with a renewed membership.

The operating year

Six issues a year; the September/October issue is the Convention issue; in 2026 the digital issues went out by email campaign in June and August. An appeal when renewals lag: in 2026 it ran in August (email to younger contacts, a letter through the mailing house to older households) before the Convention issue. The subscription price rises in 2027; auto-renew needs a saved card. An issue cycle: window opens, copy freeze, proof, press, mail and archive.

The five rungs

Not stated in the record for this programme. The five rungs are hear, show up, take part, give or serve, and lead (D25).

Who runs it

The magazine's own board, with an executive editor, a subscription manager, a general manager, an Arabic editor and a design agency (magazine overview, 2026). The magazine's authority is its own and no federation grant confers it. In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the staff are an executive editor, an Arabic editor, a general manager, a subscriptions and circulation manager, an advertising role and a treasurer who files the chequebook report, with stipends per issue; announcements reach the editor through a city correspondent or the magazine's mailbox under published guidelines. The staff's criteria govern candidates' platform articles (By-Law 9.5.2); see Q-192. The 'Manager of the Magazine' of By-Law 9.5.2 is unmapped to any seat (P24 §4; Q-250).

Money

Its assets are held by ARFECF (D55); magazine sales appear on the Educational Fund page of the approved budget (2026–27). Subscriptions, appeals and advertising. Magazine money is kept apart from Federation money on the ledger. Income is subscriptions and paid obituary placements; expenses are per-issue stipends, design, the printer and the mailing house; an account opened in September 2023 with a seed deposit; a managed investment account. By-Law 6.7.1 says the Federation shall appropriate the funds that guarantee publication; the 2020 practice of keeping magazine money apart sits alongside that duty (Q-194). Whether an obituary fee is exchange revenue or a memorial gift is open (Q-193).

Hand-offs

Fed by life events, the family tree's accepted lines, recognition tiers and RBPN listings. Feeds the Preservation archive and is the statutory notice channel for the Convention.
