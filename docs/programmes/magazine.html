Heritage · programme

Hathihe Ramallah

The Federation's magazine since 1952, in English and Arabic, carrying family news, recognition lists and statutory notices. On the platform its printed sections are generated from live data at a copy freeze.

Heritage · A cluster, used for life
Owning entity
AFRP, with its own independent board (By-Law 6.7.1)

Who it's for

Subscribers and members; members appear through life events they record and consent to print.

The operating year

An issue cycle: window opens, copy freeze, proof, press, mail and archive. The number of issues a year is an open question.

The five rungs

Not stated in the record for this programme. The five rungs are hear, show up, take part, give or serve, and lead (D25).

Who runs it

The magazine's own board and editor. The magazine's authority is its own and no federation grant confers it. Who the editor is (staff, committee or contractor) is open.

Money

Appropriated by AFRP, plus subscriptions and advertising. Magazine money is kept apart from Federation money on the ledger.

Hand-offs

Fed by life events, the family tree's accepted lines, recognition tiers and RBPN listings. Feeds the Preservation archive and is the statutory notice channel for the Convention.
