Congressional Outreach

Year-round advocacy for Palestinian rights at every level of government and across party lines: members meet their own elected officials, and the committee briefs State Department and DHS officials. An issue becomes a statement, a member alert and meetings that are logged.

In service today
An Excel outreach tracker (last edited 2023), letters and statements in Word and PDF, and e-mail alerts to members.

Who it's for

AFRP members; the committee arranges meetings with a member's own elected officials and offers a committee member or expert to join.

The operating year

Reactive, as issues arise: a statement or letter signed by the President, a member alert with a one-pager, then calls and meetings logged in a tracker. The tracker was last edited in 2023. No written procedure.

The five rungs

Not stated in the record for this programme. The five rungs are hear, show up, take part, give or serve, and lead (D25).

Who runs it

The Government Affairs Committee. Advocacy positions are the Federation's, adopted through governance, not a committee's own. Who clears a position before the President signs is not written down.

Money

AFRP General Fund (D55, D65); the platform refuses to charge advocacy to ARFECF or ARFHSN (D65). It may also be the by-laws' 'Internships in Washington' Endowed Fund purpose; confirm.

Hand-offs

Fed by exchange-mission alumni. Feeds and is fed by the Day of Action. Advocacy is organised by district, so it relies on the consented member directory.

How it runs today

From the programme's own records, read in September and October 2026 (D54). Practice, not rules: a rule is a decision in the register.

Entity, evidence
The approved budget (2026–27) moved Government Affairs and congressional coordinator lines from the Educational Fund to the General Fund (D55, D65); By-Law 8.15.1 constitutes the Government Affairs Committee.

The clubs' part

  • A government-affairs representative in each club was proposed in 2020 and not adopted (Government Affairs committee minutes, 2020)
  • Clubs host meetings with representatives in their districts (outreach tracker, 2022–2023)
  • On the platform: no seat (the 2020 representative was not adopted); a district meeting is an event role; no line (D65); meetings hosted per club as the count (Club Experience Plan §7)
