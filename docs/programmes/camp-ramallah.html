Camp Ramallah

A residential summer camp bringing young members from across the US together for Ramallah heritage, Palestinian history and culture, outdoors. For most people it is the first rung of the whole federation.

Who it's for

Campers aged 13 to 17 from member households; about 50 seats. Counselors must be 19 or have finished a year of college, and must be Federation members in their own right.

The operating year

Aug-Oct: next summer's site booked. Jan-Mar: applications. Mar-Apr: offers, waitlist by club, counselor vetting. May: health forms and consents. June or July: camp week. Aug: cohort report.

The five rungs

RungFor this programme
HearThe household of an eligible-age child is told, through the household inbox; counts are banded per club, never a roster of children.
Show upOne summer. Attending once is a complete outcome and nothing chases the camper afterwards.
Take partA returning camper, observed from the attendance record and never solicited.
Give or serveA counselor, who must first join, consent and hold standing as themselves after turning 18.
LeadA seat on the camp committee, which owns the curriculum. People can arrive here without ever having been campers.

Who runs it

A programme coordinator with a camp committee appointed under By-Law 8.1.3 (President-appointed, one-year terms), whose members must meet 8.1.2. What it escalates is not stated.

Money

AFRP, fee-funded or general fund. The camp fee is not published; camperships are modelled in the build.

Hand-offs

Fed by household records (age). Feeds Project Hope at 18 and, via counselors, Leadership Ramallah at 21. The retired HS Senior Award was the next rung; the gap is now open.
