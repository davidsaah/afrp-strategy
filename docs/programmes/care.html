Programmes on this branch

Medical Mission

Volunteer physicians, specialists and health professionals from the US and Palestine building long-term health capacity in Palestine. Active: prosthetics (under an agreement with a university and a rehabilitation centre), neurology capacity, urology and ultrasound-biopsy training, occupational therapy training. Past: a cardiovascular centre, a women's health centre, telemedicine, an emergency-medicine residency, PPE and oxygen, emergency supplies for Gaza and the West Bank, a radiology image system and hospital beds.

Designed Flagship Mission
Women to Women

Direct financial help to women in Ramallah and their families for basic needs and self-improvement. Projects are proposed in writing from Ramallah and approved by a committee, which runs its own luncheon and co-funds Care projects.

Plan only Committee
Senior Living Project

A capital project for a new senior residence in Ramallah, replacing a 1970s facility: about 30,000 square feet for 50 or more residents. Ground was broken in October 2023 and the cornerstone laid in June 2024. Little else is in the Federation's records: a senior centre appears as a grant recipient and a memorial fund.

Designed Campaign
The Relief Fund

Monthly support to families in Ramallah since 2020 and emergency appeals, administered through the Federation and the affiliate. The Board votes the support; the Federation's representative in Ramallah distributes it to families on lists from local partners, and recipients are told the Federation is the source.
