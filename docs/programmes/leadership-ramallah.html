Leadership Ramallah

A two-day summit for young Palestinian-American adults, held alongside the Mid-Year Meeting, with expert-led workshops on leadership, advocacy and cultural preservation, a case-study lab, a pitch competition and a career panel.

Who it's for

Active AFRP members aged 21 and over (with occasional flexibility); about 15 places. Standing is By-Law 4.3.1 in full: national and club dues both current; accepted participants must be current by a stated deadline or lose the place (acceptance letters, 2024).

The operating year

Applications open in October; the agenda is finalised in November; the summit runs Friday and Saturday at the Mid-Year Meeting (March in 2026; the Mid-Year is moving to January). Reimbursements on receipts afterwards; a feedback survey before leaving.

Who runs it

The Leadership Ramallah Committee, with two co-chairs and the Federation's programme director, who review and choose applicants. The selection protocol is not on file. What it escalates is not stated.

Money

The Federation pays the hotel, half of travel up to a cap, a lunch and a reduced banquet; the participant's club is expected to cover about half of travel, and the Federation's travel share applies only where the club does not; participants pay registration, shirts and other meals (written cost rules, 2026; D57). The budget sits inside the youth line, to be split out. Figures are not given here.

Hand-offs

Fed by Camp counselors, Project Hope and scholarship recipients. Feeds the Day of Action, RBPN, and Board and committee seats. A mentorship scheme and an alumni path into RBPN are planned (August 2026).
