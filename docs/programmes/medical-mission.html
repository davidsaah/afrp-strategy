Care · programme

Medical Mission

Volunteer physicians, specialists and health professionals from the US and Palestine building long-term health capacity in Palestine. Active: prosthetics (under an agreement with a university and a rehabilitation centre), neurology capacity, urology and ultrasound-biopsy training, occupational therapy training. Past: a cardiovascular centre, a women's health centre, telemedicine, an emergency-medicine residency, PPE and oxygen, emergency supplies for Gaza and the West Bank, a radiology image system and hospital beds.

Who it's for

Health professionals as volunteers, and donors. Volunteers are invited by committee physicians and pay their own travel and lodging. No credentialing step for volunteers is written down anywhere.

The operating year

Missions about once a year (April 2025, September 2026) with an academic day; specialists are invited about seven weeks ahead. Partner agreements are checked at least 30 days before their annual expiry. ARFHSN's by-laws require a report to the AFRP Board every six months, which the platform produces (D63).

Who runs it

The Medical Mission Committee, a programme of ARFHSN (D62): ARFHSN's Board appoints its chair and approves its spending; the Federation President convenes it. Draft terms of reference give three-year committee terms from January 2024. ARFHSN's Board of Trustees is elected by the AFRP Board and reports to it every six months (ARFHSN By-Laws Art. III §7).

Money

ARFHSN's (D56): gifts are receipted by ARFHSN, and the Federation's giving pages collect them as ARFHSN's agent and forward them without delay. Money leaves under D63: a Board vote with a spending ceiling, payment on delivery with two signatories, and a grant closed only with the partner's signed agreement, a delivery confirmation and an equipment inventory on record.

Hand-offs

Fed by RBPN's professional directory and the Convention. Project Hope delegations visit mission sites; Women to Women is its sister programme. Clubs co-fund its grants (D57).
