The Relief Fund

Monthly support to families in Ramallah since 2020 and emergency appeals, administered through the Federation and the affiliate. The Board votes the support; the Federation's representative in Ramallah distributes it to families on lists from local partners, and recipients are told the Federation is the source.

Who it's for

Families in need in Ramallah, as recipients; members, clubs and committees as donors. A share of each Convention registration goes to the fund (host agreement).

The operating year

Monthly during an emergency, by Board vote, from 2020 (extended in 2021). Each Convention adds a per-registration amount. The approved budget's Ramallah Charities page funds local charities from the fund each year.

The five rungs

Not stated in the record for this programme. The five rungs are hear, show up, take part, give or serve, and lead (D25).

Who runs it

The Federation's Board votes the support and requires a distribution plan; the Federation's representative in Ramallah distributes and reports. Fundraising for it goes only through the Federation's procedure (Board minutes, 2020). How the affiliate's part is divided from the Federation's is not written down.

Money

AFRP: a separate Relief Fund account, with its balance in each Board packet; the Ramallah Charities page of the approved budget (2026–27) is funded from it. Gifts also come from Convention registrations and from committees. Which entity's books hold it is not named in D55. Figures are not given here.

Hand-offs

Destination on the Care branch, entered by choice. Fed by the Convention's per-registration amount and by appeals; beside ARFHSN's relief projects and the Medical Mission.
