Arabic Program

Weekly online Arabic classes for adults, taught by a partner teaching college: a Conversational track in the Palestinian dialect and a Traditional reading, writing and speaking track. It runs today outside the Hub, through My AFRP and the CRM.

Who it's for

Adult learners. Members pay the member rate; a person who is not a member may register at the public rate, with a contact record and not a membership (D60). On the Education branch it is the one rung open at any adult age. In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the public page still says registration is open to adult AFRP members only; see Q-171.

The operating year

Three terms a year: a fall term from September, a spring term from January, each of 12 weekly online sessions (Thanksgiving or Easter week off), and a short summer enrichment term. A priority window for past students and waitlisted learners opens each term before general registration (Arabic committee records, 2025–2026). Launched in September 2024 with five Traditional and four Conversational levels; a 2026 summer pilot ran on a learning platform (annual report, 2026; Q-170).

The five rungs

Not stated in the record for this programme. The five rungs are hear, show up, take part, give or serve, and lead (D25).

Who runs it

The Arabic Program Committee, a standing committee joined through the Committee Interest Form, with the Federation's programme director. The partner college supplies the teachers. What it escalates is not stated.

Money

ARFECF's Educational Fund (D55): class fees as income and the teaching partner's cost as expense in the approved budget. The partner invoices the Federation per enrolled seat, plus books. Where last year's surplus is held is an open action for the committee. Figures are not given here. In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): from the fall 2026 term Women to Women, an ARFHSN sub-fund, sponsors two member women's registrations each term, chosen on a short statement; no rule covers a Care sub-fund paying an Education fee, against D55's separation of the entities; see Q-168. Teacher incentives tied to retention, student evaluations and measurable success were on the committee's August 2026 agenda (Q-169).

Hand-offs

Open at any age, so no milestone invitation fires into it. The prototype links it to Camp, the Preservation Project and the magazine's Arabic pages. Club presidents recruit its volunteer conversation partners (August 2026). It recruits: the annual report (2026) says learners have joined the Federation through it, a door into membership the rungs do not yet credit.
