Branch

Heritage

A cluster, used for life

The Ramallah Family Tree, the Preservation Project, Hathihe Ramallah and the Bookstore. There is no "next" here and no milestone invitations: people come and go all their lives, and nobody graduates from the archive to the magazine. The tree also holds every member's node, so the other branches draw on it too (D30, D53).

Programmes on this branch

Ramallah Family Tree

The genealogy of the original Ramallah clans and their descendants: about 28,000 individuals, with more to add among those born since 1990 (Family Tree committee, 2026). It sits on the Heritage branch (D53). Every member still has a node on it, and a node is never a lineage claim (D30). A print edition is planned.

Built other
Ramallah Preservation Project

A digital archive of Ramallah's history, with a committee since 2017 (committee records; afrp.org gives 2020): photographs, documents, articles and oral histories with migrants and first-generation Americans, shown at Convention exhibits and, in plans, with a museum partner. A digitisation pilot with a scanning vendor is under way, funded by a foundation grant.

Plan only other
Hathihe Ramallah

The Federation's magazine since 1952, published bimonthly in English and Arabic, carrying family news, recognition lists and statutory notices. On the platform its printed sections are generated from live data at a copy freeze.

Built other
Bookstore

An online store for books, magazine back issues, clan books, merchandise and digital downloads, with member pricing for members in good standing (the design). In practice the Federation sells books through two online stores, one on afrp.org and one on the member portal, with seven and six titles, to US addresses only, with no member price, stock figures or digital goods; back issues, clan books, merchandise and downloads are not sold. The Federation's own overview sheet is an empty template, and no owner is recorded.

How people move along it

A cluster, used for life. There is no next step and no milestone invitation. People take part in any of these at any point in life. Inside every programme, people move through the same five rungs: hear · show up · take part · give or serve · lead (D25).
