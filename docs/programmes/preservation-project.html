Ramallah Preservation Project

A digital archive of Ramallah's history, with a committee since 2017 (committee records; afrp.org gives 2020): photographs, documents, articles and oral histories with migrants and first-generation Americans, shown at Convention exhibits and, in plans, with a museum partner. A digitisation pilot with a scanning vendor is under way, funded by a foundation grant.

Who it's for

Anyone with materials, stories or time; submissions and interviews are welcome.

The operating year

Exhibit preparation from March for the Convention in July, with on-site scanning and oral-history recording; website renewal by January; a foundation grant for digitisation runs October 2025 to September 2026, with its report due in November 2026. The committee meets monthly on Microsoft Teams. The foundation grant's period ended on 30 September 2026 with a balance unspent (Q-108); a second scanning scope was quoted in September 2026, priced per box; the committee sought youth volunteers in February 2026 to rebuild the website, and a Convention poster and exhibit ran in July 2026.

The five rungs

Not stated in the record for this programme. The five rungs are hear, show up, take part, give or serve, and lead (D25).

Who runs it

The Preservation Project Committee, which gathers documents, builds exhibits and runs the digitisation project with a part-time archivist under the grant. Who owns copyright and takedown decisions is open. The programme director convenes the monthly meeting and handles the vendor and the grant; the committee has its own mailbox and group address.

Money

The Federation's budget line, a foundation grant for digitisation (October 2025 to September 2026) and gifts; the scanning vendor's account is on the Education Fund's tax exemption. Whose books hold it is for the Boards to confirm (D55). Figures are not given here.

Hand-offs

On the Heritage cluster: no 'next' step. It wraps the family tree (items attach to tree nodes) and uses magazine back issues and Convention ad books as archive material.
