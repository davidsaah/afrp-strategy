Senior Living Project

A capital project for a new senior residence in Ramallah, replacing a 1970s facility: about 30,000 square feet for 50 or more residents. Ground was broken in October 2023 and the cornerstone laid in June 2024. Little else is in the Federation's records: a senior centre appears as a grant recipient and a memorial fund.
