Day of Action

A yearly advocacy summit on Capitol Hill: young Palestinian-American adults meet their representatives and senators after training and advocacy-tools sessions with partner organisations.

My AFRP and the CRM (an event form for applications and guest records with travel details), with Excel sheets for participants and reimbursements.

Who it's for

Adults. First-time attendees aged 21 to 35 who are members have registration, hotel and part of their travel covered; everyone else attends at their own expense (afrp.org). Applicants answer about 15 questions on their interest, involvement and experience with elected officials.

The operating year

Preparation in August; applications open 1 September; review and the first training in October; the second training in November; the event in mid-November in Washington DC (15–18 November in 2026); travel reimbursed afterwards. A further session with a partner in May 2026.

The five rungs

Not stated in the record for this programme. The five rungs are hear, show up, take part, give or serve, and lead (D25).

Who runs it

afrp.org names a Day of Action Committee; in the Federation's records the programme director and the President run it, with the Government Affairs committee. Who scores applications is not written down. What it escalates is not stated.

Money

AFRP General Fund (D55, D65). The Federation books a hotel block and reimburses participants' travel after the event. Figures are not given here.

Hand-offs

Fed by Leadership Ramallah and Project Hope alumni and by Congressional Outreach. Its participants facilitate at Leadership Ramallah.
