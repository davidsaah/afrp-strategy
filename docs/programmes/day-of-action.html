Day of Action

A yearly advocacy summit on Capitol Hill: members meet their representatives and senators and attend advocacy-tools sessions with partner organisations.

Who it's for

Adults. First-time attendees aged 21 to 35 who are members have registration, hotel and part of their travel covered; everyone else attends at their own expense.

The operating year

Annual, in Washington DC in November (afrp.org listed 15-18 November 2026).

The five rungs

Not stated in the record for this programme. The five rungs are hear, show up, take part, give or serve, and lead (D25).

Who runs it

The Day of Action Committee, a standing committee. What it escalates is not stated.

Money

AFRP, fee-funded or general fund. Summit budget figures are not stated.

Hand-offs
