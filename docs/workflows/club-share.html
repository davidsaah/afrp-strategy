Club share and settlement

Every amount a club pays or receives through a Federation or affiliate programme sits on one statement per club: dues remitted, camp rebates, Leadership Ramallah travel shares, event reimbursements, convention and Mid-Year settlements, grants to clubs and co-funding of Care grants. Each programme sets its own terms (D57).

Built in the Hub
Designed Adopted as one shared service on the funds ledger (D57) and designed in the Club Experience Plan §5: one account per club per holding entity, sixteen line types each tied to a programme term in the register, a run with a query hold and an immutable close. The remittance statement it extends is built (slice D1); the rest is not.

Steps

  1. Set the programme's club terms: what a club pays or receives, and when (D57)Programme committee
  2. Record a club amount when it arises: dues remitted (D1), a camp rebate, a Leadership Ramallah travel share, an event reimbursement, a convention or Mid-Year settlement (D59), a grant to a club, a share of a Care grant (D63)Programme coordinator; club treasurer
  3. Carry a club attribution on any gift where the donor or the appeal names one; a co-funded grant lists each club's share (D57)Platform
  4. Produce one statement per club, every amount by programme and holding entity, with carry-forward (D55, D57)Platform
  5. Approve the statement runFederation treasurer
  6. Review the statement and confirm it or raise a queryClub treasurer
  7. Settle the balance each way per entity, never netting across entities (a design choice, Club Experience Plan §5.1); a query holds its line with an owner and a clock while the rest settles; a balance under the floor carries forwardFederation treasurer; club treasurer
  8. Close the run; a correction is a new line on the next run naming the one it correctsPlatform

Rule against practice

  • The 2024 by-laws require club money for AFRP projects to pass through headquarters; Mid-Year sponsorship cheques are payable to the host club (the Federation's Mid-Year records, 2026).
  • No written rule governs dues remittance to clubs in the Federation's records since 2009; D1 is the rule and D57 shows it on the statement (Q-36).
  • The convention share: the 2009 contract, a 2021 resolution and a 2024 General Assembly vote differ (Q-34; D59).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the Young Leaders Committee ran relief drives as a competition among clubs with a per-club tally (2025, 2026); which entity receipted them and whether the tally is a club attribution is Q-180.
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): a club's co-funding of an ARFHSN subsidy is confirmed by the club's trustee in the ARFHSN meeting; a club's closed branch balance is held by ARFHSN as an earmark (Q-216).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the Ramallah Foundation offered clubs signage at its senior home for a club gift (Q-205); clubs also give directly to Ramallah projects (Q-228).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the 2026 Convention close netted a sum the host owed the Federation and charged a Federation-hosted dinner to the host's side, neither on a schedule row (Q-221).

Hands off to

  • club share → ledger: the dues remittance stays D1; the statement is where it shows (D57)
  • club share → giving: club-attributed gifts are counted on the club's statement (D57)
  • club share → care grants: a club's co-funding of a Care grant is listed as its share (D57, D63)
