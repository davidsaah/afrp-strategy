Convention close and host payment

After the Convention the Federation and the host club settle on one reconciliation: the office prepares it, a reconciliation meeting corrects it, the approvers record dated acts (who they must be is Q-221), the General Treasurer pays the host (7.5.1; Q-241) and the accounting goes to the Board. Written from the 2026 close; that year's signed agreement remains the rule (D59).

Steps

  1. Carry the host's balance as a liability due to the host from registration to payment; the Federation's fixed share books as General Fund income from the host — in practice (research, 2 Oct 2026)Executive Administrator (bookkeeper)
  2. Prepare the reconciliation from the registration system's transaction report and the host's records, with netted items each way — in practice (Q-221)Executive Administrator
  3. Convene the reconciliation meeting with the host's leads and enter the corrections agreed on it (a sum the host owes netted against the release; a Federation-hosted event inside the Convention charged to the host's side) — in practice; neither had a schedule row, and a netted item with no schedule row is held while the rest settles (P22 §4, §9; Q-124)Deputy President; host club leads
  4. Read deficit responsibility and every other term from that year's signed host agreement; a term the agreement does not carry is shown as not stated (D59; Q-129)Federation treasurer
  5. Exclude dues collected with registration from the gross; they are remitted, never proceeds (By-Law 5.2; P13 §4)Platform
  6. Approve the settlement by a dated reply — in practice; who approves the statement for the Federation and whether the Finance Committee's audit step still runs are open (Q-221); whether a reply stands as the countersignature is Q-241 (P22 §9.4)President and Deputy President
  7. Post the per-registration Relief Fund amount from the settlement as one dated line — in practice for 2022–2025; whether it continues after 2025 is Q-126 (P22 §5.3; P23 §3; Q-243)Executive Administrator; Educational Fund Treasurer
  8. Pay the host by the channel the bank allows for the amount and record the channel against the liability — in practice a cheque (Q-222); the payment's signatories are By-Law 11.1.2 (the Treasurer, countersigned by a senior Executive Committee officer) and 7.5.1–7.5.2 (the General Treasurer and the Financial Secretary co-sign cheques), which differ; whether an approval by reply stands as the countersignature is Q-241AFRP General Treasurer (P22 §9.5; By-Law 7.5.1); in practice the office issued the cheque (2026)
  9. Release within the agreed settlement period while the host's final accounting runs on its own clock (By-Law 10.1.7; Q-128)Federation treasurer; host club treasurer
  10. Show the settlement on the host club's statement (D57)Platform
  11. Present the Convention's accounting to the Board at its next meeting — in practiceFederation office; AFRP Board

Trigger, frequency, records, money

Trigger
The Convention ends
Frequency
Yearly; in 2026 about six weeks after the Convention
Records
The reconciliation with its netted items, the meeting's corrections, the dated approvals, the payment and its channel, the Relief Fund line, the statement on the host's club account
Money
The balance due to the host; the Federation's fixed share; amounts netted each way; the per-registration Relief Fund amount

Rule against practice

  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the only earlier settlement on file (2012) ran the other way: the host collected registration and paid the Federation its fee with the dues and Relief Fund amounts, less two costs the Federation bore.
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the design's ten-line statement (P13 §4) has no line for a Federation-hosted event charged to the host or a host debt netted against the release; 2026 did both (Q-124, Q-221).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): part of the host fee appears in ARFECF's books as due to AFRP, though the Convention is AFRP's (D1 G; D55's test, the register entry; Q-224).

Hands off to

  • close → club share: the settlement lands on the host club's statement (D57, D59)
  • close → relief: the per-registration amount posts to the Relief Fund as one line (Q-126)
  • close → ledger: the liability due to the host clears on payment; the fixed share is General Fund income
  • close → agreements: the statement reads only the signed agreement's terms (D59; Q-93)
  • close → reporting: the accounting goes to the Board's next meeting
