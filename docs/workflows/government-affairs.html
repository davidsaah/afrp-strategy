Government Affairs: meetings, town halls and local action

The Government Affairs Committee's year-round work as it ran in 2025–26: meetings with offices logged on the committee's own automated tracker, town halls with an action segment, local action committees inside clubs, and paid coordinators on AFRP's General Fund (D65).

Trigger, frequency, records, money

Trigger
A bill or issue the committee takes up; a town hall; a member's or club's meeting with an office
Frequency
Year-round; town halls every few months
Records
Meetings by office on the tracker, town-hall agendas and attendance, constituent stories (proposed)
Money
Coordinators' contract labour and town-hall costs, AFRP General Fund only (D65); the internship contribution's fund undecided (Q-182)

Rule against practice

  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the record carries the 2020 proposal of a representative per club as not adopted; local action committees were launched inside clubs in 2025–26 (Q-131).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the record's tracker was last edited in 2023; the committee replaced it with an automated one (Q-183).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the record refuses a candidate subject; whether any candidate-related activity is permitted is Q-132.
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the March 2025 Mid-Year voted a contribution to Washington internships with no fund named; D65 puts Government Affairs on the General Fund and ARFECF By-Law 6.3.1 names Internships in Washington as an Endowment purpose (Q-182).

Hands off to

  • government affairs → advocacy: positions, statements and alerts run in the advocacy workflow (P14)
  • government affairs → events: a town hall or a district meeting at a club is an event with its host
  • government affairs → clubs: a club's local action committee is not yet a seat on the officers form (Q-131)
  • government affairs → reporting: meetings and town halls report to the Board, and cost rows to the lobbying record (Q-134)
