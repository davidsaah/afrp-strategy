Workflow

Scholarship award letters, renewal and instalment release

After selection the office checks documents, writes the letters and the first cheque; each later instalment is released only when the scholar's semester documents are verified. The rule is written in the award letter, not in an adopted policy (Q-159).

Built in the Hub
Designed The committee engine (slices 4, 4b) verifies enrolment before disbursement; the letters, the document chase, the instalment release and named-scholarship assignment are designed in P26 (`AFRP-Scholarship-Awards.md`, SA1–SA7); not built. Written from the committee's 2026 report and the 2026 award letter.

Steps

  1. Verify each selected applicant's documents (acceptance letter, transcript, recommendation, statement, and the Ramallah track's school-leaving scores) and list the gaps — in practice (research, 2 Oct 2026)Programme director
  2. Ask the committee secretary whether to chase a gap or hold the cheque — in practiceExecutive Administrator; Scholarship Committee secretary
  3. Send award, missing-documents and rejection letters in early September, in the Federation's name over the President's and the committee secretary's signatures — in practice (Q-158). On the platform each letter is an LT-P row in ARFECF's name; with no signatory row it is a draft the office sees and is not sent (P26 §4)Federation office
  4. Write the first cheque with the award letter; a missing-documents letter holds it until the gaps close — in practiceExecutive Administrator
  5. File the semester transcript, proof of full-time enrolment with the next semester's schedule, and the cumulative GPA after the fall and the winter semester (the 2026 award letter)Scholar (continuing recipient)
  6. Verify and release the instalment; a late filing, dropping below full time or below the minimum GPA may suspend or cancel the next payment, and a scholar in hardship contacts the office (the 2026 award letter; Q-159). The platform itself suspends nothing; see the instalment states below (P26 §2.4)Federation office
  7. Draw the award on ARFECF's Scholarship Fund under the committee's authority (D3, D55); which account pays in practice is not shown (Q-158)Executive Administrator, for the fund
  8. Hold the instalment's state (scheduled, due, verified, released, held, suspended, cancelled) with its verification rows; a missed filing holds and never lapses, and only the committee's act suspends or cancels (P26 §2.1–§2.4; Q-256, Q-257)Federation office; Scholarship Committee
  9. Ask for hardship by a category the committee defines, with no free text; the request opens a follow-up to the office and a decision item for the committee (P26 §2.4; Q-159)Scholar; Federation office; Scholarship Committee
  10. Announce counts and amounts by track, never names — in practiceProgramme director
  11. Assign a named scholarship to a recipient under its donor's criteria; no instrument or criteria are on file for any of them (Q-102)Scholarship Committee

Trigger, frequency, records, money

Trigger
The committee's recommendations are reported; each semester ends
Frequency
Letters yearly in September; two instalments a year per scholar
Records
The selected list, document gaps, letters sent, instalments with the verification behind each, suspensions, named-scholarship assignments
Money
Annual awards from ARFECF's Scholarship Fund in two instalments, by cheque by post on the US track; the Ramallah track's channel is not stated

Rule against practice

  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the programme register reads a missed renewal after a bereavement as not lapsed by silence; the 2026 letter says failure to file may suspend the award and asks the scholar to contact the office. Neither is a written policy (Q-159).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the programme register has the ARFECF Treasurer pay approved awards; the Executive Administrator writes the cheques and the letters are in AFRP's name (Q-158).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): ARFECF By-Law 6.4.4 gives the fund committee alone the spending authority; the 2023 community report says the AFRP Board approved the year's scholarships (Q-161).

Hands off to

  • scholarship cycle → renewal: the selected list and its gaps arrive from the committee secretary
  • renewal → giving: a named scholarship's donor sees a count, never the scholar (Q-102)
  • renewal → comms: letters and reminders go to the scholar on a verified channel
  • renewal → alumni: a scholar who completes becomes an alumnus (UC-7)
