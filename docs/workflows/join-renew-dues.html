Workflow

Join, renew and pay dues

A person joins through one front door, then renews national and club memberships that lapse independently. Standing is always scoped: national or a named club, never "in good standing" alone.

Built in the Hub
Partly built Join wizard, sign-in, recovery, renewal and scoped standing are built. The card processor is simulated and email uses a console backend; real payments and email wait on credentials (slice 5, gated).

Steps

  1. Verify an email and mobile channel by code before anything is stored beyond a draftApplicant
  2. Choose a membership path (individual, family, student, newlywed) and any club membershipsApplicant
  3. Set consents per field and per channel; the source of each consent is recordedApplicant
  4. Pay once at checkout; a payment carrying national and club dues splits at captureApplicant; platform posts
  5. A contact who joins confirms the contact record is theirs; the membership is added and each role keeps its own consents; no role consent becomes a member consent (P21 §7.2)Applicant; platform
  6. Offer 'Find yourself on the family tree'; skipping is a complete answer (D73; P28 §4.4)Applicant or member
  7. Receive AFRP's receipt and a first-login checklist (household, consent, directory, tree)Platform
  8. Renew each scope on its own clock; grace is shown as grace, never rounded to currentMember
  9. Record dues collected by a club; standing starts at collection, not remittanceClub treasurer

Trigger, frequency, records, money

Trigger
A person applies to join, or a membership scope reaches renewal
Frequency
On demand for joins; yearly per scope for renewals
Records
Member, household, verified channels, consents with their source, each membership scope with its dates, payments and receipts
Money
National dues to AFRP; club dues held for the club; gifts beyond dues receipted separately

Rule against practice

  • The 2024 by-laws make good standing need AFRP and club dues; the 2026 restated draft defines standing on AFRP dues alone. Whether the restatement was adopted is unknown (Q-33).
  • The 2024 by-laws make club dues part of good standing; no document says whether a member may opt out of local dues, and one club collects national and local dues separately (the Federation's club records; Q-36, the rule stays D1).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): membership runs on the calendar year with auto-renewal; dues are recognised when paid, and the books carry an unused deferred-dues account (Q-233). No account on either side of the chart carries club-collected national dues (Q-36).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the current registration system creates a staff-vetted account before any membership is bought, with a profile (club, date of birth, occupation, address) and no consent row; that account is the largest set of non-members the Federation holds, and its occupation field has been used for outreach. D60's contact record is narrower (Q-70, Q-236).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): at least one club's 2026 draft by-laws admit members more widely than By-Law 4.1.1; what such a member's standing is on the platform is not written (Q-226).

Hands off to

  • join → tree: the join creates the member's tree node, which is never a lineage claim (D30)
  • join → ledger: national dues are AFRP's money at the first dollar; club dues are held for the club (D1, R21)
  • join → directory: per-field consent set at join governs what the directory shows (JD-023)
  • join → comms: consent defaults bind to the verified channel (S1)
