Giving, restricted funds and the endowment

Gifts land in named funds held by the right entity, with restriction set only by donor designation. Funds such as the Endowed Fund are shown on the Care branch and cannot be joined.

Rule against practice

  • D9 receipts a gift by the entity that receives it; Medical Mission gifts were taken through AFRP's general fund and forwarded to ARFHSN (ARFHSN board minutes, 2023). D56 now sets the rule.
  • D2 places the endowment in ARFECF; the 2025 distribution guide, an unadopted draft, names an AFRP Endowed Fund held across AFRP and ARFECF accounts (Q-35).
  • The 2025 distribution guide (draft) sets a cap on a three-year average; the Federation's budgets apply the policy statements' fixed split to the 31 May valuation (Q-35).
  • ARFECF By-Laws 6.3.4 and 6.4.4 let each fund committee alone authorise its spending; in practice budgets are adopted at the Convention and the ARFECF Board approves restricted spending (the Federation's approved budgets).
  • The fund policy statements (2012, 2015) require an annual report of donations and distributions by 31 March; none is seen after 2013 (the Federation's officer records).
  • D8 requires a purpose catalogue for raising in another entity's name; emergency appeals in 2020 and 2023 were set up ad hoc (ARFHSN board minutes).

Hands off to

  • giving → ledger: gifts post gross by fund; reversals run through receivables (R20, R25, S3)
  • giving → care grants: money given to a Care fund leaves it only through a Care grant (D63)
  • giving → club share: a gift carrying a club attribution shows on that club's statement (D57)
  • giving → comms: the four contributor moments, then an annual statement (D10)
  • giving → reporting: lapsed donors surface as a report that asks for a call (JF-003)
