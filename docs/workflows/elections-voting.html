Elections and voting

Secret ballots with a one-time inclusion receipt and recorded ballots for motions, on one engine. The electorate is frozen at a record date and never recomputed.

Built in the Hub
Partly built The elections console, digital ballots, receipts, the live floor and frozen rolls are built. The mailed CPA ballot and club elections (no club electorate) are not built; the suspension refusal fails to name its rule (JM-042).

Steps

  1. Draft the election and freeze the certified roll at the record dateElections officer
  2. Clear the merge queue as a gate on certificationFederation staff
  3. Offer eligible members candidates and a real blank; show the ineligible the refusal and its by-lawPlatform
  4. Cast and seal; show the receipt once, warning it cannot be reissuedMember
  5. Honour a paper request by closing the electronic ballot for that voterElections officer
  6. Count with two key-holder shares after close; no interim count existsKey-holders
  7. Certify the result against a hashNamed certifying officer

Hands off to

  • voting → rules: quorum, thresholds and eligibility are read from the versioned ruleset
  • voting → reporting: certified results and turnout are recorded; voter-to-token links are never stored

Programmes that use it

All, through the shared services.

Open

  • By-Law 9.2 amendment for digital voting awaits ratification; the 2024 mailed process is still the law (D4).
  • Decision 5 is open: when extraction stops, and where at-large members are counted.
  • Proxy voting and tie-breaking rules (Four-Lens §7.5).
