Workflow

Directory, RBPN and consent

A members-only directory in three formats - the members' directory with membership and club identified, at Federation and club scale; the professional services directory; the leadership directory of seats and their votes - where display, contact and export are three separate consents. No minor appears in any payload, nothing about the living shows signed out, and nothing is printed or exported.

Built in the Hub
Partly built Directory, consents, the broker, RBPN listings and the job board are built (slice 10); the 90-day grace is attested (slice D1). The membership mark and club filter (DIR1), the leadership directory of seats (DIR2) and any "near me" axis (DIR3, gated on Q-48) are not built (design note P19, AFRP-Directory-Formats.md). Bulk export refuses for everyone; on David's direction of 1 October 2026 no format is printed or exported.

Steps

  1. Set per-field visibility; the ceiling is members, never publicMember
  2. Browse the members' directory, where presence means membership in good standing or within the grace, with the club or clubs shown as the Federation's fact; filter it to one club from the club's pageMember
  3. Search with transliteration folding and aliasesMember
  4. Contact through the broker; the address itself never shipsMember
  5. Publish an RBPN profession listing with the place of business as listing data, never the home address; request a promoted business listing (the professional services directory, members only)Member
  6. Verify membership and ownership before a listing exists; a licence is never verified and the listing says soRBPN desk
  7. Read the leadership directory: every seat on the Executive Committee, the Board (By-Law 6.2.2), the Council, the committees and each club's officers, with whether the seat carries a vote under the text that composes the body, and a seat channel for contact; never the holder's personal details or their own franchiseMember; the Board and the Council on the same page
  8. Keep the seats true from the officers form, the election results and a dated committee register until the committee workspace exists (the crosswalk's design note P1)Federation office
  9. Drop a lapsed member after the grace windowPlatform

Trigger, frequency, records, money

Trigger
A member sets visibility, searches or lists a profession
Frequency
On demand; lapsed members drop after the grace window
Records
Per-field visibility and contact consents, brokered contacts, RBPN listings and their verification, seats with their dates, votes and channels
Money
None (paying for a listing is not decided)

Rule against practice

  • The Federation printed and sold a membership directory and printed an annual Committee Directory with contact details (the officer archive, 2009–2026); the platform prints neither (David, 1 October 2026) and the Magazine's professional directory is the one print surface, on print consent.

Hands off to

  • directory → comms: contact runs through the broker, never an exported address
  • directory → magazine: a lapsed listing drops out of the magazine's professional directory
