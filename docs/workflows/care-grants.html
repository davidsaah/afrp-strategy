Workflow

Care grants and partner projects

A partner or a committee asks for support; the Board of the entity that holds the money decides with a ceiling, the grant is paid on delivery with two signatories, and it closes only when the partner's signed agreement, a delivery confirmation and an inventory of any equipment are on record. The outbound side of giving.

Steps

  1. Submit a request with its purpose, the receiving partner, quotes and the fund it would draw onProgramme committee, for a partner or a committee member
  2. Check fit with the programme and with the fund's restriction; a restricted gift is spent only on its purpose (D2, D3)Programme committee
  3. Dispose of each request in one of four ways — approve with a sum, reject with a reason, refer to the Medical Mission committee, or return for itemised detail — in practice (research, 2 Oct 2026); whether the register carries the last two as states is Q-217ARFHSN Board
  4. Approve with a spending ceiling and record the vote; no approval tiers apply until the holding entity adopts a finance manual written for a volunteer board (D63)The holding entity's Board (ARFHSN's Board for the Medical Mission, Women to Women and Human Services funds; D55, D62)
  5. Record each club's share where clubs co-fund the grant (D57)Programme coordinator; club treasurer
  6. Record a club's co-funding when the club's trustee confirms it in the meeting — in practice (research, 2 Oct 2026; D57)Club trustee on the ARFHSN Board; programme coordinator
  7. Sign the agreement with the receiving partner and place it on the agreements registerEntity chair or designated signatory
  8. Pay on delivery from the named fund, with two signatories (D63; ARFHSN By-Laws Art. III §5)Entity treasurer and a second designated signatory
  9. Confirm delivery and record an inventory of any equipment handed overFederation representative in Ramallah; receiving partner
  10. Close the grant only when the partner's signed agreement, the delivery confirmation and the inventory are on record (D63)Platform
  11. Tell contributors the funds were transferred and the purpose fulfilled (D10); report the outcome to the BoardProgramme committee; platform
  12. Produce ARFHSN's six-monthly financial and operational report to the AFRP Board from the ledger and the grants register (D63)Platform; ARFHSN treasurer

Rule against practice

  • ARFHSN By-Laws Art. III §5 require two co-signatures on all cheques; ARFHSN's signatories for the Ramallah account are not recorded by role (Q-104).
  • ARFHSN's draft finance manual (2026, not adopted) sets approval tiers for paid roles; the Board votes a ceiling on each request (ARFHSN board minutes). D63 applies no tiers until a manual for a volunteer board is adopted.
  • ARFHSN By-Laws Art. VI set up a standing grant committee; it is not seen in ARFHSN's board minutes.
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): a multi-year tuition programme abroad is approved as an earmark and drawn down per instalment with a trainee count and no names; who confirms 'delivery' is Q-217.
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the monthly ARFHSN meeting is convened by the Federation's Program Director with the ARFHSN secretary's agenda; D62 says the Federation President convenes (Q-214).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): Women to Women's committee approves its projects and reports to the ARFHSN board, and ARFHSN's minutes show the committee reporting rather than the board voting, against D63 (Q-114). The practice is described in its own workflow.
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): a club's closed branch balance is held by ARFHSN as an earmark 'for' that club, a shape the record does not have (Q-216).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): requests arrive as letters and proposals under 'new requests for funding'; no request form or criteria sheet exists.

Hands off to

  • care grants → giving: the grant draws on gifts held by fund; contributors hear at transfer and at fulfilment (D10)
  • care grants → reporting: grants made and delivered are a Care baseline count (D67) and feed the six-monthly ARFHSN report (D63)
  • care grants → agreements: each grant's signed partner agreement sits on the agreements register
  • care grants → club share: a co-funded grant lists each club's share on the club statements (D57)
  • care grants → Women to Women: the sub-fund's published procedure is a grant run by its committee (Q-114)

Open

The sources show three paths decided three ways (the Relief Fund workflow; Q-109, Q-207). *Since 3 Oct 2026:* D83 names AFRP as the fund's holding entity; who approves a transfer between the Board's votes stays open (Q-109).

  • Federation grants to clubs (start-up stipends, revival travel) have no written procedure; whether they run on this workflow is not decided.
