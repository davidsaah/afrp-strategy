Workflow

Care grants and partner projects

A partner or a committee asks for support; the Board of the entity that holds the money decides with a ceiling, the grant is paid on delivery with two signatories, and it closes only when the partner's signed agreement, a delivery confirmation and an inventory of any equipment are on record. The outbound side of giving.

Steps

  1. Submit a request with its purpose, the receiving partner, quotes and the fund it would draw onProgramme committee, for a partner or a committee member
  2. Check fit with the programme and with the fund's restriction; a restricted gift is spent only on its purpose (D2, D3)Programme committee
  3. Approve with a spending ceiling and record the vote; no approval tiers apply until the holding entity adopts a finance manual written for a volunteer board (D63)The holding entity's Board (ARFHSN's Board for the Medical Mission, Women to Women and Human Services funds; D55, D62)
  4. Record each club's share where clubs co-fund the grant (D57)Programme coordinator; club treasurer
  5. Sign the agreement with the receiving partner and place it on the agreements registerEntity chair or designated signatory
  6. Pay on delivery from the named fund, with two signatories (D63; ARFHSN By-Laws Art. III §5)Entity treasurer and a second designated signatory
  7. Confirm delivery and record an inventory of any equipment handed overFederation representative in Ramallah; receiving partner
  8. Close the grant only when the partner's signed agreement, the delivery confirmation and the inventory are on record (D63)Platform
  9. Tell contributors the funds were transferred and the purpose fulfilled (D10); report the outcome to the BoardProgramme committee; platform
  10. Produce ARFHSN's six-monthly financial and operational report to the AFRP Board from the ledger and the grants register (D63)Platform; ARFHSN treasurer

Trigger, frequency, records, money

Trigger
A partner or committee submits a funding request
Frequency
On demand; the Relief Fund monthly; the report to the AFRP Board six-monthly
Records
Requests, quotes, Board votes with ceilings, partner agreements, payments with both signatories, delivery confirmations, equipment inventories, outcomes
Money
Grants and partner-project payments from restricted Care funds; club co-funding shares

Rule against practice

  • ARFHSN By-Laws Art. III §5 require two co-signatures on all cheques; ARFHSN's records show difficulty keeping a second signatory for the Ramallah account, and the signatories are not recorded by role.
  • ARFHSN's draft finance manual (2026, not adopted) sets approval tiers for paid roles; the Board votes a ceiling on each request (ARFHSN board minutes). D63 applies no tiers until a manual for a volunteer board is adopted.
  • ARFHSN By-Laws Art. VI set up a standing grant committee; it is not seen in ARFHSN's board minutes.

Hands off to

  • care grants → giving: the grant draws on gifts held by fund; contributors hear at transfer and at fulfilment (D10)
  • care grants → reporting: grants made and delivered are a Care baseline count (D67) and feed the six-monthly ARFHSN report (D63)
  • care grants → agreements: each grant's signed partner agreement sits on the agreements register
  • care grants → club share: a co-funded grant lists each club's share on the club statements (D57)

Open

  • Safeguarding beyond the three closing records (inspections, a hospital agreement drafted by counsel, a local committee in Ramallah) has been proposed since 2023 and is not written (Q-24; D63 settles the closing records only).
  • Which body approves the Relief Fund's monthly transfers is Not stated: D55 does not name the Relief Fund; the Federation runs it in practice (the Federation's records, 2020 on). The next approved budget would state its holding entity.
  • Who the designated signatories are, by role, is not recorded; ARFHSN's by-laws require two co-signatures (Art. III §5) without naming them.
  • Federation grants to clubs (start-up stipends, revival travel) have no written procedure; whether they run on this workflow is not decided.
