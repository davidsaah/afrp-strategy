Events and registration

One event row seen by the member, the club, the programme and the federation. Each event carries one scope; its money routes by host.

Built in the Hub
Partly built Member registration, household registration and club events with door check-in and attendance (B2) are built. Member QR check-in, banquet seats as inventory and volunteer roles (B2b) are not built; the deep read of Breeze (1 October 2026) adds to B2b a self check-in kiosk, check-out times, an anonymous headcount beside named attendance, volunteer reminders and the volunteer's own blockout dates (Breeze Parity §1a).

Steps

  1. Create and publish an event with one scope; cross-listing never moves the moneyClub officer, programme organiser or federation staff
  2. Register self or household, with RSVP and waitlistMember or household adult
  3. Collect a ticket price; a money-taking event must print its surplus and shortfall rules first (D7)Organiser
  4. Check in at the door; a minor leaves only with a standing holder or someone on the release listClub check-in role
  5. Record attendance; "missed the last N" raises a follow-up by nameClub officer
  6. Run the club's standard monthly meeting from an agenda template, and the club's own ticketed events on the club's money; cross-list a national club night held on one day to every club without moving the moneyClub officers; Federation staff for the national night

Trigger, frequency, records, money

Trigger
An organiser publishes an event
Frequency
On demand, all year
Records
Events with one scope and host, registrations, household attendees, waitlists, check-ins, attendance and release lists
Money
Ticket purchases routed by the event's host; gifts beyond a ticket receipted separately

Rule against practice

  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): RBPN's 2026 speaker series registered through a Microsoft Teams event, outside the member portal and the registration system; which door counts as attendance is Q-181.
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the Mid-Year registration did not ask at least one 2026 registrant for dues; the membership-mandatory gate does not always fire today (Q-225).

Hands off to

  • events → ledger: event money routes by the event's host; the class code is attribution only (R23)
  • events → clubs: attendance gaps raise follow-ups on an officer's desk (B2)
  • events → giving: a fundraising ticket is non-deductible; a gift beyond it is receipted separately (D9)
