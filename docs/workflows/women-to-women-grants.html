Women to Women: proposal to disbursement

The procedure Women to Women publishes: a project is proposed to the committee's contact in Ramallah, approved by the committee in writing, paid from ARFHSN's US account to an account in Ramallah, and disbursed by the contact against receipts. D63 has the holding entity's Board approve with a ceiling; practice is the committee (Q-114).

Built in the Hub
Designed The procedure is published on the Federation's website and six years of projects are in ARFHSN's records. Designed as a committee sub-fund on the Care grants register (P11 §2, §6); its tuition awards are P11 grant rows (P26 §6.1). Who approves a project is Q-114. Not built.

Steps

  1. Propose a project in writing, with the need and the amount, within the published project band (Q-213)Women's organisation in Ramallah, through the committee's Ramallah contact
  2. Put the proposal to the committee; its September meeting finalises donations, proposals and student evaluations — in practice (research, 2 Oct 2026)Ramallah contact; Women to Women committee
  3. Approve the grant with a ceiling and record the vote (D63) — in practice the committee approves and reports to the ARFHSN board, which does not vote (Q-114)ARFHSN Board under D63; Women to Women committee in practice
  4. Account for the gifts in ARFHSN's US account and transfer to the account in Ramallah (Q-104)ARFHSN treasurer
  5. Disburse against receipts and return the receipts to the committeeRamallah contact
  6. Close only with the partner's agreement, a delivery confirmation and any inventory on record (D63); repeat partners have no agreement todayPlatform
  7. Evaluate student applications and award tuition support — in practice since 2026 (Q-211)Women to Women committee
  8. Sponsor a member's Arabic-class seat each term on a short statement — in practice (Q-168)Women to Women committee
  9. Post the sub-fund's income from direct gifts, a book sale, the Convention women's luncheon and the representatives' yearly minimum (Q-213)ARFHSN treasurer
  10. Report the sub-fund as its own section in ARFHSN's report to the AFRP Board (D63; ARFHSN By-Laws Art. III §7)ARFHSN treasurer

Trigger, frequency, records, money

Trigger
A proposal reaches the Ramallah contact
Frequency
On demand; decisions at the committee's meetings, the September one finalising the year
Records
Proposals with need and amount, committee decisions, transfers, the contact's receipts; student applications (where held is not stated)
Money
Gifts, book proceeds, luncheon proceeds and representatives' minimums in; project payments, tuition and class-fee sponsorships out through the Ramallah account

Rule against practice

  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): D63 has the holding entity's Board approve each grant with a ceiling; the published procedure and ARFHSN's minutes have the committee approve (Q-114).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): D55 makes Women to Women a sub-fund of ARFHSN; ARFECF's statements carry Women to Women gifts and a project payment for one year (Q-212).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the CPA's view is that endowed Women to Women money stays in ARFHSN's investment accounts as a separately accounted fund; the AFRP President asked that no new endowed fund be made before one fundraising strategy exists (Q-210).
