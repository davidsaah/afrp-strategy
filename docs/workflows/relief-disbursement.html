Relief Fund: money in and three paths out

A separate account in the Federation's name, reported at every Board meeting by the Educational Fund Treasurer. Money comes in after each Convention and from appeals; it leaves by three paths decided three ways, and only the first has a written rule. AFRP holds the fund (D83, 3 Oct 2026); the receipt's deductibility text waits on the CPA, and ARFECF's booking of the fund is a reconciliation item.

Steps

  1. Deposit the per-registration amount from each Convention's settlement as one dated line; take appeal gifts in an emergency through the Federation's procedure (By-Law 11.1.4) — in practice (research, 2 Oct 2026)Executive Administrator
  2. Path 1, emergency support: vote a sum per month for a term with a distribution plan and the condition that recipients are told the Federation is the source; extend by a further vote (the only written rule; the Board's votes of 2020–21 and the General Assembly's of 2021)AFRP Board; General Assembly
  3. Path 2, the annual Ramallah charities: vote the line on the Educational Fund page of the budget at the Convention, funded from the Relief Fund; the list of organisations and the amount for each is not written (Q-207); the platform refuses to mark the distribution approved while that list's authoriser row is empty (P23 §4.2)General Assembly; the Federation's representative in Ramallah
  4. Path 3, ad hoc: a seasonal distribution or a one-off wire went out in practice with no approval on file; on the platform it is refused without an approval act (P23 §4.3; Q-109)Executive Administrator executes; approver not on file
  5. Transfer from the Relief Fund to the Education Fund account and wire the Federation's Ramallah account, recorded as one linked act on both chequebooks — in practice since 2024 (in 2020 the wire went direct)Executive Administrator; Educational Fund Treasurer
  6. Distribute in Ramallah from an account that needs two local signatures; report counts, amounts, dates and organisations from the beneficiary-organisation register, never a person (P23 §7; Q-246, Q-247)The Federation's representative in Ramallah
  7. File the one-page statement (opening balance, deposits, expenses, closing balance) to every Board meeting beside the Education Fund and Cook Book statements (Q-174)Educational Fund Treasurer
  8. Post to AFRP, the holding entity (D83); a gift is receipted without a deductibility claim until the CPA settles the text (D9; P11 §4); ARFECF's booking of the fund is a reconciliation item (the books follow D83, or the Boards revisit)Platform
  9. Keep family lists outside the platform; a relief payment carries a count and an amount (P11 §4)Platform

Trigger, frequency, records, money

Trigger
A Board vote on emergency support; the budget's charities line; a Convention settlement; an appeal
Frequency
Monthly during emergency support; the charities yearly; a deposit after each Convention; a statement at every Board meeting
Records
Deposits with their source, the votes and distribution plans, transfers and wires as linked pairs, the representative's counts and totals, the per-meeting statement; never family lists
Money
Per-registration Convention amounts and appeal gifts in; monthly support, the annual charities and seasonal distributions out, through the Education Fund account since 2024

Rule against practice

  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the record names no holding entity; ARFECF's reviewed statements book relief gifts as its donor-restricted contributions and the Ramallah charities as its programme expense, and ARFECF's balance sheet holds the account with AFRP owing it, while the account carries the Federation's name and the AFRP Board votes emergency support (Q-109). *Since 3 Oct 2026:* D83 names AFRP; ARFECF's booking is a reconciliation item (the books follow D83, or the Boards revisit).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): By-Law 11.1.4 bars soliciting for a Federation project without the Board's and the Executive Committee's authority, and 11.1.5 routes club-raised funds for it through headquarters; the Board said in May 2026 that club gifts to needy families should come through one channel, preferably the Relief Fund, and clubs give directly to Ramallah projects (Q-228).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): a treasurer's projection assumes the per-registration amount continues to 2030; no decision continues it after 2025 (Q-126).
