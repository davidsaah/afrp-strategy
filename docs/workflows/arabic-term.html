Workflow

Arabic classes, term by term

Three terms a year of weekly online classes on two tracks, taught by a partner institution paid per seat, with a placement test, a disclaimer, volunteer conversation partners from the clubs, certificates and a closing survey. Members pay the member rate; non-members the public rate (D60).

Built in the Hub
Designed Runs today through the Federation's current member portal, outside this platform (the programme committee's records, 2025–2026). Designed in P12 §3 (`AFRP-Selection-Programmes.md`, AR1–AR4); the non-member learner is P21's learner role (`AFRP-Contact-Record.md`, CR1). Not built.

Steps

  1. Publish the term: classes, the two tracks (Traditional, Conversational), levels, dates, the member rate and the public rateArabic committee
  2. Register and pay; a member pays the member rate; a non-member pays the public rate and is kept as a contact record (name, verified channel, consent), not a membership (D60)Learner
  3. Sponsor a member's class fee from another programme's fund — in practice (research, 2 Oct 2026) the Women to Women committee pays a small number of member women's fees each term on a short statement; the cross-entity payment has no rule (Q-168)Women to Women committee; Arabic committee
  4. Accept the programme's disclaimer before the first class (the committee's practice)Learner
  5. Place by level with a placement testInstructor; platform
  6. Pair learners with volunteer conversation partners recruited through club presidents; the club's part is recorded against the club (D57)Arabic committee; club presidents
  7. Pay the teaching partner per seat under its agreement, from the holding entity's fund (D55)Federation office, for the holding entity
  8. Issue certificates and run the end-of-term surveyArabic committee

Trigger, frequency, records, money

Trigger
The committee publishes a term
Frequency
Three terms a year
Records
Classes, registrations, members and non-member contact records (name, verified channel, consent; D60), placements, disclaimers, certificates, survey results
Money
Class fees at the member and public rates; per-seat payments to the teaching partner

Rule against practice

  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the public page says registration is open to adult members only, against D60's public rate (Q-171).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): a 2026 summer pilot ran on a separate learning platform at its own price (Q-170); teacher incentives tied to retention are on the committee's agenda (Q-169); some learners joined the Federation through the programme.

Hands off to

  • Arabic term → agreements: the teaching partner's agreement and per-seat terms sit on the register
  • Arabic term → comms: a non-member contact receives class messages on the consent they gave (D60)
  • Arabic term → join: a non-member learner may join later; the contact record is not a membership (D60)
  • Arabic term → club share: clubs that recruit volunteer partners are recorded against the club (D57)
