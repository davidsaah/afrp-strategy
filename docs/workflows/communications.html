Communications and consent

Two senders, club and federation, on one consent model. Consent is checked when the audience is built, and the withheld are stored by name.

Built in the Hub
Partly built Consent-filtered audiences, classes and the withheld list are built for clubs and federation. Real email waits on a provider (slice 5); texting waits on a provider, budget and either a registered brand on a long code or a shared short code through a provider, which is what the clubs have in Breeze today (B5); when it exists, each club texts under its own sender name on the one account where the route allows it (Club Experience Plan §4.5; Breeze Parity §1a).

Steps

  1. Build the audience from a segment, group or rosterClub officer or federation staff
  2. Remove no-consent and no-verified-address recipients before composingPlatform
  3. Compose with a class: operational, marketing or programme invitationSender
  4. Send, gated on message:sendSender with the grant
  5. Show undeliverable as not tracked, never as zeroPlatform
  6. Mute or change consent per channel; each change is a dated rowMember
  7. Treat a member with no consent rows as "no" for marketing and programme invitations and "yes" for operational messages they must receive (a design choice, Club Experience Plan §9.2; Q-46); treat an opt-in imported from a club's Breeze as evidence, not consent, until the member confirms on first sign-inPlatform
  8. Address a household about a child to the adult who holds authority (R6); a minor is never an audience memberPlatform
