Workflow

Communications and consent

Two senders, club and federation, on one consent model. Consent is checked when the audience is built, and the withheld are stored by name.

Built in the Hub
Partly built Consent-filtered audiences, classes and the withheld list are built for clubs and federation. Real email waits on a provider (slice 5); texting waits on a provider, budget and 10DLC registration (B5).

Governed by

Steps

  1. Build the audience from a segment, group or rosterClub officer or federation staff
  2. Remove no-consent and no-verified-address recipients before composingPlatform
  3. Compose with a class: operational, marketing or programme invitationSender
  4. Send, gated on message:sendSender with the grant
  5. Show undeliverable as not tracked, never as zeroPlatform
  6. Mute or change consent per channel; each change is a dated rowMember

Hands off to

  • comms → reporting: sends and exclusions are recorded; undeliverable is reported as not tracked
