Selection programmes

Programmes with limited places choose participants the same way: apply, vet, vote with a tie-break, offer, travel, reimburse, alumni. The member's club may share the cost on its statement.

Built in the Hub
Designed Found in the Exchange Mission's 2020 operating procedure, the 2026 Leadership Ramallah rules and the Day of Action records. Steps

  1. Publish the call: places, eligibility, dates, the costs covered and any club shareProgramme committee
  2. Apply in the platform with the programme's form; past cycles' applications never enter (D61)Member (applicant)
  3. Where the programme takes registration instead of applications (the Day of Action in 2026), register by the deadline; the first-timer's covered costs are the only gate — in practice (research, 2 Oct 2026; Q-115)Member (registrant)
  4. Vet applications against the programme's criteria (the 2020 Exchange Mission operating procedure)Vetting subcommittee
  5. Vote; the coordinator and the chair break a tie (the 2020 Exchange Mission operating procedure)Programme committee
  6. Offer a place; a declined place goes to the next ranked applicantProgramme coordinator
  7. Record the club's share of travel or fees on the club's statement, where the programme sets one (D57)Club treasurer; programme coordinator
  8. Train participants and run the trip or eventProgramme committee
  9. Reimburse eligible costs on receipts from the holding entity's fund (D55; the Day of Action from AFRP's General Fund, D65)Treasurer of the holding entity
  10. Add the participant to the programme's alumni (name, year, club); applications, essays, résumés and passports never enter (D61)Platform

Trigger, frequency, records, money

Trigger
A programme opens its call
Frequency
Yearly per programme (the Day of Action's applications open in September)
Records
Applications in the platform for future cycles, vetting notes, votes and tie-breaks, offers, club shares, reimbursements, alumni (D61)
Money
Travel and fee reimbursements from the holding entity's fund; club travel shares

Rule against practice

  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the Day of Action in 2026 is an open registration, free for adult members, with no application and no selection; the page and the mailing differ on the deadline and the eligibility sentence; inviting other village and town associations at their own expense is under discussion (Q-115, Q-186, Q-187).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): in 2024 Project Hope did not travel and ran jointly with the Day of Action in Washington; which entity holds a joint year's money and which committee decides places is Q-172.
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the Exchange Mission's October delegation has not run since 2023 (Q-188); the Fellowship's committee reads a one-paragraph summary per applicant and does not score; a vetting template holds free-text interview notes about an applicant's views (Q-118).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): Leadership Ramallah's 2025 window ran from the autumn to a January deadline, and the 2027 summit moves to January with the Mid-Year (Q-176); its co-chairs are the Young Leaders Committee's (Q-175).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): Women to Women now evaluates student applications for tuition support (Q-211).

Hands off to

  • selection → club share: a club's travel share shows on its statement (D57)
  • selection → alumni: participants become programme alumni, without their applications (D61)
  • selection → advocacy: the Day of Action's participants are chosen here
  • selection → comms: calls and offers go through the consent filter

Programmes that use it

Leadership Ramallah · Project Hope · Educational & Cultural Exchange Mission · Day of Action

Open

  • Leadership Ramallah's holding entity is Not stated in D55; the next approved budget would state it.
  • Project Hope's selection procedure is a draft copied from the Exchange Mission's; Leadership Ramallah's protocol is not on file; the Day of Action's selection criteria are not written.
  • Eligibility (age, membership) is set per programme by its committee; the record does not hold it for every programme.
  • Deleting past applications and passports from the shared drive is the committee's rule (D61), outside the platform.
  • Application-and-selection or open registration for the Day of Action (Q-115).
  • A joint Day of Action and Project Hope year (Q-172).
  • Women to Women's tuition awards as a selection cycle (Q-211).
