Workflow

Family tree and life events

The tree sits on the Heritage branch, and every member has a node on it and the platform resolves people through it. Births, marriages, graduations and deaths queue for the Family Tree Committee before anything changes.

Built in the Hub
Partly built Node, hourglass, committee queue, life-event pipeline and mailings are built (slices T1a–T1c). *Since 2 October 2026:* the plate replaces the hourglass as the default view (D68 supersedes D27); since 3 October 2026 the hourglass stays available as an alternative view (D77), drawn in the plate grammar (D80). Steps

  1. Open a plate in the book's grammar (D68) and find a line by the oldest ancestor you can name; draft changes in edit mode and submit the sitting as one bundle (D21, D70; P28 §4.1)Member in current national standing
  2. At join or renewal, offer 'Find yourself on the family tree'; a link proposal queues and the node stays unattached meanwhile; skipping is a complete answer and no membership decision reads the node (D73, D30; P28 §4.4)Applicant or member
  3. Declare a life event; R6 decides who may declare about a minorMember or household adult
  4. Queue the declaration; the queue triages itself, unsourced structural claims firstPlatform
  5. Pre-review and recommend for the clan; never approve (D72)Clan steward
  6. Decide, send back for information, or reject; only then does the record change. One approval applies an own-household life event or a non-structural correction; a structural change needs two approvals from different committee members (D72)Family Tree Committee
  7. Fan out on acceptance: node, household, offers, magazine hold, heritage layerPlatform
  8. Import the record keeper's GEDCOM only through the diff preview, against the Hub's current version, during D69's parallel run: a change touching nothing changed in the Hub applies as an audited committee edit, a change made on both sides is held as a conflict; an import never deletes anyone. After the run an external import is refused naming D69 (P28 §4.3)Family Tree Committee (tree:moderate)
  9. Write a GEDCOM backup to private storage every night, each a dated row; a failed night shows on the committee console (D69; P28 §4.3)Platform

Rule against practice

  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): one project lead receives, approves and applies every change to a master file in a desktop genealogy product; the committee has two members and no queue; corrections arrive by a form or email (D13, D14; Q-200). The practice is described in its own workflow (Family-tree submission and correction, as run).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): access to the clan books is by a request the lead approves and to the interactive tree by personal invitation, against D17's 'all members see everything', now D71's own branch (Q-200); the clan-book PDFs may carry living persons' dates (D28; Q-202).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): a volunteer prototyped a second site holding the tree with archive profiles (D66; Q-190).

Hands off to

  • tree → magazine: a birth announcement runs on household consent (D33); a death is held until the family approves (R34)
  • tree → membership: a death terminates memberships at save; incentives are offered, never auto-granted
  • tree → directory: the deceased are withheld; living people show no dates signed out (R34, D28); signed in, the living show in full only inside the viewer's own branch (D71, out to third cousins by D76); the directory integration comes after magazine and life events (D78) and Heritage (D86)
  • tree → scholarship: relationships the tree can see are disclosed with their degree on review
