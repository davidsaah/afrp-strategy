Reporting and oversight

Federation, club and programme views built from counts, not rows. Every aggregate carries complementary suppression so no arithmetic across reports names a person.

Built in the Hub
Partly built Federation dashboard, lapsed donors, club roll-up and programme pipeline counts are built. The board workspace and committee reporting wait on a design note; QuickBooks and 990 support are not built.

Steps

  1. Read membership health, giving by fund and open items on the dashboardFederation staff
  2. Call lapsed donors from the reportFederation staff
  3. Benchmark a club against federation aggregatesClub officer
  4. Read programme pipeline counts, suppressed under the floorProgramme organiser
  5. Publish the cross-programme funnel annually as a frozen snapshotExecutive director's office (data custodian)
