Steps

  1. Read membership health, giving by fund and open items on the dashboardFederation staff
  2. Call lapsed donors from the reportFederation staff
  3. Benchmark a club against federation aggregatesClub officer
  4. Read the clubs page: per club, its standing for the year, membership, club life, the Federation's work in it (campers, scholars, participants, volunteers, gifts attributed), the statement balance each way and its migration state; every cell under the floor (Club Experience Plan §10)Executive Director; the Council of Chapter Club Presidents
  5. Read programme pipeline counts, suppressed under the floorProgramme organiser
  6. Publish the cross-programme funnel annually as a frozen snapshotExecutive director's office (data custodian)
  7. Produce ARFHSN's six-monthly financial and operational report to the AFRP Board from the ledger and the grants register (D63)Platform; ARFHSN treasurer
  8. File one-page statements for the Education Fund, the Relief Fund and the Cook Book fund (opening balance, deposits, expenses, closing balance) to every Board meeting — in practice (research, 2 Oct 2026); whether the platform produces them, and in whose name, is Q-174Educational Fund Treasurer
  9. Report each branch's year-one baseline: two or three counts that exist today, such as campers per club per year, scholars by track, mission volunteers, grants made and delivered, members by level (D67)Programme committees; platform

Rule against practice

  • ARFHSN By-Laws Art. III §7 require a report to the AFRP Board every six months; the minutes show one presentation (ARFHSN board minutes, 2023).
  • ARFHSN By-Laws Art. III §9 have the AFRP Board retain an outside accountant to certify ARFHSN's finances; in 2026 an accountant's review is under way, not an audit (ARFHSN board minutes, 2026).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): ARFHSN's report to the AFRP Board carries sections for its general account, Women to Women, Gaza relief transfers, Mission gifts, expenses, earmarks (one holding a club's closed branch balance) and investments (Q-216, Q-217).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the Executive Committee planned in August 2026 that committee chairs receive budget-versus-actual sheets fed from the books without the organisation's full statements.

Hands off to

  • reporting → clubs: federation context (a club's rate against the median) returns to the club lens as counts
  • reporting → AFRP Board: the six-monthly ARFHSN report is built from the ledger and the grants register (D63)
