Camp Ramallah application and season

Camp is a selection problem, not an enrolment funnel. A parent applies under the household; selection is by quota with disclosed odds; minor authority runs on the ratified R6.

Steps

  1. Apply for a camper seat under the householdParent or guardian with standing
  2. Select by quota with per-club odds disclosed; a four-per-family cap bounds first-comeCamp Ramallah committee
  3. Offer a freed seat one rank at a time in serialized windowsPlatform
  4. Gate arrival on the health form; the health record itself is a bought systemParent; camp staff
  5. Screen staff on the check and credential expiry; a paid CIT is an employeeCamp staff lead
  6. Release a camper only to a standing holder or someone on the release listCamp staff
  7. Publish photos per camper consent; report retention at k≥3Camp staff; platform
