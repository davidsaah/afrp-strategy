The Convention

The junction where all four branches meet once a year: registration, credentialing, the governance floor, the participant layer and the financial close.

Built in the Hub
Designed The floor and election consoles exist; the Convention element (registration desk, credentialing, room block, on-site run, money) is not built; it is designed in P22 (`AFRP-Convention-Operations.md`, CV1–CV8) between P8 and P13.

Steps

  1. Award the Convention to a qualified bidding club two years ahead; a region that fields no qualified bid forfeits its turn (By-Law 10.1.1, as the Legal Advisor read it for 2028 — in practice, research, 2 Oct 2026; P22 §3.1; Q-240); one qualified bid by voice or hands (9.1.2), two by a weighted delegate vote (9.1.1(2))General Assembly
  2. Register self and household; membership is mandatory for attendees; a non-member guest is a line on the registration with a credential class, not a contact (P21 §2; Q-238)Member
  3. Select delegates and file the club roster by live feed or dated manual roster; how a club chooses its delegates is not written (9.1.3) and is recorded as a dated act (P22 §6; Q-37)Club officer
  4. Credential delegates against standing on the remittance ledger and freeze the rollCredentials committee (time-boxed grant)
  5. Record the host agreement: the Federation's share, the holdback, the settlement date, the liaison and each side's obligationsConvention committee; host club president
  6. Check in delegates and members at the desk; presence feeds the floor's weights (9.1.3; P22 §5.4, §6)Federation office desk; host volunteers on a time-boxed grant
  7. Run the floor with weighted delegation; weights freeze when voting opens on a motion (S4)Presiding officer; elections officer
  8. Post stamped announcements and answer the help desk, owned and clockedConvention organisers
  9. Sell store items and banquet seats; post gross under the convention classConvention organisers
  10. Close the books; who bears a deficit reads from that year's host agreement (D59)Federation treasurer
  11. Settle with the host club within the agreed period and issue the settlement statement; the close runs as its own workflow (Convention close and host payment)Federation treasurer

Trigger, frequency, records, money

Trigger
A host club is set for the year's Convention
Frequency
Yearly, in July
Records
The host agreement's terms, registrations, delegate rosters and the frozen roll, floor votes, announcements, sales and the settlement statement
Money
Registration fees, store and banquet sales, sponsorships; the Federation's share and holdback; the settlement with the host club

Rule against practice

  • The 2009 convention contract set the Federation's share by host size and was never signed; a 2021 resolution set one flat share; the 2024 General Assembly voted a different one; the Convention Innovation Committee is discussing a split (Q-34; D59 now makes each year's host agreement the rule).
  • The host agreement template (2022) has the host pay keynote costs; a 2026 keynote agreement has the Federation paying (the Federation's convention records).
  • The 2024 by-laws credential delegates; in practice per-club voting lists are produced from the member database and no certification step is written (Q-37).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the 2026 books were reconciled under a revised host agreement the Board's minutes mention and no folder holds (Q-93); the close is described in its own workflow (Q-221, Q-222).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the host coordinator's role in today's registration system holds every guest's contact details with an export, seating control and a payment-by-payer report; the design's scoped grant (P13 §2.4) is narrower (Q-127).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the Legal Advisor described delegate voting in May 2026: each community certifies its paid members, those members vote on delegates, delegates cast the votes, and a member present may vote in person with the vote deducted from a delegate's total. The quorum rule and how a community's vote is held are unwritten (Q-37).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the Mid-Year guideline (2020–21) has the Executive Committee select the host and approve a sharp fee rise in writing; in 2026–27 the Board chose the host after quotes, the Federation signed the hotel contract, and sponsorship was raised by committee members by region and processed through ARFECF (Q-92, Q-224).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): a 2029 Convention in Ramallah is under review against a requirements checklist with gates and a GO / HOLD / RELOCATE / POSTPONE / CANCEL decision; a Federation-hosted Convention abroad is a model the templates do not carry (Q-223).

Hands off to

  • convention → voting: the certified roll frozen at record date drives delegate weight (R12)
  • convention → agreements: the host agreement is on the agreements register with its terms (D59)
  • convention → club share: the settlement with the host club shows on that club's statement (D57, D59)
  • convention → ledger: convention money is federation money at the first dollar (D1-G)
  • convention → magazine: statutory notices are computed back from the convention date
  • convention → close: the reconciliation, approval and payment to the host run in the close workflow (Q-221)
