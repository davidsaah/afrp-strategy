The Convention

The junction where all four branches meet once a year: registration, credentialing, the governance floor, the participant layer and the financial close.

Built in the Hub
Designed The floor and election consoles exist; the Convention element (registration desk, credentialing, room block, on-site run, money) is not built and is scheduled as its own project.

Shared services

Governed by

Steps

  1. Register self and household; membership is mandatory for attendeesMember
  2. Select delegates and file the club roster by live feed or dated manual rosterClub officer
  3. Credential delegates against standing on the remittance ledger and freeze the rollCredentials committee (time-boxed grant)
  4. Run the floor with weighted delegation; weights freeze when voting opens on a motion (S4)Presiding officer; elections officer
  5. Post stamped announcements and answer the help desk, owned and clockedConvention organisers
  6. Sell store items and banquet seats; post gross under the convention classConvention organisers
  7. Close the books; deficit responsibility reads from the Convention ContractFederation treasurer

Hands off to

  • convention → voting: the certified roll frozen at record date drives delegate weight (R12)
  • convention → ledger: convention money is federation money at the first dollar (D1-G)
  • convention → magazine: statutory notices are computed back from the convention date
