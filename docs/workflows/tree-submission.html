Workflow

Family-tree submission and correction, as run

Today one project lead receives, approves and applies every change to a master file held in a desktop genealogy product, and approves access to the clan books. The designed workflow (family tree and life events) queues every change for the Family Tree Committee; this records the practice it would replace.

Built in the Hub
Designed Practice found in the project's public page and the project's and committees' correspondence (2023–2026); it runs outside the platform. The designed workflow is partly built (slices T1a–T1c); the migration from this practice is designed in P27 (`AFRP-Family-Tree-In-Practice.md`, amended for D68–D74) and P28 (`AFRP-Family-Tree-Module.md`, T2a–T2h).

Steps

  1. Ask for a change (a birth, death, marriage or divorce date, an immigration date, a name correction) through the change-request form or by email — in practice (research, 2 Oct 2026)Member
  2. Apply the change to the master file; de-duplicate and prune — in practiceFamily-tree project lead
  3. Request access to the clan books; the lead approves 'by agreement with the board', with no written test (Q-200)Member; family-tree project lead
  4. Invite a person to the interactive tree through the product's cloud sharing — in practiceFamily-tree project lead
  5. Queue the same request for the Family Tree Committee before the record changes: a clan steward may recommend and never applies; one approval applies an own-household life event or a non-structural correction, two approvals from different committee members a structural change (D70, D72; P28 §4.2) — the design; today the committee has two members and no queueClan steward; Family Tree Committee
  6. Import the master file's edits into the Hub as diffs during D69's parallel run, conflicts held for a decision; after the run the file is read-only and the Hub exports every night (P28 §4.3); no licence holder or account is written (Q-201)Family Tree Committee (tree:moderate); the record keeper supplies the file
  7. Change the public page's text through one approver and the web agency — in practiceProgramme director
  8. Adopt the platform's queue as the committee's one intake by a dated act, with the committee's seats on CW1 (P27 stage 2)Family Tree Committee

Rule against practice

  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): D13 and D14 (now D70 and D72) have members in standing propose and the committee decide every change; a single approver decides and applies (Q-200).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): D17 (now D71) has members see the living by membership, not approval; the clan books need an approved request and the interactive tree a personal invitation (Q-200).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): D11 (superseded by D69) made the platform a governed mirror; the body of record is a desktop file under an account not in the Federation's name (Q-201).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): D28 shows nothing about the living without sign-in; the clan books may carry living persons' dates and go to approved requesters, not to members by standing (Q-202).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): D66 makes the shared drive the archive of record; a volunteer prototype proposes a new site holding the tree with archive profiles (Q-190).

Hands off to

  • submission → tree: a form row becomes a declaration in the committee's queue (D14). *Since 2 October 2026:* the form's rows are not imported; members propose in the platform, and requests already received arrive through the next export labelled as made outside the queue (P27 §4.3; D70)
  • submission → directory: access to the living follows D71 and D28, not a personal approval (Q-200)
  • submission → magazine: a birth or death reaches the magazine only through the queue (D33 for a birth, R34 for a death)
