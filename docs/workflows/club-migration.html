Workflow

Moving a club from Breeze

One club at a time, with a named owner on each side, from read-only access to a dated cutover. Nothing is written to Breeze; no pair is merged by software; the club's officers verify their own roster before anything moves.

Built in the Hub
Proposed The connector and the extract exist from the Portal era (Addendum 2 §3.4, steps 1–3); staging, identity resolution, the parallel-run report, sign-off and cutover are not built (B4). Before the first club: volunteer roles (B2b), a real email provider (slice 5), the secretary's queue and the officers form. The order of clubs: one pilot that uses Breeze for roster, groups and events and not for giving or texting; then the clubs whose Breeze is failing; clubs on no system by spreadsheet in parallel; the remaining Breeze clubs after texting and giving are answered (Club Experience Plan §8).

Steps

  1. Choose the club and name its owner on each sideFederation staff with the club president
  2. Give read-only API access; the key is held as a secret, never in the repoThe club's Breeze Account Owner, the only holder of the key
  3. Extract people, families, tags, events, attendance, form entries, volunteer roles, the custom fields and the consent flags as found into staging rows, through the vendor's API at its rate limit (attendance is not in the account export) with the account export as the fallback; report which custom fields and forms the club used and what will not move (notes, files, photos, recurring gifts)Platform
  4. Resolve identities against the register; send the review band to the merge queueMatch-queue reviewer, then the club secretary
  5. Run both systems in parallel for two to four weeks, the platform read-only for the club; verify the roster, groups and eventsThe club's officers
  6. Sign off and cut over as a dated decision; writes move to the platform and Breeze becomes read-onlyClub president and treasurer
  7. Keep the final export on the drive (D66); the club cancels its own subscription only after the export is verified, because the vendor queues the account for deletion on cancellationFederation staff; the club
  8. A club not on Breeze follows the same path from a spreadsheet uploadThe club's officers

Trigger, frequency, records, money

Trigger
A club agrees to move
Frequency
Once per club; two to four weeks each
Records
Staging rows, the merge queue, the parallel-run report, the sign-off, the archived export
Money
None moves; giving history only if B3 is answered yes

Rule against practice

  • About ten clubs are on Breeze and the rest use other tools or none (the club presidents' meeting, August 2026); each club's Breeze is its own account and its own key.

Hands off to

  • migration → club: the verified roster, groups and events become the club's on cutover
  • migration → join: a person in Breeze and not on the register enters as a contact or a club member as the officers decide, never as a national member (Article IV)

Programmes that use it

All, through the shared services.

Open

  • Which club goes first, and who holds its key (Breeze Parity §5 q4); the plan asks for one that does not take gifts or text in Breeze. Only the club's Account Owner holds the key; a club whose owner has left recovers ownership by the vendor's paper process first.
  • Whether the clubs accept what the platform refuses by design (export, printed directories and labels, free-text notes, deleting a person) (Breeze Parity §5 q7).
  • What the clubs recorded in Breeze custom fields; answered per club at the extract step (§5 q2).
  • What clubs built in Breeze forms beyond signup, join and application; answered the same way (§5 q6).
  • Whether Breeze giving history enters at all, which follows the B3 answer (§5 q3).
