Running a club

The club lens replaces Breeze: roster with dual status, groups, follow-ups, events, dues and the officer year, from the officers form on 1 August to the hand-over. The Federation sees counts, never names.

Steps

  1. File the officers form, started from last year's, naming every seat and any programme seats; this is the By-Law 3.2 filing and turns the club's standing pill for the yearClub president (by 1 August)
  2. Seat the Council of Chapter Club Presidents, the Board roster, the Membership Committee seat and the programme seats from the form; a mid-year change is a dated change on the formPlatform
  3. Read the roster with national and club status side by side, sorted by household; work the secretary's queue (a join naming this club, a household split, a move between clubs, a reported death)Club secretary or officer
  4. Make groups and raise follow-ups with due dates; a lapse raises one only if the club switched the rule onClub secretary or officer
  5. Hold the club's own contact roles: a prospect once the person acts on the club's invitation, referrals after consent, event guests with no export; never another club's contacts or a programme's roles (P21 §10; Q-72)Club secretary or officer
  6. Run the treasurer's month: collect, close, remit, reconcile (D1); review the club statement when a run is issued (D57)Club treasurer
  7. Select the delegation from certified members (By-Laws 9.1.3, 9.1.4) and hand it to the convention workflow; file a host bid if biddingClub president and officers
  8. Report to the Council twice a year from the dashboard counts (By-Law 10.5.1)Club president
  9. Hand over at the end of the officer year as a dated change of seat; the statement balance, open follow-ups and the calendar carry; the outgoing grants end the same day (a design choice, Club Experience Plan §4.1)Outgoing and incoming officers
  10. See a club's groups, events and follow-ups as counts under the floorFederation staff

Rule against practice

  • By-Law 3.2 requires clubs to file an affiliation form by 1 August; no forms are on file (the Federation's club records).
  • The 2020 letters set an attendance rule for the monthly club presidents' meeting; minutes from 2020 to 2026 exist and attendance is not tallied.
  • The 2024 by-laws require club money for AFRP projects to pass through headquarters; Mid-Year sponsorship cheques are payable to the host club (the Federation's Mid-Year records, 2026).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the chapter clubs are separate exempt organisations of several kinds (social clubs, a charity, a social club with a sister charitable foundation); the club register holds no legal columns (Q-227). The public annual report counts fourteen active chapters, the office's list seventeen and the voting lists eighteen (Q-229).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): after each presidents' meeting the Program Director compiles the clubs' priorities and events into a summary the Deputy President reviews before it returns to the clubs; it is the Council's working record.
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the president's view in today's registration system shows members' names and contact details, and clubs have asked for more; the record's counts-only and per-field-consent rules narrow this (Q-237, Q-44).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): clubs raise and send money to Ramallah projects directly; the Board said in May 2026 that club gifts to needy families should go through one channel, preferably the Relief Fund (Q-228).

Hands off to

  • club → ledger: collections are attested and remitted on the run (D1)
  • club → club share: the treasurer reviews each statement run (D57)
  • club → migration: a club arrives from Breeze or a spreadsheet through one path (B4)
  • club → comms: a club mailing reaches only its own roster, consent-filtered
  • club → convention: the delegation is selected and credentialed against standing
  • club → reporting: the Federation roll-up shows counts, never names
  • club → government affairs: a club's local action committee meets officials year-round (Q-131)
