Workflow

Partner agreements

Every agreement with an outside party (a hospital or university partner, the Arabic teaching partner, the scanning vendor, a sponsor, a Convention host club) is recorded with its parties, term and obligations, signed for the entity that carries the obligation, reminded before it lapses, and renewed or let lapse on the record.

Built in the Hub
Designed Found in the ARFHSN, ARFECF and Federation records (2016–2026): the Medical Mission's partner agreement with an annual renewal, the Arabic teaching partner, the scanning vendor, a multi-year sponsorship and the host-club agreement template. Steps

  1. Record the agreement: the parties, the term, the renewal date, each side's obligations and the programme it servesProgramme committee
  2. Name the entity that carries the obligation: the fund that budgets the programme (D55)Programme committee
  3. Sign for that entityEntity president or chair, or a designated signatory
  4. Link the signed copy in the shared drive; the platform keeps the register, not the document (D66)Platform
  5. For a Convention or Mid-Year host agreement, record the Federation's share, the holdback, the settlement date, the liaison and each side's obligations on the event (D59)Convention committee; host club president
  6. For a sponsorship, record the programmes the sponsor's money is allocated toFederation treasurer
  7. Remind the committee before the agreement expiresPlatform
  8. Record the decision to renew, renegotiate or let lapse, with its dateProgramme committee; the entity's Board

Trigger, frequency, records, money

Trigger
A programme enters an agreement with an outside party
Frequency
On demand; a reminder before each expiry
Records
Parties, term, renewal date, obligations, signatory, a link to the signed copy in the shared drive (D66), renewal decisions
Money
None directly; the agreements set per-seat payments, sponsorship allocations and convention shares

Rule against practice

  • The Medical Mission's partner agreement requires a renewal review before its annual expiry; no record shows the review was tracked (ARFHSN records, 2025–2026).
  • The host agreement template sets the convention's terms; no signed host agreement or settlement is on file (the Federation's convention records; D59 now makes the agreement the rule).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the Preservation Project's foundation grant ended on 30 September 2026 with funds unspent and a report due in November; a second scanning scope was quoted per box (Q-108).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the 2026 Convention closed under a revised host agreement the Board minuted and no folder holds (Q-93); for the 2027 Mid-Year the Federation, not the host, negotiated and signed the hotel contract (Q-92).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): Women to Women's repeat partners and the Medical Mission's training, screening and tuition partners have no agreement on paper (Q-217).

Hands off to

  • agreements → care grants: a Care grant closes only with the partner's signed agreement on record (D63)
  • agreements → convention: the host agreement's terms drive the settlement statement (D59)
  • agreements → Arabic term: the teaching partner is paid per seat under its agreement
  • agreements → club share: a host club's settlement shows on its statement (D57)

Open

  • How far ahead the reminder goes is Not stated; each agreement sets its own notice. The design note for Care grants and partner agreements would state the default.
  • Who may sign for each entity, by role, is not recorded; ARFHSN's by-laws name two co-signatures on cheques (Art. III §5), not signatories for agreements.
  • The Mid-Year has no host agreement on file; D59 says the same shape serves it.
  • Whether foundation grant applications and their report deadlines sit on the same register is not decided.
