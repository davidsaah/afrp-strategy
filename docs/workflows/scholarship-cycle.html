Scholarship cycle

Application to award for the Scholarship held by ARFECF. Median ranking, relationships disclosed on the record, encumbrance against the committee's budget, and disbursement gated on enrolment.

Built in the Hub
Built The committee engine is built (slices 4, 4b). Applicant material never enters the Hub. The spend rule and the committee's constitution route OPEN by design.

Steps

  1. Apply with transcripts, need documents and a lineage or sponsorship pathApplicant
  2. Review and score; relationships the tree sees are disclosed with their degreeScholarship committee reviewer
  3. Rank by median; wide spreads are flagged, ties left tiedScholarship committee
  4. Enter the award budget from the committee's report with a source and dateScholarship committee
  5. Match named awards most-constrained first; encumber forward commitmentsScholarship committee
  6. Verify enrolment each term before disbursementCommittee; staff
  7. Issue award receipts as ARFECF'sPlatform

Hands off to

  • scholarship → alumni: award history becomes the alumni register
  • scholarship → membership: a scholar receives a free student membership (J06 step 6)
  • scholarship → funds: awards draw on the ARFECF Scholarship Fund under its committee's authority
