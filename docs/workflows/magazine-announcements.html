Workflow

Magazine announcements, subscriptions and the issue as run

How Hathihe Ramallah runs today beside the designed issue cycle: announcements reach the editor through a city correspondent or the magazine's mailbox; obituaries are paid placements; the print run goes only to paid subscribers through a mailing house; the digital issue goes out as an email campaign; an appeal goes out when renewals lag.

Built in the Hub
Designed Practice found in the Magazine's and the office's records (2014–2026). The Hub's magazine module (slices 7, 7b) builds the designed cycle; the correspondent seat, paid placements, the subscription list and the mailing-house hand-off are designed in P24 (`AFRP-Magazine-Operations.md` §3, §5; MG1–MG5) and not built.

Steps

  1. Send a wedding, birth, graduation or obituary to the editor, directly or through the city correspondent, under the published guidelines — in practice (research, 2 Oct 2026)Family (member household); city correspondent
  2. Enter the life event on the household's behalf with the family's consent recorded, so that the section is generated and the household is not asked twice — the seat the design needs; the entry names who entered it and from what, and prints on the correspondent's recorded attestation that the family agreed (D89, answering Q-195) (P24 §3.2)City correspondent
  3. Approve the death as the named family contact (R34) and place the obituary as an order with its tribute; the fee is held unposted with a "payment received" receipt that claims no deductibility, until it is classed (P24 §3.3; Q-193)Family
  4. Hold a death until the family approves (R34)Magazine editor
  5. Lay out the issue for the editorDesign agency
  6. Produce the print mailing list without lapsed print subscribers and hand it to the mailing house — in practice since the May/June 2025 issueSubscriptions manager
  7. Send the digital issue as an email campaign; a digital copy comes with membership — in practice (Q-196)Federation office
  8. Record every subscription payment, by card, cheque or at an event, in one list with its print or digital flag; in 2026 the manager's own ledger and the registration system diverged; a non-member subscriber is a role on the contact record (P21 §2; Q-239)Subscriptions manager
  9. Reconcile the two ledgers once into the platform's subscriber ledger, then produce each issue's print list from it for the mailing house (P24 §5.2, §5.3)Subscriptions manager; Federation office
  10. Send an appeal to lapsed subscribers, by email to younger contacts and by letter to older households, with a cut-off before the next issue — in practice (August 2026). On the platform the channel is each person's consented channel, never chosen by age, and a subscriber who paid since the appeal is not written to again (P24 §5.5)Magazine editor; general manager chooses the channel
  11. File the magazine's chequebook report (subscriptions and obituary income; per-issue stipends, design, print, postage)Magazine treasurer
  12. Publicise the Member-at-Large nomination call before the Mid-Year and take candidates' platform articles under the staff's criteria (By-Laws 6.5.2, 9.5.2)Magazine manager

Trigger, frequency, records, money

Trigger
An announcement arrives; an issue is due; renewals lag
Frequency
Six issues a year; appeals as needed
Records
Announcements with the household's consent, placement fees, the subscriber list with its print or digital flag (members and non-members), the mailing-house file, appeal sends, the chequebook report
Money
Subscriptions and paid obituary placements in; per-issue stipends, design, printing and postage out

Rule against practice

  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): By-Law 6.7.1 gives the Magazine an independently selected board; a staff and a treasurer are evidenced and no board (Q-192).
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the record has an appeal each November; the 2026 appeal ran in August.
  • In practice (research, 2 Oct 2026): the record holds no subscriber data beyond member records; the subscriber list includes non-members and is kept in two places.

Hands off to

  • announcements → tree: a life event the correspondent enters is a declaration in the committee's queue (D14)
  • announcements → magazine: the designed cycle snapshots what the queue accepted at the copy freeze
  • announcements → comms: the digital issue and the appeal go through the consent filter
  • announcements → ledger: subscriptions and placement fees post as the CPA classes them (Q-193)
